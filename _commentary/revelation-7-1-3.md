Judgment is stopped mid-air until a mark is applied.

## Where this comes

Between the sixth seal and the seventh.

The sixth has just been opened — the sun black as sackcloth, the moon like blood, and men hiding
in the caves saying *hide us from the face of Him who sits on the throne*, and the question that
ends the chapter: *who is able to stand?* See the note on Revelation 6:12–14.

Chapter 7 is the answer, and it arrives as an interruption.

## The four angels

*I saw **four angels posted at the four corners of the earth**. They had **the four winds gripped
tight** so that **not a breath of wind could move** — not over the land, not over the sea, not
through a single tree.*

Four corners, four winds, four angels.

The Greek is **kratountas** — holding fast, restraining by force. It takes effort.

## The stillness

*Not a breath of wind* — and John lists three places it is not blowing: land, sea, and a single
tree.

The detail about the tree is the one that makes it uncanny. Not even one leaf moving.

## The fifth angel

*And I saw one more angel **coming up out of the sunrise**, carrying **the seal of the living
God**.*

The Greek is **apo anatoles heliou** — from the rising of the sun. The east.

The direction the glory comes from in Ezekiel: *the glory of the God of Israel came from the way
of the east*. And the direction Jesus names: *as the lightning comes from the east, so also will
the coming of the Son of Man be*.

## The order

*And he **shouted to the four angels who had been given the power to wreck the land and the
sea**.*

They have the power. They are told to hold it.

*"**Do nothing** to the land, he said, or to the sea, or to the trees."*

## The condition

*"**Not until we have put the seal on the foreheads of the servants of our God**."*

The Greek is **sphragisomen** — until we seal.

A seal was pressed into wax or clay to show ownership and to guarantee that what was sealed had
not been tampered with.

## What is being said about the timing

The winds are not held because judgment has been cancelled. They are held because the sealing is
not finished.

The order in the chapter is: restrain, seal, then release.

## Where the image comes from

Ezekiel 9: a man with a writer's inkhorn told to *go through the midst of the city, and put a
mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and cry over all the abominations*, and then the
destroyers follow — *but do not come near anyone on whom is the mark*.

And Passover, where the blood on the doorposts is what the destroyer passes.

## What the seal is

*And I looked, and behold, a Lamb standing on Mount Zion, and with Him one hundred and
forty-four thousand, **having His Father's name written on their foreheads**.* See the note on
Revelation 14:1–2.

The seal is a name, and the name is the Father's.

## The other mark

The beast's, seven chapters later: *he causes all… to receive a mark on their right hand or on
their foreheads*.

Two marks, both on foreheads, and Revelation makes the sealing the earlier of the two.

## What Paul calls it

*In whom also, having believed, **you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise**, who is the
guarantee of our inheritance.* See the note on Ephesians 1:11–14.

## Who is standing at the end of the chapter

*A great multitude which no one could number, of all nations, tribes, peoples, and tongues,
standing before the throne.* See the note on Revelation 7:9–10.

The chapter that begins with judgment held ends with a crowd nobody can count.

> **What stops the winds.** Not mercy replacing judgment — an unfinished sealing. The four angels
> already hold the power to wreck; they are told to wait until the foreheads are marked.
