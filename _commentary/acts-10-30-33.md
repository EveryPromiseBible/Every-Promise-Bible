Cornelius explains why he sent for a stranger, and the last sentence is the most hospitable in
**Acts**.

## What he was doing

*"**Four days ago I was fasting until this hour; and at the ninth hour I prayed in my house.**"*

Three in the afternoon — the hour of the evening sacrifice in a temple he was not allowed inside,
kept by a Roman officer in his own front room in Caesarea.

## What he was told

*"**Cornelius, your prayer has been heard, and your alms are remembered in the sight of God.**"*

**Eis mnemosynon** — for a memorial. It is the sacrificial word: the portion of a grain offering
burned so that the rest could be accepted.

His prayers and his giving are described in the language of an offering, by an angel, in a Gentile
house.

*Therefore by Him let us continually offer **the sacrifice of praise** to God… But **do not forget to
do good and to share, for with such sacrifices God is well pleased**.*

## What the angel does not do

Tell him anything.

*"**Send therefore to Joppa and call Simon here, whose surname is Peter… When he comes, he will speak
to you.**"*

An angel appears, in a blaze, and his entire message is a forwarding address. He will not preach the
gospel himself — a man has to walk thirty miles and do it.

*And how shall they hear without a preacher? And how shall they preach unless they are sent?… So
then **faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God**.*

*But we have this treasure in **earthen vessels**, that the excellence of the power may be of God and
not of us.*

## The sentence

*"**So I sent to you immediately, and you have done well to come. Now therefore, we are all present
before God, to hear all the things commanded you by God.**"*

**Enopion tou theou paresmen** — we are present in the sight of God.

A room full of Gentiles, assembled and waiting, telling a Jewish fisherman they are ready. It is the
best audience any preacher in Scripture is given.

## What Peter says to them

*"**To Him all the prophets witness that, through His name, whoever believes in Him will receive
remission of sins.**"*

And **Luke** notes that the Spirit fell *while Peter was still speaking these words* — before the
sermon was finished, on the sentence with **whoever** in it.

> **What the angel would not do.** Preach. He appears in shining clothes and gives a street address
> in Joppa, and the gospel has to be carried the last thirty miles by a man who needed three visions
> to agree to go.
