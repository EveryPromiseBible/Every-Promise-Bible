---
key: Genesis 31:4–13
title: God had been doing it the whole time
updated: 2026-08-14
---
Jacob calls his wives out to a field to tell them two things: what Laban has done, and what he
has just found out God was doing underneath it.

## Where the conversation happens

*So Jacob sent word to Rachel and Leah **to come out to the field** where his flock was.*

Not in the house. He will not have this conversation inside their father's compound.

## What he can see

*"I can see that **your father's face toward me has changed**."*

The Hebrew is *the face of your father is not toward me as before*. The atmosphere shifted
before anything was said.

## What he has done

*"You know that I have **worked for your father with everything in me**."*

He is not exaggerating, and Laban has already agreed with him. The next chapter has Jacob
itemise it: twenty years, drought by day and frost by night, sleep driven from his eyes, torn
animals paid for out of his own pocket.

## What was done to him

*"**Yet he has cheated me and changed my wages ten times over.**"*

The Hebrew is **hetel bi** — he has trifled with me, made a fool of me.

**Ten times.** Every time a category started producing, the category changed.

## The clause underneath

*"**Still, God would not let him harm me.**"*

That is the sentence the whole speech turns on.

The cheating happened. All ten changes happened. And every one of them was overruled while it
was happening.

*"If he said, 'The speckled ones will be your wages,' then **the whole flock bore speckled
young**; and if he said, 'The streaked ones will be your wages,' then **the whole flock bore
streaked young**."*

Laban set the terms and the terms kept coming true in Jacob's favour, whichever way he set
them.

## Who was actually doing it

*"So **God has taken away your father's livestock and given them to me**."*

Chapter 30 has Jacob peeling rods of poplar and almond and setting them in the water troughs —
a piece of folk breeding lore that Genesis reports without endorsing.

Here, telling the story himself, he does not mention the rods at all. He says God did it, and
he says he was shown so in a dream.

The scheme was not what worked. The dream tells him it was never the rods.

## The dream

*"And **the angel of God spoke to me in the dream, 'Jacob.' 'Here I am,' I said**."*

**Hinneni.** The same word Abraham said on the mountain, and Joseph said to his father before
walking fifty miles to brothers who hated him. See the note on Genesis 37:12–17.

*"'**Look up and see** — all the males mating with the flock are streaked, speckled, and
spotted, **for I have seen everything Laban has done to you**.'"*

## What God says about it

*I have seen.*

Not *I will judge it*, and not *you should have handled it better*. He tells a man who has been
defrauded ten times that none of it went unobserved.

*Your Father who sees in secret will Himself reward you openly.*

## The name He introduces Himself by

*"**I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed a pillar and made a vow to Me.**"*

Twenty years earlier, running from his brother with nothing, Jacob had slept on a stone, seen a
stairway, poured oil on the stone and made a vow. He has not been back since, and he had
nothing to promise with at the time. See the note on Genesis 28:16–19.

God introduces Himself by that night.

*Being confident of this very thing, that **He who has begun a good work in you will complete
it** until the day of Jesus Christ.*

The scheming with the peeled rods is not what worked, and Jacob himself leaves it out when he
tells the story. What was working was a vow God had not forgotten and a fraud God had been
overruling every time it was attempted.

## The instruction

*"Now get up, **leave this land, and go back to the land of your birth**."*

Which is the second half of what God said at Bethel: *I will bring you back to this land.*

> **What he had not known.** Ten changed wages, twenty years, and a man peeling sticks in a
> water trough — and then a dream telling him that God had seen every one of the ten, and had
> been the one turning them round the whole time.
