title: "The verse quoted three times, and never the same way twice"

The writer closes his warning by quoting Habakkuk — and the sentence he picks is the one the New
Testament keeps coming back to.

## He will not be late

*In a very short time now, the One who is coming will come, and **He will not be late**.*

Habakkuk was told to write the vision plainly on tablets because *though it tarries, wait for it; it
will surely come, it will not tarry*. A prophet who had asked God how long was given an answer about
timing and told to write it large enough to read at a run.

The writer applies it to a Person: not *it* will come, but **ho erchomenos** — *the Coming One*, a
title, and it is Jesus.

## The clause the whole New Testament argues from

*Now **the one who is right with God will live by faith**.*

Habakkuk 2:4 is quoted three times in the New Testament, and each time the weight falls somewhere
different.

**Romans 1:17** presses *the just* — the letter then spends four chapters on how anybody gets to be
called that, and the answer is a righteousness from God apart from law.

**Galatians 3:11** presses *by faith* — *no one is justified by the law in the sight of God, for
"the just shall live by faith."*

**Hebrews 10:38** presses *shall live* — written to people tempted to give up under pressure, and
the point here is endurance. The righteous do not merely begin by faith. They go on breathing that
way.

One sentence from a minor prophet, and between the three quotations you have justification,
freedom from law, and perseverance.

## Where he refuses to leave them

*And **we are not people who shrink back** into ruin. **We are people who believe**.*

Read who he includes. After the severest paragraph in the letter, the writer switches to *we*, puts
himself and his readers in the same sentence, and tells them which group they are in.

He does not say *make sure you are not among the shrinkers*. He announces that they are not.

> **He tells them what they are.** That is how this letter handles fear every time — it warns
> honestly and then hands the frightened person a description of himself he did not produce. You
> are of those who believe, and the One you are waiting for is not running late.
