Moses tells the new generation to remember something none of them did.

## The command

*"**Remember, and do not forget**, how you provoked the LORD your God to anger in the
wilderness."*

Both halves, positively and negatively, in one sentence. Deuteronomy does this when it will
not be softened.

## Who he is talking to

People who were children at Horeb, or not yet born. The generation that made the calf is dead
— Moses has spent the previous chapters saying so.

He says *you* anyway. *Even at Horeb **you** stirred up His anger.*

Israel's memory is corporate. What happened to the fathers is recounted in the second person
to the sons, and the Passover liturgy still does it: *we were slaves in Egypt.*

## What comes immediately before

*"**Do not say in your heart** … 'Because of my righteousness the LORD has brought me in to
possess this land.'"*

And the verse before that: *it is not because of your righteousness or the uprightness of your
heart that you go in… but because of the wickedness of these nations.*

Three times in four verses he removes the same explanation.

## What comes immediately after

*"From the day you left Egypt until you arrived at this place, **you have been rebellious**."*

Forty years, summarised in one clause, by the man who led them through it.

## Why he says it here

Because chapter 9 is about to describe the calf in detail — the forty days, the broken
tablets, the ground powder in the water — and the whole point of the recital is to settle
what they are **not** getting the land for.

*Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to His mercy He saved us.*

*For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of
God, **not of works, lest anyone should boast**.*

## What is remembered, and what is not

They are told to remember their record. God says of His own memory: *their sins and their
lawless deeds **I will remember no more**.* See the note on Hebrews 8:10–12.

The two are not in tension. The point of remembering the record is to stop believing it earned
anything — and the reason the record can be looked at squarely is that it has been dealt with
by somebody else.

> **The instruction.** *Remember, and do not forget.* Said to a generation that had not done
> it, immediately after three separate denials that they were being let in on merit.
