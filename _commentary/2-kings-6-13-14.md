A king sends an army to arrest one man, and the operation is textbook.

## Why they want him

Elisha has been telling the king of Israel where the Aramean camp is, repeatedly, and the king
of Aram has been assuming there is a traitor on his staff.

*"None, my lord, O king; but **Elisha, the prophet who is in Israel, tells the king of Israel
the words that you speak in your bedroom**."*

## The order

*"Go, **find out where he is**, so I can send men and capture him."*

Reconnaissance first. It is a competent instruction, and the intelligence comes back correct:
*he is in Dothan.*

## What is sent

*Horses and chariots and **a strong force**.*

An army corps, for one unarmed man.

## When

*They went **by night** and surrounded the city.*

Everything about the operation is right. The location is accurate, the force is
overwhelming, the timing is professional, and the encirclement is complete before anybody
inside is awake.

It is a well-run mission against a target who cannot be taken.

## Where Dothan is

The town where Joseph's brothers were grazing when he came looking for them, and where they
put him in a pit. See the note on Genesis 37:12–17.

Two men surrounded at Dothan, generations apart, and neither story ends the way the men
surrounding them intended.

## What is on the hill

*The mountain was full of **horses and chariots of fire** all around Elisha.*

Already. Before the Arameans arrived, before the servant woke, before anybody prayed. See the
note on 2 Kings 6:15.

The besieging army is itself surrounded and does not know it.

*You are of God, little children, and have overcome them, because **He who is in you is greater
than he who is in the world**.*

## What happens to them

Struck blind, led by Elisha into Samaria, and then — at his insistence, against the king's
wishes — fed.

*"Set food and water before them, that they may eat and drink and go to their master." Then he
prepared a great feast for them.*

Jesus: *"love your enemies, bless those who curse you."* And Paul, quoting Proverbs: ***if your
enemy is hungry, feed him**; if he is thirsty, give him a drink.*

The army that came to arrest him goes home fed, and *the bands of Syrian raiders came no more
into the land of Israel*.

> **What the night operation could not account for.** The intelligence was right and the force
> was overwhelming. What the plan did not have was the hillside.
