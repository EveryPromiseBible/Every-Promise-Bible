The chapter before this one ends with a forest being cut down. This one begins with
what grows out of the stump.

## The stump

*A shoot will spring up **from the stump of Jesse**.*

Not from the tree — from the stump. The house of David is described as felled.

And it is *Jesse*, not David. Back past the throne to the farmer in Bethlehem whose
youngest son was out with the sheep. The royal line is reduced to the family it came
from.

Isaiah 10 closes with God cutting down the high trees with an axe: *the thickets of
the forest He will cut down with iron, and Lebanon will fall.* Then this.

## The shoot

The Hebrew is **netzer** — a sprout, a green shoot. Matthew's *He shall be called a
Nazarene* has been read against this word.

A shoot out of a stump is what a tree does when it has been cut down and is not
dead.

## The Spirit resting

*The Spirit of the LORD **will rest on him**.*

The Hebrew is to settle, to come to rest and stay. It is the same root as Noah's
name.

## The sevenfold list

Read it as it is written and it is three pairs under one heading:

- **the Spirit of the LORD**
- the Spirit of **wisdom** and **understanding**
- the Spirit of **counsel** and **might**
- the Spirit of **knowledge** and **the fear of the LORD**

Seven, and it is one Spirit named seven ways rather than seven Spirits.

Revelation opens with *the seven Spirits who are before His throne*, and the lampstand
in Exodus had seven lamps on one hammered stand. See the notes on Revelation 1:4–6
and Exodus 25:31–40.

## "His delight will be in the fear of the LORD"

The Hebrew is that His scent, His breathing, is in it — an unusual verb, related to
smell. He finds it fragrant.

## How He judges

*He will **not judge by what his eyes see**, nor decide by what his ears hear.*

The two things every human court runs on — testimony and appearance — are set aside.

The next verse says what He judges by instead: *with righteousness He shall judge the
poor, and decide with equity for the meek of the earth.*

## Where the chapter goes

The wolf with the lamb, the leopard with the young goat, the calf and the lion
together, and a child leading them. A nursing infant playing at a cobra's hole.

*They shall not hurt nor destroy in all My holy mountain, for the earth shall be full
of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.*

> **What the chapter ends with.** *In that day there shall be **a Root of Jesse**, who
> shall stand as a banner to the peoples; for the Gentiles shall seek Him.* He is the
> shoot out of Jesse in verse 1 and the root of Jesse in verse 10 — after him, and
> underneath him.
