The content of the secret, and it is three words with the same prefix.

## What was not said before

*In the ages before us it was **never told to the children of men** the way the Spirit has now told
it.*

The Greek is **hetereais geneais ouk egnoristhe** — in other generations it was not made known.

## To whom it is told now

*The way the Spirit has now told it **to His holy apostles and prophets**.*

The Greek is **en Pneumati** — in the Spirit.

Paul has already named the foundation: *built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets,
Jesus Christ Himself being the chief cornerstone*.

## The secret

*That **the nations are heirs right along with us, members of the same body, sharers together in
the promise**.*

The Greek is three compounds, each beginning **sun-** — together with:

**Sunkleronoma** — joint-heirs.

**Sussoma** — of the same body. A word that appears nowhere else in Greek literature; Paul seems
to have coined it.

**Summetocha** — joint-partakers.

## What the repetition does

Three times, the same prefix, in one clause.

The point is not that Gentiles are included. It is the *together with*.

## Why that was hidden

The Old Testament says repeatedly that the nations will be blessed. *In your seed all the nations
of the earth shall be blessed.* Isaiah has foreigners joined to the LORD, and Gentiles coming to
the light.

What was not said is the terms: one body, on the same footing, without becoming Jews first.

## Where

*All of it **in Christ**, all of it **through the good news**.*

The Greek is **en Christo Iesou dia tou euangeliou**.

Two prepositions. In Him, and through the message.

## The servant

*Of that news **I was made a servant**.*

The Greek is **diakonos** — servant, minister.

Passive: **egenethen** — I was made.

## What made him one

*And **the gift of God's grace made me one**, given me by **the working of His power that gets things
done**.*

The Greek is **kata ten energeian tes dunameos autou** — according to the working of His power.

**Energeia** — operative power, effective working. The root of *energy*.

## Who is saying this

The man who was holding coats at Stephen's stoning, and *breathing threats and murder against the
disciples of the Lord*.

He was arrested for bringing Gentiles too close to the temple, and he calls that grace.

## What he says about himself next

*To me — **the last and littlest of all God's holy people, less than the least of them** — this grace
was given.* See the note on Ephesians 3:8–9.

## What the wall was

*For He Himself is our peace, who has made both one, and **has broken down the middle wall of
separation**.* See the note on Ephesians 2:14–16.

A literal wall stood in the temple, with inscriptions warning Gentiles not to pass it on pain of
death.

## What the church demonstrates

*To the intent that now **the manifold wisdom of God might be made known by the church to the
principalities and powers in the heavenly places**.*

The Greek is **polupoikilos** — many-coloured, richly variegated.

The audience named for the demonstration is not human.

> **The three words.** **Sunkleronoma, sussoma, summetocha** — joint-heirs, same-bodied,
> joint-partakers. The secret is not the inclusion; it is the *together with*.
