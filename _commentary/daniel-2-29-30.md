Standing in front of the most powerful man alive, with the answer in his hand, Daniel gives
away the credit.

## Where he is

Before Nebuchadnezzar, after every wise man in Babylon has failed and the execution order
has been issued.

He has just told the king: *there is a God in heaven who reveals secrets, and He has made
known to King Nebuchadnezzar what will be in the latter days.*

## What the king was doing

*"As you were **lying there**, O king, your thoughts turned to **what would come after
this**."*

The Aramaic is *your thoughts came into your mind upon your bed*.

Daniel tells the king what he had been thinking about before he fell asleep. Nobody could
have known it.

An emperor lying awake wondering what happens after him.

## Who answered

*"And **the One who uncovers mysteries showed you what will be**."*

The Aramaic is *the revealer of secrets*.

Daniel has already used the title twice in the chapter. It is his name for God through the
whole book.

## The disclaimer

*"As for me, **this mystery was not opened to me because I have more wisdom than anyone
living**."*

He is holding the one thing the king wants, and the sentence he chooses is a denial of
personal capacity.

He had been assessed as *ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers in all his
realm*, and had every ground to say so.

## The two reasons he gives

*"It was opened **so that the king would know its meaning**."*

*"And so that **you would understand the thoughts of your own heart**."*

Both purposes are about the king. Neither is about Daniel.

The second is the more surprising: the dream was given so a pagan emperor would understand
what was in his own head.

## What he had been given

*To Daniel He gave more still: understanding in every kind of vision and dream.* See the
note on Daniel 1:17.

He has the gift and refuses to be the reason.

## What the dream was

A statue: head of gold, chest and arms of silver, belly and thighs of bronze, legs of iron,
feet of iron mixed with clay.

*You watched while **a stone was cut out without hands**, which struck the image on its feet…
and the stone that struck the image **became a great mountain and filled the whole
earth**.*

**Without hands** — the same construction Paul uses of the circumcision *no human hand
performed*. See the note on Colossians 2:11–15.

## The interpretation

*"**You are this head of gold**."*

And then four kingdoms, and: *in the days of these kings the God of heaven will set up a
kingdom which shall never be destroyed.*

## The king's reaction

*Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell on his face, prostrate before Daniel… and said, "**Truly your
God is the God of gods, the Lord of kings, and a revealer of secrets**."*

Daniel's own title for God, in the king's mouth.

## What Daniel does with the promotion

He is made ruler over the province of Babylon and chief of the wise men — and immediately
*Daniel petitioned the king, and he set Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego over the affairs of
the province of Babylon*.

The first thing he does with the office is get his three friends appointed.

> **What he says at the moment of maximum leverage.** That it was not his wisdom, and that
> the whole thing was arranged so a king would understand his own thoughts.
