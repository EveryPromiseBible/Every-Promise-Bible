Four substitutions, and then two names.

## What she was

*Once you were **forsaken and hated, with no one passing through**.*

The Hebrew is **azuvah usenu'ah** — abandoned and hated.

**Ve'eyn over** — and no one passing through. A road nobody uses, which is what a ruined city is.

## What she becomes

*Now I will make you **the everlasting pride, the joy of all generations**.*

The Hebrew is **lige'on olam** — an eternal majesty.

## The nursing

*You will **drink the milk of nations and nurse at royal breasts**.*

The image is startling and deliberate: the powers that plundered her now feed her.

It picks up an earlier promise: *kings shall be your foster fathers, and their queens your nursing
mothers*.

## The recognition

*Then you will know that **I, the LORD, am your Savior, your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob**.*

Three titles: **Moshia** — saviour. **Go'el** — kinsman-redeemer. See the note on
Leviticus 25:23–28.

**Avir Ya'aqov** — the Mighty One of Jacob, a title from Jacob's own blessing on Joseph.

## The four exchanges

*Instead of **bronze** I will bring you **gold**, and **silver** instead of **iron**, **bronze** instead of
**wood**, and **iron** instead of **stone**.*

Each material replaced by one a grade above it.

Bronze becomes gold. Iron becomes silver. Wood becomes bronze. Stone becomes iron.

Nothing is destroyed; everything moves up.

## What that describes

Rebuilding. The materials of an ordinary construction upgraded at every level.

## The two officials

*I will make **peace your governor and righteousness your ruler**.*

The Hebrew is **pequddatecha shalom venogesayik tzedaqah**.

**Nogesim** — taskmasters, oppressors. The word used of the Egyptian slave-drivers: *taskmasters
to afflict them with their burdens*.

The office that was oppression becomes righteousness. The word is kept and the occupant changed.

## The silence

*No more will **violence be heard in your land**, nor **ruin or destruction within your borders**.*

The Hebrew is **chamas** — violence. **Shod vashever** — devastation and breaking.

## The names

*You will call your **walls Salvation** and your **gates Praise**.*

The Hebrew is **veqarat yeshu'ah chomotayik ushe'arayik tehillah**.

**Yeshu'ah** — salvation. The noun behind the name Jesus.

**Tehillah** — praise. The root behind **Tehillim**, the Hebrew title of the Psalms.

## What walls and gates are for

Walls keep things out. Gates let things in.

The city is told to name the barrier *Salvation* and the opening *Praise*.

## What the gates were doing four verses earlier

*Your gates will stand open always. They will never be shut, day or night.* See the note on
Isaiah 60:10–14.

## What follows

*The sun shall no longer be your light by day, nor for brightness shall the moon give light to
you; **but the LORD will be to you an everlasting light**, and your God your glory.*

*Your sun shall no longer go down, nor shall your moon withdraw itself.*

## Where it ends up

*The city had no need of the sun or of the moon to shine in it, for **the glory of God illuminated
it**. The Lamb is its light.*

*Its gates shall not be shut at all by day (there shall be no night there).* See the note on
Revelation 21:22–23.

## The last line of the chapter

*I, the LORD, will hasten it in its time.*

The Hebrew is **achishennah** — I will hurry it.

> **The names.** The wall is called *Salvation* and the gate is called *Praise* — the barrier and
> the opening, each given a name that says what it is for.
