Six verses of itinerary, and the list of names in the middle is the point of them.

## The seven

*"**Sopater of Berea, Aristarchus and Secundus of the Thessalonians, Gaius of Derbe, and Timothy, and
Tychicus and Trophimus of Asia.**"*

**Luke** names seven men and their home towns: Berea, Thessalonica, Derbe, Lystra, and the province
of Asia — Macedonia, Galatia and Asia Minor, three regions.

They are the delegates carrying the collection. **Paul** had told the Corinthians how it would work:
*whomever you approve by your letters I will send to bear your gift to Jerusalem.*

## What they were carrying

Money from Gentile churches to poor Jewish believers in Judea, and **Paul** spends two whole chapters
of **2 Corinthians** on it.

*For you know **the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sakes He
became poor**, that you through His poverty might become rich.*

That is the argument he uses. The collection is not charity administration; it is the incarnation
applied to a budget.

*For if the Gentiles have been partakers of their spiritual things, **their duty is also to minister
to them in material things**.*

## The we

*"**But we sailed away from Philippi.**"*

**Luke** slips back into the first person. The *we* passages begin at Troas in chapter 16, stop when
**Paul** leaves Philippi, and resume here — which reads as though **Luke** stayed in Philippi for
several years and rejoined the party when it came back through.

He does not explain, and he never names himself in the book.

## The plot

*"**And when the Jews plotted against him as he was about to sail to Syria, he decided to return
through Macedonia.**"*

A pilgrim ship at Passover would have been the obvious way to Jerusalem, and the obvious place for
an ambush. **Paul** changes the route and adds weeks to the journey.

*"**Behold, I send you out as sheep in the midst of wolves. Therefore be wise as serpents and
harmless as doves.**"*

## Where he is going

*"**And now, behold, I go bound in the spirit to Jerusalem, not knowing the things that will happen
to me there, except that the Holy Spirit testifies in every city, saying that chains and
tribulations await me.**"*

Seventeen verses later. He is travelling toward it deliberately, with seven men and a bag of money,
by the long road.

*"**But none of these things move me… so that I may finish my race with joy, and the ministry which
I received from the Lord Jesus, to testify to the gospel of the grace of God.**"*

> **The list.** Seven men from three provinces, named with their home towns, carrying a Gentile
> collection to Jewish believers — which is Ephesians 2 happening in cash before Paul wrote it down.
