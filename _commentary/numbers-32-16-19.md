The two tribes come back with a counter-proposal, and it is better than the request Moses had
just refused.

## The approach

**32:16** *They **came close** and answered.*

The Hebrew is **vayyiggeshu** — they drew near. The same verb Judah uses when he steps forward
in front of Joseph. See the note on Genesis 44:18–34.

They had been accused of repeating the sin of Kadesh. They do not argue the charge. See the
note on Numbers 32:6–15.

## What they will build first

**32:16** again: *"We will build **sheepfolds here for our flocks and towns for our children**."*

Folds before towns, in the Hebrew word order — and Moses notices. When he restates the terms he
reverses it, **32:24**: *build cities for your little ones, and folds for your sheep*.

He puts the children first, and does it without comment.

## What they will do

**32:17** *"But **we ourselves will arm and go ahead of the Israelites, leading them into battle**."*

Not merely cross. Go in front. The tribes with the least to gain from the campaign volunteer
for the vanguard.

## The term

**32:17** again: *"**Until every one of them is settled in their inheritance.**"*

And again, two clauses later, **32:18**: *"**We will not come home until every Israelite has received his
portion.**"*

Said twice. Not until the last tribe is in.

## What they give up

**32:19** *"**We ask for no share on the far side of the Jordan**, because our inheritance will be here."*

They fight a seven-year war for land they have formally renounced any claim to.

## Whether they kept it

**Joshua 22:3**: *"You have not left your brethren these many days, up to this day, but have kept the charge of
the commandment of the LORD your God."*

Joshua, years later, sending them home with a blessing and a share of the spoil.

## The shape of it

Somebody whose own portion is already secured, crossing over anyway, going in front, and
refusing to go home until everybody else is in.

**Philippians 2:4–5**: *Let each of you **look out not only for his own interests, but also for the interests of
others**. Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus.*

**2 Corinthians 8:9**: *For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sakes
He became poor, **that you through His poverty might become rich**.*

## What Moses does with it

Accepts it, and writes it into terms. See the note on Numbers 32:20–27.

The chapter that opens with two tribes asking to stop short ends with them leading the
crossing.

> **The sentence said twice.** **32:18** again: *We will not come home until every Israelite has received his
> portion.* Volunteered by the two tribes who had already been promised theirs.
