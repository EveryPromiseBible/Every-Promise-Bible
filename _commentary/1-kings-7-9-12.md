Four verses about masonry, and the detail the writer will not leave out is which surfaces were
finished.

## What the stones were

*Costly stones, **quarried to size, sawn smooth on every face**.*

The Hebrew is *sawn with saws, inside and outside*.

Every face. Including the ones laid against another stone, and the ones bedded into mortar,
and the ones facing the earth.

## Where they were

*From **foundation to eaves**.*

*The foundations were huge stones, **some ten cubits long and some eight**, all of the finest
kind.*

Fifteen feet of dressed, polished, costly stone — underground, in the footings, where the
finish would never be seen by anybody for as long as the building stood.

## What that costs

Sawing limestone by hand, to a smooth face, on a block fifteen feet long, is weeks of work.
Doing it to the buried face is weeks of work nobody will ever look at.

## Why it is in the Bible

Because the same principle is stated about people, in the passage that uses this exact
imagery:

*You also, as **living stones**, are being built up a spiritual house.*

*But **let it be the hidden person of the heart**, with the incorruptible ornament of a gentle
and quiet spirit, which is very precious in the sight of God.*

Peter writes both sentences, in the same letter, and the second one is the buried face.

## Who sees it

*Your Father who **sees in secret** will Himself reward you openly.*

Jesus says it three times in one chapter — about giving, about praying, about fasting. Each
time the instruction is that the thing be done where it will not be seen, and each time the
reason is that Someone is looking.

## The one built the same way

*And **the great courtyard was walled with three courses of cut stone and one course of cedar
beams — just like the inner court of the house of the LORD** and its portico.*

Solomon's own house is built to the specification of God's. The writer notes the match and
does not comment on it.

The book will comment later. Seven years on the house of the LORD; thirteen on his own.

> **The face nobody would see.** Sawn smooth on every side, including the sides laid against
> other stones and the ones set in the ground — on foundation blocks fifteen feet long, buried
> for the life of the building.
