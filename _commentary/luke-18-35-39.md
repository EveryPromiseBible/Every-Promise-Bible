A blind beggar outside Jericho gets one chance and will not be quieted.

## What he could not do

*"A blind man was **sitting by the side of the road, begging**."*

He cannot see who is passing and cannot get through a crowd. He hears it and has to ask what is
happening.

## What he shouts

*"**Jesus! Son of David! Have mercy on me!**"*

**Huie David, eleeson me.**

**Son of David** is a messianic title, and it is the first time in **Luke** anybody uses it of Him out
loud. A blind man at a roadside says what the disciples have been told to keep quiet.

*"**And the Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David.**"*

*Concerning His Son **Jesus Christ** our Lord, who was **born of the seed of David** according to the
flesh.*

## What the crowd does

*"**Those in front told him sharply to be quiet.**"*

**Epetimon auto** — they rebuked him. It is the verb **Luke** uses of **Jesus** rebuking demons and a
fever.

The people between him and **Jesus** are the ones He is on His way to Jerusalem for.

## What he does about it

*"**But he shouted all the louder.**"*

**Polla mallon ekrazen** — he kept crying out much more.

Nothing changes in his argument. He repeats the same sentence at greater volume, and it is the whole
of his contribution.

*"**Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened.**"*

*"Then shall we not hear the cry of the widow who keeps coming?"* See the note on
Luke 18:6–8.

**Luke** puts the persistent widow twenty-nine verses earlier in the same chapter, and this man is the
parable happening.

## What Jesus does

*"**Jesus stopped.**"*

**Statheis de ho Iesous** — and Jesus, having stood still.

He is on the last leg of the road to the cross, with a crowd around Him, and He stops in the middle
of it for a voice He cannot see.

*"**What do you want Me to do for you?**"*

He asks a blind beggar what he wants — a question with an obvious answer, put to a man nobody had
asked anything in years.

## What follows

*"**Receive your sight. Your faith has made you well.**"*

**He pistis sou sesoken se** — your faith has saved you. The same sentence He says to the woman who
touched His clothes and to the leper who came back.

And then: *immediately he received his sight and followed Him, glorifying God.* The next paragraph is
Zacchaeus, and the chapter after that is the entry into Jerusalem, where the crowd shouts the same
title the crowd here had tried to silence.

> **What the crowd tried to stop.** The first person in Luke to call Him Son of David out loud, and
> they told him to be quiet. He said it louder, and Jesus stopped walking.
