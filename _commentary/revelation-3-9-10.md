The smallest church on the list gets the only promise of being kept out of the hour.

## The church

Philadelphia. One of two of the seven with no rebuke at all.

*"**You have a little strength**, have kept My word, and have not denied My name."*

The Greek is **mikran echeis dunamin** — you have little power.

It is stated as a fact, not a failing.

## The open door

*"See, **I have set before you an open door, and no one can shut it**."*

Said just before this passage. He holds *the key of David* — *He who opens and no one shuts*. See
the note on Revelation 3:7–8.

## The opponents

*"Watch what I do with the ones **out of Satan's assembly**, who **say they are Jews and are not** —
they are lying."*

The Greek is **sunagōgēs tou Satana**.

The same phrase appears in the letter to Smyrna, the other church with no rebuke. Both of the
unblamed churches are the ones being pushed out.

## The reversal

*"I will make them **come and bow down right at your feet**."*

The Old Testament image runs the other way: the nations bowing to Israel. Isaiah's *they shall bow
down to you with their faces to the earth*.

Here it is turned round.

## The point of it

*"And I will make them **know that I have loved you**."*

The Greek is **hoti egō ēgapēsa se**.

The purpose of the bowing is not the church's vindication in itself. It is stated as a
demonstration of a fact about who was loved.

## The commendation

*"**You held on to what I told you about holding out**."*

The Greek is **etērēsas ton logon tēs hupomonēs mou** — you kept the word of My endurance.

**Hupomonē** — remaining under. Not passive waiting; bearing up under weight without moving.

## The promise

*"So **I will keep you from the hour** when everything is put to the test."*

The Greek is **tērēsō ek tēs hōras** — I will keep you out of the hour.

The verb answers the verb: *you kept*, therefore *I will keep*.

## The preposition

**Ek** — out of, from within.

Whether it means preservation through the hour or removal before it has been argued for centuries.
The text pairs it with **tēreō**, to guard, and does not settle the question.

## What the hour is

*"**The hour coming over the whole inhabited world**, to try out everyone living on the earth."*

The Greek is **tēs oikoumenēs holēs** — the whole inhabited earth.

Not a local persecution. The scope is stated.

## What follows

*"**Behold, I am coming quickly! Hold fast what you have**, that no one may take your crown."* See the
note on Revelation 3:11–13.

And the promise of a pillar in the temple, with three names written on it.

## The church that follows

Laodicea, a few miles down the road, rich and needing nothing. See the note on Revelation 3:16–17.

Little strength and an open door; great wealth and a closed one. The two letters are consecutive.

> **The demonstration.** The bowing is not for the church's sake — *I will make them know that I have
> loved you*. That is the stated purpose.
