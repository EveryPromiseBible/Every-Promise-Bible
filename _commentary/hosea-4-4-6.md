The most quoted line in Hosea, and the verse names who was supposed to supply what was missing.

## Who is being addressed

*"Let no one accuse, let no one blame, for **your quarrel is with Me, you who are meant to be My
priests**."*

The charge is not against the nation generally. It is against the priesthood.

God stops the mutual blaming — *let no one accuse* — and takes the dispute to Himself.

## The picture

*"**You stumble by day**, and **the prophet stumbles with you by night**."*

Stumbling in daylight, when there is nothing to trip over.

Priest and prophet, day and night — the two offices that were meant to hold the people steady,
both down.

## The sentence

*"**My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge**."*

The Hebrew is **beli hadda'at** — for lack of *the* knowledge, with the article.

Not knowledge in general. A particular body of it, which the priests held and were supposed to
hand on: *the priest's lips should keep knowledge, and people should seek the law from his
mouth*.

## Why the lack happened

*"Because **you have rejected knowledge**."*

The Hebrew is **ma'asta** — you have refused it.

The people lacked it because the priests had thrown it away. The verse does not blame the
uninformed for being uninformed.

## The consequence

*"**I reject you from serving Me as priest**."*

The same verb, turned round. They rejected; they are rejected.

*"Because you have **forgotten the teaching of your God**, **I too will forget your children**."*

**Torah** — the instruction. The word that means both *law* and *teaching*.

## What the priests were doing instead

The next verse: *they eat up the sin of My people, and they set their heart on their iniquity*.

The sin offerings were their food, so more sin meant a fuller table. The system paid them by
the transgression.

## The line about the people

*"Like people, like priest."*

Whatever the priest is, the congregation becomes.

## What Hosea's own marriage was doing

The prophet had been told to marry Gomer, and to name the children *Lo-Ruhamah* — not pitied —
and *Lo-Ammi* — not My people.

And then the reversal: *I will say to those who were not My people, "You are My people," and they
shall say, "You are my God."* See the note on Hosea 2:21–23.

## Where Paul takes it

*As He says also in Hosea: "**I will call them My people, who were not My people**, and her
beloved, who was not beloved."* See the note on Romans 9:22–26.

The book that names the destruction is the same book Paul uses for the inclusion of the
Gentiles.

## What the priesthood becomes

*You are a chosen generation, **a royal priesthood**, a holy nation, His own special people.* See
the note on 1 Peter 2:9–10.

The office that failed in Hosea is handed to everybody.

> **Where the lack came from.** *Because you have rejected knowledge* — addressed to the priests,
> in a sentence about what happened to the people. The uninformed are not the ones being charged.
