Two things are said to count for nothing, and the second one would have surprised them more.

## The posture

*For we, through the Spirit, **wait with longing** for the righteousness we hope for.*

The Greek is **apekdechometha** — a triple-compound verb. To await eagerly, from off and out.

The same word Paul uses of creation craning its neck. See the note on Romans 8:18–22.

## What is being waited for

*The righteousness **we hope for**.*

The Greek is **elpida dikaiosunēs** — the hope of righteousness.

Not a righteousness still to be earned. The full public outcome of one already given.

## The means

*And we wait for it **by faith**.*

**Ek pisteōs** — out of faith. The letter's refrain: *the just shall live by faith*.

## What has just been said

*You have become estranged from Christ, you who attempt to be justified by law; **you have fallen
from grace**.* See the note on Galatians 5:2–4.

The Greek is **tēs charitos exepesate**.

And *if you become circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing*.

## The first thing that counts for nothing

*In Christ Jesus **neither being circumcised**… counts for anything at all.*

The Greek is **oute peritomē ti ischuei**.

This is the point the whole letter has been fighting for.

## The second

*Nor **being uncircumcised**.*

The Greek is **oute akrobustia**.

## Why that is the surprise

A man who has won the argument against circumcision could easily make uncircumcision the new badge.

Paul closes that off in the same breath. The party opposing the rule does not get to become a rule.

## What does count

*What counts is **faith working itself out through love**.*

The Greek is **pistis di' agapēs energoumenē**.

**Energeō** — to be at work, to be operative. The root of *energy*.

## The grammar

The participle is middle or passive: faith being made effective, faith energising itself.

Either way, love is the channel — **di' agapēs**, through love — not the addition.

## The relation

Faith is not supplemented by love. Love is what faith does when it is working.

## Where the letter takes it

*For you, brethren, have been called to liberty; only do not use liberty as an opportunity for the
flesh, but **through love serve one another**.* See the note on Galatians 5:13–15.

*For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this: "You shall love your neighbour as
yourself."*

## And what it produces

*But the fruit of the Spirit is **love**, joy, peace, longsuffering.* See the note on
Galatians 5:22–24.

The list begins with the same word this verse ends on.

## The phrase Paul repeats

*For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision avails anything, but **a new
creation**.* See the note on Galatians 6:14–16.

The same sentence at the end of the letter, with a different closing term.

Faith working through love, and a new creation. Two names for the thing that replaces the badge.

> **The second nothing.** Having circumcision counts for nothing — and so does not having it. The
> man who wins the argument does not get a new badge out of it.
