The part of the dream the dream was given for.

## The clause

*"A stone was cut out, **but not by any human hand**."*

The Aramaic is **di-la bidayin** — without hands.

It is the only thing in the vision with no maker named, and it is the only thing left standing at
the end.

## Where that phrase goes

*"We heard Him say, 'I will destroy this temple made with hands, and within three days **I will
build another made without hands**.'"*

*But **Christ** came as High Priest of the good things to come, with **the greater and more perfect
tabernacle not made with hands**, that is, not of this creation.*

*In Him you were also circumcised with **the circumcision made without hands**, by putting off the
body of the sins of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ.*

*For we know that if our earthly house, this tent, is destroyed, we have a building from God, **a
house not made with hands**, eternal in the heavens.*

**Mark**, **Hebrews**, **Colossians**, **2 Corinthians**. The Greek is **acheiropoietos**, and it is
a New Testament category: temple, tabernacle, circumcision, resurrection body. Everything the
gospel says is real is described with Daniel's adjective.

## Where it strikes

*"It struck the statue **on its feet of iron and clay**."*

The weakest joint, and the whole weight was on it. Nothing above it is hit directly and all of it
comes down.

*"And **whoever falls on this stone will be broken; but on whomever it falls, it will grind him to
powder**."*

**Matthew** 21, and **Jesus** says it to chief priests immediately after quoting *the stone which
the builders rejected*. He puts the two stones together — the rejected cornerstone of Psalm 118 and
the grinding stone of Daniel 2 — and both are Him.

*This is **the stone which was rejected by you builders**, which has become the chief cornerstone.
Nor is there salvation in any other.*

## What is left

*"It became like **chaff on a summer threshing floor**, and the wind carried it away until **no
trace of it could be found**."*

The Aramaic is **vekol-atar la-hishtakach lehon** — no place was found for them.

Gold, silver, bronze, iron and clay all go at once. The dream does not have the empires fall one
after another; the statue is hit at the base and the whole of it goes together.

*The ungodly are not so, but are **like the chaff which the wind drives away**.*

## What the stone becomes

*"But the stone that struck the statue **grew into a great mountain and filled the whole earth**."*

A stone becomes a mountain. The thing that looked smallest in the dream ends up larger than
everything it destroyed.

*"**The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed**, which a man took and sowed in his field, which
indeed is the least of all the seeds; but when it is grown it is greater than the herbs and becomes
a tree."*

*"And in the days of these kings the God of heaven **will set up a kingdom which shall never be
destroyed**… it shall stand forever."*

## Who cut it

Nobody, in the dream. That is the whole force of the clause.

*"Therefore My Father loves Me, because I lay down My life that I may take it again. **No one takes
it from Me, but I lay it down of Myself**."*

> **The adjective.** *Not by any human hand.* The New Testament takes the word over —
> **acheiropoietos** — and uses it of the temple, the tabernacle, the circumcision and the
> resurrection body. Everything the gospel calls real, it calls by Daniel's word for the stone.
