The hand is already up. Everything in these two verses is about timing.

## The interruption

*But the angel of the LORD **called to him from heaven** — "**Abraham! Abraham!**"*

The doubled name. It is how the urgent moments in Scripture open — *Moses, Moses* at the
bush; *Samuel, Samuel* in the night; *Simon, Simon* at the supper table. See the note on
Luke 22:31–34.

## The answer

*"**Here I am**," he answered.*

The Hebrew is **hinneni** — the same word he gave when God first called him at the top of
the chapter, and the same word he gave Isaac on the way up when the boy asked where the
lamb was.

Three times in one chapter. He answers the same way to God, to his son, and to the voice
that stops him.

It is the answer the Son gives in Hebrews: *So I said, Here I am… I have come to do what
You want.* See the note on Hebrews 10:5–7.

## The command

*"**Do not lay a hand on the boy**," the voice said. "**Do nothing to him**."*

Twice, in two forms. The prohibition is absolute and immediate.

## What is now known

*"**Now I know** that you honor God, for you have **not held back your son, your only
son**, from me."*

The Hebrew for *honor God* is *you are one who fears God* — **yare Elohim**.

**Have not held back** is **chasak** — to withhold, to spare, to keep back.

Paul uses the same idea about God in Romans: *He who **did not spare** His own Son, but
delivered Him up for us all.* The one phrase Abraham is commended for is the one turned
back on God Himself.

## "Your son, your only son"

The chapter says it three times — in the command, here, and again in the oath at the
end.

Ishmael was alive. Isaac is the only son of the promise, the one the covenant runs
through.

## The last line

*The knife never fell.*

Moses does not say what Isaac did, or what was said on the way down, or whether they
spoke at all.

## What was already there

Four verses on, Abraham looks up and there is a ram caught in a thicket by its horns —
behind him, already on the mountain, while he was building the altar.

He names the place **Jehovah Jireh**: *the LORD will provide.* And Moses adds the
saying that was still current when he wrote: *in the Mount of the LORD it shall be
provided.*

## Where the mountain is

*The land of Moriah.* 2 Chronicles: *Solomon began to build the house of the LORD at
Jerusalem on Mount Moriah.*

The same ridge.

## What Abraham had worked out

Hebrews says he had reckoned that God could raise the dead — which is why he told the
servants *we will come back to you*. See the note on Hebrews 11:17–22.

> **What stopped it.** A voice, at the last possible second, with the hand in the air.
> The chapter that opens *take now your son* closes with a substitute already caught in
> a bush.
