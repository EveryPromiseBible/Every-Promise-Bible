One verse, and it is God stating the terms of an agreement in His own voice.

## Where it sits

At the end of the chapter about a nation with nothing to offer — *your iniquities have
separated you*, *we grope for the wall like the blind* — and after the line that turns
it: *He saw that there was no man, and wondered that there was no intercessor; therefore
**His own arm brought salvation**.* See the note on Isaiah 59:18–20.

The Redeemer comes to Zion in verse 20. This is the next sentence.

## The heading

*"And **this is My covenant with them**," says the LORD.*

He calls it a covenant, and He is the only party who does anything in it.

Nothing is asked. There are no conditions in the verse.

## The two things

*"**My Spirit**, which rests on you, and **My words**, which I have put in your mouth."*

Both are possessives — **My** Spirit, **My** words. Neither originates with the person
holding them.

**Which I have put in your mouth** — the Hebrew is *which I have placed*.

Jeremiah's call has the same picture: *then the LORD put forth His hand and touched my
mouth, and the LORD said to me, "Behold, **I have put My words in your mouth**."*

## What is promised about them

*"**Will not leave your mouth**."*

The Hebrew is *shall not depart out of your mouth*.

## The three generations

*"Nor the mouths of **your children**, nor the mouths of **their children**."*

Three generations named, and then:

*"**From now on and forever**."*

## The other "shall not depart from your mouth"

Joshua 1: *this Book of the Law **shall not depart from your mouth**, but you shall
meditate in it day and night.* See the note on Joshua 1:6–9.

There it is an instruction to Joshua. Here it is a promise from God about what He has
put there.

## What the next verse is

*Arise, shine, for **your light has come**, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon you.*

Isaiah 60 opens on it, with no break — the chapter division is a later addition. See the
note on Isaiah 60:1–3.

The covenant of Spirit and words is followed immediately by the light rising.

## Where Paul quotes the passage

Romans 11, at the end of three chapters about Israel: *"The Deliverer will come out of
Zion, and He will turn away ungodliness from Jacob. **For this is My covenant with them,
when I take away their sins**."*

Paul quotes verse 20 and the opening of verse 21 together, and completes it from Jeremiah.

## The covenant Jeremiah described

*I will put My laws inside their hearts, and I will write them on their minds… and their
sins and their lawless deeds I will remember no more.* See the note on
Hebrews 10:15–17.

Same word — covenant — and the same direction: God putting something inside a person.

> **What is in the covenant.** Not obligations. A Spirit that rests and words that stay,
> both described as His, and a duration of three generations and then *forever*.
