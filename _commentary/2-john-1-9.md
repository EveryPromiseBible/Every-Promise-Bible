---
key: 2 John 1:9
title: "Running past the line"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
One verse, and the verb in it is about going too far forward.

## The word

*"**Anyone who runs past the line and will not stay with what Christ taught.**"*

The Greek is **pas ho proagon** — everyone going ahead, running out in front.

**Proago** is a neutral word elsewhere — it is what **Jesus** does in *I will go before you into
Galilee*. Here it is a fault, and that is the point **John** is making: the people he is warning
against thought of themselves as advanced.

Some manuscripts read **parabainon**, transgressing. The better texts have the word that means
*progressing*, which is sharper.

*Beware lest anyone cheat you through philosophy and empty deceit… **For in Him dwells all the
fullness of the Godhead bodily**.*

## The teaching of Christ

*"What **Christ** taught."*

The Greek is **te didache tou Christou** — the teaching of Christ, and the grammar allows both *what
He taught* and *the teaching about Him*. In the context of verse 7 it is both, and they are the same
subject.

## What is lost, and what is had

*"Does not have God. **Stay with that teaching and you have the Father and the Son.**"*

**John** does not say the person forfeits a reward or a rank. He says they do not have God, and that
the alternative is having both persons named.

*"He who has the Son has life; **he who does not have the Son of God does not have life**."*

**1 John** 5, and it is the same construction — possession, stated twice, positive and negative.

*"**No one comes to the Father except through Me.**"*

*"**He who has seen Me has seen the Father.**"*

## What abiding is

**Menon** — remaining, staying, abiding. It is **John**'s favourite verb and it occurs about forty
times in his gospel and letters.

*"**Abide in Me, and I in you.** As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, unless it abides in the
vine, neither can you, unless you abide in Me."*

The instruction in **2 John** 9 is not to achieve anything. It is to stay where you are put.

## Why the letter is so short about it

Because the alternative on offer was novelty, and **John**'s answer is that there is nowhere further
forward to get to.

*Jesus Christ is **the same yesterday, today, and forever**. **Do not be carried about with various
and strange doctrines.**"*

**Hebrews** 13, and the two sentences are consecutive.

*In whom are hidden **all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge**.*

> **The verb.** *Proagon* — running on ahead. It is a compliment everywhere else in the New
> Testament, and John uses it as the charge: the trouble with these teachers is that they thought
> they had moved on.
