John opens the body of his letter with a sentence he says he did not compose.

## Where he got it

*Here is the message we heard **from Him**, the one we are passing on to you.*

**Angelia** — the announcement. And John does not present it as a conclusion he reached after sixty
years of thinking. He heard it, from Jesus, and he is relaying it.

He has just spent four verses insisting on that: *what we have heard, what we have seen with our
eyes, what we have looked upon and our hands have handled.* An old man establishing that he was in
the room.

## The double negative

*God is light. **There is no dark in Him — none anywhere**.*

The Greek is emphatic in a way English struggles with: **skotia ouk estin en auto oudemia** —
darkness is not in Him, not any. Two negatives stacked, which in Greek strengthens rather than
cancels.

Not *very little darkness*. Not *darkness He keeps under control*. None, of any kind, anywhere.

James says the same thing with astronomy — no variation, no shadow cast by turning, because
everything in the sky has a phase and its Maker does not. See the note on James 1:16.

## What "walking in the light" is not

Here is where this is usually misread. Two verses on:

*If we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and **the blood
of Jesus Christ His Son cleanses us from all sin**.*

If walking in the light meant sinlessness, the second half of that sentence would be pointless.
Cleansing is named as the ongoing condition of people who are already in the light — so the light is
not a place you get to by being clean. It is the place where cleansing happens.

Darkness is where things are hidden. Light is where they are seen and dealt with. John is describing
honesty in the presence of God, not achievement.

And he says it in the present tense: **katharizei**, keeps on cleansing.

## Why it matters that there is no dark

Because a God with a dark side could never be safely approached in the light. You would have to
manage what He saw.

*But if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us.* You can bring anything into a
place that has no shadows, because there is nothing there that can turn on you.

> **He is light, and He came into the open.** *In Him was life, and the life was the light of men…
> and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome it.* The same writer, the
> same word — and what he saw with his eyes and handled with his hands was that light standing in a
> body, walking toward people who were hiding.
