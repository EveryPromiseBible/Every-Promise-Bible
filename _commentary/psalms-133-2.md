The verb is used twice, and the whole picture is about direction.

## What it illustrates

*Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!* See the note on
Psalms 133:1.

The Hebrew is **na'im** — pleasant, delightful.

## The comparison

*It is like **fine oil upon the head**.*

The Hebrew is **kashemen hattov** — like the good oil.

## Which oil

*And you shall make from these a holy anointing oil… **it shall be a holy anointing oil**.*

Myrrh, cinnamon, cane, cassia and olive oil, compounded by the perfumer. See the note on
Exodus 30:22–25.

*Whoever compounds any like it, or whoever puts any of it on an outsider, shall be cut off.*

A single recipe, forbidden for any other use.

## Where it is poured

*Upon the head.*

The Hebrew is **al-harosh**.

## The journey

*Running down over the beard, **the beard of Aaron**, running down over **the collar of his robe**.*

The Hebrew is **yored** — descending. Used twice, and again in the next verse.

## The three stages

Head. Beard. The collar of the garment.

## What is at the bottom

The robe of the ephod, and at its hem the pomegranates and golden bells. See the note on
Exodus 28:31–35.

The oil that starts on the head ends up at the edge of the garment, on the part that touches
everything.

## The direction

Downward, in every clause.

Nothing in the picture is achieved by anyone lower reaching up.

## The identification

*The beard of Aaron.*

The psalm could have said *a man's beard*. It names the high priest.

The unity of brothers is compared to something that happened once, to one man, at an ordination.

## Where he was anointed

*And he poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him, to consecrate him.* See
the note on Leviticus 8:10–12.

## What the sons got

Not this.

The ordination oil went on Aaron's head; his sons were sprinkled with oil and blood together. See
the note on Leviticus 8:30.

The abundance is the point: enough came down that everyone stood in it.

## The next verse

*Like the dew of Hermon, descending upon the mountains of Zion.* See the note on Psalms 133:3.

The same participle, **yored**, a third time.

Hermon is in the far north; Zion is in the south. Dew from one arriving on the other is not
meteorology.

## What is commanded there

*For there **the LORD commanded the blessing — life forevermore**.*

## The word

**Tzivvah** — commanded, ordered. The blessing is placed by decree at the point where brothers dwell
together.

## Where the anointing goes

*But **you have an anointing from the Holy One**, and you know all things.* See the note on
1 John 2:20–21.

The Greek is **chrisma** — from **chriō**, to anoint. The root of **Christos**.

> **The direction.** Head, beard, collar — the verb *descending* three times in two verses. Nothing
> in the picture is reached for from below.
