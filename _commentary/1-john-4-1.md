---
key: 1 John 4:1
title: "The test is a Person"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
John tells them not to be credulous, and then gives them a test so simple it can be applied by a
child.

## Do not take every spirit at its word

*Do not take every spirit at its word. **Test them**.*

**Dokimazete** — assay, the word used for testing metal to establish whether it is what it claims to
be. The same verb Paul uses for *test all things; hold fast what is good*.

Notice the assumption. Spiritual experience is not self-authenticating. Something can be genuinely
supernatural and not from God, and John expects believers to be able to tell — which is the opposite
of both credulity and cynicism.

## The reason

*Because **a great many false prophets have fanned out across the world**.*

**Exeleluthasin** — have gone out. And John has already used that verb in this letter about the
antichrists: *they went out from us*.

These are not distant pagans. They are people who left the fellowship and are still speaking its
language.

## The test itself

*By this you know the Spirit of God: **every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the
flesh is of God***.

That is the whole examination. Not doctrinal breadth, not moral impressiveness, not power. What does
it say about Jesus Christ, and specifically about His having come in a body?

Paul gives the identical test to Corinth from the other side: *no one speaking by the Spirit of God
calls Jesus accursed, and no one can say that Jesus is Lord except by the Holy Spirit.*

Both apostles put the whole weight on a confession about a Person. The Spirit's business is to
testify of Him — *He will glorify Me, for He will take of what is Mine and declare it to you* — so a
spirit that will not do that has identified itself.

## And then he reassures them

John does not leave them anxious. Three sentences later:

***You are of God, little children, and have overcome them***, because **He who is in you is greater
than he who is in the world**.

Past tense: have overcome. He hands them the result before they have run a single test.

> **Discernment without fear.** The test is short, it is about Jesus Christ, and the outcome for the
> believer is announced in advance. You are not being sent out to police the spiritual world at your
> own risk — you are being told what to listen for, and reminded who is living in you while you
> listen.
