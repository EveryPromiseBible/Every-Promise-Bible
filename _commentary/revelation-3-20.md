key: Revelation 3:20
title: "Outside His own church, knocking"

The most quoted verse in the book, and it is not addressed to the people it is usually quoted at.

## Who is on the other side of the door

Laodicea. A church — not a crowd of unbelievers, not an individual who has never heard. A
congregation with a building, a reputation and an income.

And the risen Christ is outside it, knocking to be let in.

That is the sharpest picture in the seven letters, and it is not a threat. He is not leaving. He is
standing there.

## Standing and knocking

**Hesteka** — a perfect: I have taken my stand and I am standing. **Krouo** — present tense: I keep
knocking.

Neither verb has an endpoint in it. The posture is settled and the knocking is continuous, at the
door of the church that has just been told it is naked and does not know it.

## What He does not do

He does not force the door, and He does not walk away. The whole invitation rests on a voice being
heard and a handle being turned from the inside — which is remarkable given that He is the one
described in this same book as holding the keys of Death and Hades.

He could open it. He knocks.

## The meal

*I will walk in, and **I will sit down to a meal with him, and he with Me**.*

**Deipneso** — from **deipnon**, the evening meal. Not a snack and not a formality: the long one at
the end of the day, the one that takes hours and is the only unhurried thing in an ancient
household's schedule.

And the reciprocity is stated twice: with him, and he with Me. Nobody is being inspected. Two people
are eating.

This is the Man who was accused of nothing worse, over and over, than that *He receives sinners and
eats with them* — and whose last act before the cross was to keep a supper with the men who were
about to leave Him.

## What Laodicea had said

*I am rich, have become wealthy, and have need of nothing.*

That is the door. It was not locked by sin; it was locked by sufficiency. And His answer to a church
that needs nothing is not a rebuke followed by conditions — it is dinner.

> **He is still outside, and still knocking.** No church is beyond that offer and no person is: the
> terms are *if anybody hears My voice and opens*. **Anybody**, and one hinge. Everything He wanted
> from the wealthiest, coldest congregation of the seven was to be let in and to sit down with
> them.
