The disciples come back with lunch and find He is not hungry any more.

## Where this happens

At Jacob's well, at the sixth hour, in Samaria. The disciples had *gone away into the
city to buy food* — which is why the woman found Him alone.

The conversation with her has just ended and she has run back to the town leaving her
waterpot behind.

## The urging

*In the meantime the disciples were **urging Him**, "Rabbi, **eat something**."*

He had been *wearied from His journey* and sat down at the well because of it. John
says so plainly in verse 6. The tiredness was real and so was the hunger.

## The answer

*"**I have food to eat that you know nothing about**."*

The Greek is *food you do not know of*.

## The misunderstanding

*So the disciples said to one another, "**Has somebody brought Him something to
eat?**"*

A running feature of John's Gospel. Nicodemus asks whether a man can enter his mother's
womb; the woman at this same well asks for water so she will not have to come back and
draw.

Every time, a physical reading of a sentence meant otherwise, and every time it gives
Jesus the opening to explain.

## What the food is

*"**My food is to do the will of the One who sent Me, and to finish the work He gave
Me**."*

Two clauses: **do** the will, and **finish** the work.

The Greek for *finish* is **teleioo**, from the same root as the word He says from the
cross — *it is finished*, **tetelestai**.

He names His food, and it is a completed assignment.

## What had just happened

A Samaritan woman with five husbands had been told everything she ever did and had gone
into the town saying *come, see a Man who told me all things that I ever did. Could this
be the Christ?*

That is the meal He is talking about. It had happened while the disciples were queueing
in a shop.

## What He says immediately after

*"**Lift up your eyes and look at the fields, for they are already white for
harvest**."*

Said with the townspeople walking out toward the well across the open ground — John
notes in verse 30 that *they went out of the city and came to Him.*

The harvest He points at is arriving on the road while He speaks.

## The other statements of the same thing

*"I have come down from heaven, **not to do My own will, but the will of Him who sent
Me**."* See the note on John 6:35–40.

*"I do exactly as the Father has commanded Me."* See the note on John 14:28–31.

He says it in nearly every discourse in the Gospel.

## The outcome in Samaria

*And many of the Samaritans of that city believed in Him because of the word of the
woman… **and He stayed there two days**.*

Then: *now we believe, not because of what you said, for we ourselves have heard Him.*

> **What He did not do.** He does not refuse the food or make a point about fasting. He
> tells them there is a kind of eating they have not noticed, and then points at a field
> with people walking across it.
