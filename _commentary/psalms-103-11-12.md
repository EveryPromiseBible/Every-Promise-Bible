He picks the one direction on a globe that never meets itself.

## The two measurements

*As high as **the heavens are above the earth**… As far as **the east is from the west**.*

Vertical, then horizontal. The love is measured up; the sins are measured across.

## The first

*So great is **His love for those who fear Him**.*

The Hebrew is **chasdo** — His **chesed**. Covenant loyalty, steadfast love.

The word runs through the psalm: verse 4, verse 8, verse 11, verse 17.

## The pairing

Fear and **chesed** in one line.

The fear in this psalm is not terror — three verses later it is compared to a father pitying his
children.

## The second

*So far has He **carried our sins from us**.*

The Hebrew is **hirchiq mimmennu et-pesha'einu** — He has made our transgressions far from us.

**Pesha** — not the accidental sin, but rebellion, revolt. The strongest of the Hebrew words for
sin.

## Why east and west

North and south have poles. Travel north far enough and you begin travelling south.

East and west have no such point. Go east indefinitely and you never once start going west.

## What the psalmist chose

The only axis on which the distance cannot be closed.

## What he did not say

He did not say the sins were forgotten, or overlooked, or excused. He said they were moved, and
gave the distance.

## Who did the moving

*He has carried*. The verb is His.

## What the psalm has just said

*Who **forgives all your iniquities**, who **heals all your diseases**, who **redeems your life from
destruction**.* See the note on Psalms 103:1–5.

Four *alls* in that stanza.

## The verse before

*He has **not dealt with us according to our sins**, nor punished us according to our iniquities.*

The line immediately before the two measurements. See the note on Psalms 103:8–10.

## The verse after

*As a father pities his children, so the LORD pities those who fear Him. **For He knows our frame;
He remembers that we are dust**.* See the note on Psalms 103:13–14.

The distance is set, and then the reason: He knows what we are made of.

## Where it goes

*"**I, even I, am He who blots out your transgressions** for My own sake; and I will not remember your
sins."* See the note on Isaiah 43:25.

And *"**their sins and their lawless deeds I will remember no more**." Now where there is remission
of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.* See the note on Hebrews 10:16–18.

> **The axis.** North and south meet at a pole. East and west never do — and that is the one He
> chose to measure with.
