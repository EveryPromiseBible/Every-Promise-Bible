---
key: 2 Corinthians 4:5–6
title: "The glory of God has a face"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
Two verses, and between them Paul reaches back to the first sentence God is recorded as speaking
and lands it on a human face.

## Not ourselves

*For it is **not ourselves** we preach. We preach Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your
servants for Jesus' sake.*

The word for servants here is **doulous** — slaves, the strongest word available. Paul makes himself
the property of the Corinthians, and then adds the clause that keeps him free: **dia Iesoun**, *for
Jesus' sake*.

So they get all the service and none of the ownership. A man who is a slave to a church because of
Christ can be told what to do by Christ; a man who is a slave to a church can only be told what to
do by the church.

## The first thing God ever said

*For the God who said, **Light shall shine out of the darkness**.*

Genesis 1:3, and it is the opening line of God's recorded speech. Paul goes back past Abraham, past
Adam, to the moment before there was anything to work with.

That light had no dawn before it and nothing on earth cooperated in it. Nothing was consulted;
nothing contributed; the darkness did not improve itself first.

*Is the One who has made His light **shine in our hearts**.*

Which makes conversion a creation event, not a persuasion event. Paul has just said that the god of
this age blinds minds — and blindness is not cured by better arguments. It is cured by a voice that
says *let there be light* over a place where there was none.

## In the face of Jesus Christ

*To give us the light of knowing **the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ**.*

Moses asked to see that glory and was put in a cleft of a rock and shown a back, *for no man shall
see Me and live*.

Paul locates the same glory in a face — and a face is the one part of a person you are meant to
look at. Not a mountain, not a cloud, not a fire that had to be fenced off. A face, at eye level,
that ate with tax collectors.

And two verses later he says where that treasure is kept: *in earthen vessels, that the excellence
of the power may be of God and not of us.*

> **Look at the face.** What is required of you here is not effort but attention. The light was
> commanded by the same voice that commanded the first one, and what it shows is not a rulebook or
> a standard — it is a Person, and He is looking back. *He who has seen Me has seen the Father.*
> All the glory there is, gathered where you can safely look at it.
