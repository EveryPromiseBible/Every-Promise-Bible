The instruction is given in four parts, and one word in it is different from last time.

## Take the staff

*"**Take the staff**, and gather the people together, you and your brother Aaron."*

The Hebrew has the definite article — **the** staff, and the next verse says Moses took it
*from before the LORD*.

That is where Aaron's rod was kept. It had been laid up in front of the Testimony after
Korah's rebellion, and it had budded, blossomed and produced almonds overnight.

A dead stick with fruit on it, kept as the answer to the question of who was chosen.

## Speak

*"Then **speak to the rock in front of them all**, and **it will give up its water**."*

The Hebrew for *speak* is the ordinary verb. Nothing is to be done to the rock.

At Rephidim, thirty-eight years earlier, the instruction was **strike it**. See the note
on Exodus 17:4–7.

Same nation, same problem, different instruction.

## Who brings the water

*"**You will bring water out of the rock for them**."*

Moses is given the credit in advance, in the instruction itself.

## Who it is for

*"And give **the community and their animals** something to drink."*

The animals are included. The people had complained about them in verse 4 — *us and our
animals* — and the answer covers them.

## The obedience

*So Moses **took the staff from before the LORD, just as He had told him**.*

The first half is exact. He does what he is told.

## What happens next

He gathers the assembly, calls them rebels, asks *must we bring you water out of this
rock*, and strikes it twice. See the note on Numbers 20:10–11.

The water comes anyway.

## The two rods

Moses' rod is the one that struck the Nile and turned it to blood, struck the rock at
Horeb, and was stretched over the sea. It is a rod of judgment throughout Exodus.

Aaron's rod is the one that budded — the dead branch that produced almonds and was kept
in front of the ark.

The chapter says the staff was taken *from before the LORD*, which is where the budded
one was.

## What Paul says the rock was

*They drank from **a spiritual Rock that went with them, and that Rock was Christ**.* See
the note on 1 Corinthians 10:1–5.

## The consequence

*"**Because you did not trust Me enough to honor Me as holy** in the sight of the people
of Israel, **you will not bring this assembly into the land**."* See the note on
Numbers 20:12.

The verdict is about representation — what the action said about God in front of the
nation.

> **The change in the instruction.** Struck once, and after that spoken to. Numbers gives
> the new instruction plainly and then records that it was not followed.
