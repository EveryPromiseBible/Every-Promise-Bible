A chapter of desolation turns on one word.

## What comes before

The verses just above are about a land stripped: *thorns and briers*, *the palace
forsaken*, *the populous city deserted*, and the women of Jerusalem told to strip
themselves and put on sackcloth.

*The forts and towers will become lairs forever, a joy of wild donkeys, a pasture of
flocks.*

## The turn

*Until.*

One word, and everything after it is reversal.

## The pouring

*Until **the Spirit is poured on us from on high**.*

The Hebrew is **arah** — to be emptied out, poured until nothing is left in the vessel.

**From on high** — the source is named and it is not in the land.

## The three stages

*And **the desert becomes a fruitful field**, and **the fruitful field seems like a
forest**.*

The Hebrew word for *fruitful field* is **karmel** — orchard land, the same word as the
mountain.

Three levels: wilderness, orchard, forest. The desert does not merely recover; it goes
two steps past where it started, and the good land is re-graded as ordinary next to what
follows.

## Where justice lives

*Then **justice will dwell in the desert**, and **righteousness will live in the fruitful
field**.*

Both places, not one. The word for *dwell* is the ordinary verb for settling down and
staying.

## The fruit

*And **the fruit of righteousness will be peace**.*

Not the root. Righteousness first, peace as what grows on it.

Paul in Romans: *therefore, having been justified by faith, we have **peace** with God.*
The order is the same. See the note on Romans 5:1–2.

## The work

*And **the work of righteousness will be quietness and confidence forever**.*

The Hebrew for *work* is **abodah** — labour, service, what a thing produces by
operating.

And what it produces is:

- **Quietness** — **shaqat**, stillness, undisturbed rest. It is the word used of a land
  that *had rest* from war.
- **Confidence** — **betach**, security, safety, from the root for trusting.

Both are states of not having to do anything.

## The oddity

Righteousness works, and what its work amounts to is quietness.

Isaiah puts an active noun and a passive result in the same clause and leaves them there.

## Where else Isaiah says it

*In returning and rest you shall be saved; **in quietness and confidence shall be your
strength**.*

Same two words, in chapter 30, and there the sentence ends *but you would not*.

## What follows in the chapter

*My people will dwell in a peaceful habitation, in secure dwellings, and in quiet resting
places.*

Three words for the same thing, one after another.

> **Where it starts.** Not with reform. With something poured out from above onto a
> place described as thorns and briers — and everything in the paragraph after that is
> consequence.
