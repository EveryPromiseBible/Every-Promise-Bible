The death of Abraham, and two estranged sons at the graveside.

## The span

*Abraham lived **a hundred and seventy-five years**.*

He was seventy-five when he left Haran. A hundred years in the land he was promised and never
owned, except for one field.

## The phrase

*Then he breathed his last and died **at a good old age, full of years**.*

The Hebrew is **beseivah tovah zaqen vesavea** — in a good grey age, old and satisfied.

**Savea** — full, satisfied, having had enough. The word used of a full stomach.

## What he was told

*"**Now as for you, you shall go to your fathers in peace; you shall be buried at a good old
age**."*

Said to him in Genesis 15, at the covenant of the pieces, in the same conversation where he was
told his descendants would be strangers in a land not theirs for four hundred years. See the note
on Genesis 15:12–16.

The promise about his own death was kept word for word.

## The gathering

*And he was **gathered to his people**.*

The Hebrew is **vayye'asep el-ammav**.

An idiom, and a strange one for this man. His people were in Ur and Haran. He is buried alone in
Canaan, five hundred miles from any of them.

The phrase is used separately from the burial notice, which suggests it is not about the grave.

## The two sons

*His sons **Isaac and Ishmael** buried him.*

Ishmael was sent away with Hagar and a skin of water when Isaac was weaned.

They have not appeared together since. They bury their father together, and Genesis does not
explain how that was arranged.

## The plot

*In **the cave of Machpelah**, near Mamre, in the field of **Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite** — the
field **Abraham had bought** from the Hittites.*

The only land he ever owned.

Chapter 23 records the negotiation in full — Ephron offering it free, Abraham insisting on paying,
four hundred shekels of silver weighed out *according to the currency of the merchants*.

He would not take it as a gift.

## Why he bought it

To bury Sarah.

*I am a foreigner and a visitor among you. **Give me property for a burial place among you**.*

The first thing he owned in the promised land was a grave.

## Who is there

*There Abraham was buried **beside his wife Sarah**.*

And in time Isaac, Rebekah, Leah, and Jacob — who made Joseph swear to carry him out of Egypt for
it.

Six of them, in a field bought from a Hittite.

## What Hebrews says

*By faith he dwelt in the land of promise as in a foreign country… for he waited for the city
which has foundations, **whose builder and maker is God**.*

*These all died in faith, **not having received the promises**, but having seen them afar off.* See
the note on Hebrews 11:13–16.

## The same phrase later

*Then Isaac breathed his last and died, **an old man full of years**.* See the note on
Genesis 35:27–29.

*And he died at a good old age, **full of days**, full of riches and honour.* — of David. See the
note on 1 Chronicles 29:26–28.

*And Job grew old, **full of days and full of gladness**.* See the note on
Job 42:16–17.

Four men, and the same Hebrew word each time.

> **The word.** **Savea** — satisfied, having had enough. Used of a man who owned one field in the
> country he was promised, and bought it to bury his wife in.
