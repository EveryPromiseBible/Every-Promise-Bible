Three descriptions of what they were, and then one sentence about what was done.

## What they were

*You were once **strangers to Him, cut off from Him, enemies in the way your minds ran**.*

The Greek is **apellotriomenous** — alienated. And **echthrous te dianoia** — enemies in the mind.

**Dianoia** — the mind, the faculty of thinking.

The hostility is located in how they thought, not in what happened to them.

## The evidence

*And it **showed in the wicked things you did**.*

The Greek is **en tois ergois tois ponerois** — in evil works.

The conduct is presented as the symptom. The mind is the site.

## The turn

*Yet now **He has brought you back**.*

The Greek is **apokatellaxen** — reconciled. An intensified compound, and Paul uses it only in
this letter and Ephesians.

Aorist. Done.

## The means

*In **His own flesh-and-blood body, through death**.*

The Greek is **en to somati tes sarkos autou dia tou thanatou** — in the body of His flesh through
death.

The wording is deliberately physical, aimed at the Colossian teaching that matter was a problem.

## The purpose

*To **set you before Him holy, with no flaw in you and no charge against you in His sight**.*

Three words: **hagious** — holy. **Amomous** — without blemish. **Anenkletous** — unaccusable.

**Amomos** — the sacrificial word for an animal without defect.

**Anenkletos** — a legal word. Nobody can bring a charge.

## Where the presentation happens

*In His sight* — **katenopion autou**.

Not in the neighbours' estimation. In front of Him.

## The condition

*This holds **if you stay with the faith, settled on your foundation and not to be shifted**.*

The Greek is **ei ge epimenete te pistei tethemeliomenoi kai hedraioi**.

**Tethemeliomenoi** — having been founded. **Hedraioi** — seated, settled, immovable.

## What the clause does

It has been argued over since the Reformation.

The Greek **ei ge** with the indicative assumes the condition is being met — closer to *since you
continue* than *provided that you might*.

Paul writes the same construction elsewhere and then says *He who has begun a good work in you will
complete it*.

## What is not to be moved from

*Never moved away from **the hope that the good news held out to you**.*

The Greek is **me metakinoumenoi apo tes elpidos tou euangeliou**.

The anchor named is hope, not effort.

## The scope

*The news announced **to every creature under heaven**.*

The Greek is **en pase ktisei te hupo ton ouranon** — in all creation under heaven.

## The signature

*The news of which **I, Paul, became a servant**.*

The Greek is **diakonos** — servant.

He says it again five verses later: *of which I became a minister according to the stewardship
from God*.

## What the letter is arguing against

Colossae had teachers pressing food rules, festivals, angel worship and *do not touch, do not
taste, do not handle*.

*Beware lest anyone cheat you through philosophy and empty deceit… **for in Him dwells all the
fullness of the Godhead bodily; and you are complete in Him**.* See the note on
Colossians 2:8–10.

## What is on the other side of the sentence

*And you, who once were alienated… **now He has reconciled**.*

The verse is one long before-and-after, and the hinge is a body and a death.

## Who does the presenting

The sentence has a subject and it is easy to lose in the grammar: **He** reconciled you, in order
that **He** might present you.

You are not asked to produce the holiness, and you are not asked to conduct the presentation. The
One who did the reconciling walks you in.

*Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling, and **to present you faultless** before the
presence of His glory with exceeding joy.*

**Amomous** again in Jude — the same sacrificial word — and it is His ability that is in view, not
ours. Paul says the same thing of Christ and the church in Ephesians: *that He might **present it to
Himself** a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle.*

The presenter and the cleanser are the same Person. Nobody is going to be handed over at the door to
a standard He has not already met on their behalf.

> **The three words.** Holy, without blemish, unaccusable — one sacrificial term and one legal
> one, describing a presentation made in His sight rather than anybody else's.
