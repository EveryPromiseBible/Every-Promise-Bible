Two short sentences, and the second one is an instruction Peter had once been given and failed.

## The end, and what it is offered as

*Everything is coming to its end, and it is close.*

**Panton to telos engiken** — the end of all things has drawn near. The verb is a perfect: it has
come near and it is near.

Notice what Peter does *not* do with that. There is no alarm, no calculation, no instruction to
withdraw. He is writing to people already being burned out of their livelihoods for the name, and
the nearness of the end is offered as a reason to be steady.

For a believer the approach of the end is not a threat. *When these things begin to happen, look up
and lift up your heads, because your redemption draws near.*

## A clear head

*So **keep a clear head**.*

**Sophronesate** — be of sound mind, sane, self-possessed. It is the opposite of the state a rumour
about the end of the world usually produces in a congregation.

Peter adds a second word in the same breath: **nepsate**, be sober — literally not drunk, and by
extension not carried away by anything.

## Awake enough to pray

*And **stay awake enough to pray**.*

Now hear where that sentence comes from. In a garden, on the last night, the same man was told:
*watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation.* And Mark records the answer — *He came and found
them sleeping, and said to **Peter**, "Simon, are you sleeping? Could you not watch one hour?"*

Peter is passing on the one instruction the Gospels record him failing, and he does not mention that
he failed it. He does not have to. Everybody reading his letter knew.

That is what restoration looks like in practice: a man handing on the very thing he got wrong,
without shame, because the One who woke him up that night is the One who later cooked him breakfast
on a beach and gave him the sheep.

> **The end is near — so pray.** Not so pack, and not so panic. Peter's whole application of the
> last day is a clear head and an open line to the Father, which is the calm of someone who knows
> exactly what is coming and who is bringing it. *This same Jesus… will so come in like manner as you
> saw Him go.*
