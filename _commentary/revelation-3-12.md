---
key: Revelation 3:12
title: A pillar, and three names
updated: 2026-08-07
---

The promise is made to a church in a city that kept falling down.

## The condition

*Whoever wins.*

The Greek is **ho nikōn** — the one overcoming.

Each of the seven letters ends with this participle and a promise attached.

## The promise

*"**I will set him in the temple of My God as a pillar there**."*

The Greek is **stulon en tō naō tou Theou mou**.

**Naos** — the sanctuary itself, not the precincts.

## What a pillar does

Stands still and holds weight.

Nothing in a building is more fixed.

## The city it was written to

Philadelphia sat on a fault line.

The earthquake of AD 17 destroyed it, and the aftershocks went on for years. Strabo records that the
inhabitants took to living outside the walls because the buildings kept cracking.

## The clause

*"And **he will never have to go outside again**."*

The Greek is **exō ou mē exelthē eti**.

## What that meant there

People who had spent years sleeping in the fields, told they will never be put outside a building
again.

## The first name

*"I will write on him **the name of My God**."*

Ownership, and the priestly plate: *HOLINESS TO THE LORD*, engraved and bound on the turban. See the
note on Exodus 28:36–38.

## The second

*"And **the name of My God's city — the new Jerusalem, the one coming down out of heaven from My
God**."*

Citizenship.

Philadelphia had been renamed twice by imperial patrons — Neocaesarea after the earthquake relief,
and later Flavia. A city used to taking a benefactor's name is told which city's name will be on
it.

## The third

*"And I will write **My own new name** on him."*

The Greek is **to onoma mou to kainon**.

Not disclosed.

## What is not said

What the name is.

The letter ends on a name withheld, in the same book that says *He had a name written that no one
knew except Himself*. See the note on Revelation 19:11–13.

## What the church had

*"You have **a little strength**, have kept My word, and have not denied My name."*

And *"see, I have set before you **an open door, and no one can shut it**."* See the note on
Revelation 3:7–8.

## What He had promised them

*"I will keep you from the hour when everything is put to the test."* See the note on
Revelation 3:9–10.

## The verse before this one

*"Behold, I am coming quickly! **Hold fast what you have, that no one may take your crown**."* See the
note on Revelation 3:11.

The Greek is **stephanos** — the victor's wreath, not the royal diadem.

## The pattern of the seven

Ephesus is promised the tree of life. Smyrna, no hurt from the second death. Pergamos, hidden manna
and a white stone. Thyatira, the morning star. Sardis, white garments and the name kept in the book.

Laodicea, a seat on the throne. See the note on Revelation 3:21–22.

## Where the pillar image goes

*Having been built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the
chief cornerstone… **in whom you also are being built together for a dwelling place of God**.* See the
note on Ephesians 2:19–20.

> **The local reading.** A city whose buildings kept coming down in aftershocks, and whose people
> slept outside the walls, is promised a pillar that never has to leave the sanctuary.
