The largest building project in Israel's history, and the site was silent.

## The sentence

*The stone was **dressed and finished at the quarry before it ever reached the
house** — so that **no hammer, no chisel, no iron tool of any kind was heard in the
house** while it was going up. **Not a sound.**"*

Every stone was cut, squared and finished miles away, underground, and carried up
already fitted.

## What that requires

The measurements had to be exact before anything was moved. A stone dressed in the
dark of a quarry has to arrive and sit down into a place prepared for it, with no
adjustment possible on site.

There is no trimming at the wall. It fits or it does not.

## Where the stones came from

*Eighty thousand who **cut stone up in the hill country**… at the king's command they
**quarried great, costly stones, dressing them square**.*

The shaping happened in the quarry — Jerusalem's stone was cut from tunnels under the
city, in the dark, out of sight of the building.

The noise was all somewhere else.

## The scale

Thirty thousand in shifts to Lebanon, seventy thousand carrying loads, eighty
thousand quarrying, and thirty-three hundred supervisors.

Nearly two hundred thousand men, and the site was quiet.

## No iron

The specification is not incidental. When Israel built an altar, the law said *you
shall not build it of hewn stone, for if you use your tool on it, you have profaned
it.*

Iron on stone, in that law, spoiled it.

## The ledges

*He set **narrow ledges all around the outside** of the house, so the supporting
beams would rest on those ledges **and not be driven into the walls of the house
itself**.*

The side rooms lean on the temple and nothing is driven into it. The main structure
is not pierced to carry the additions.

## The stair

*A **stairway wound up** to the middle story, and from the middle to the third.*

The Hebrew is a winding stair. You could not see the top from the bottom.

## Where Peter takes this

*You also, **as living stones**, are being built up a spiritual house, a holy
priesthood.*

And of Christ: *a living stone, rejected indeed by men, but chosen by God and
precious.* See the note on 1 Peter 2:4–5.

Paul builds the same picture: *in whom the whole building, being fitted together,
grows into a holy temple in the Lord.* See the note on Ephesians 2:17–19.

The stones in Solomon's temple were finished in the dark and set in place without a
sound.

> **How long it took.** Seven years, from Ziv in the fourth year to Bul in the
> eleventh. And Solomon spent thirteen years on his own house. The book records both
> figures without comment.
