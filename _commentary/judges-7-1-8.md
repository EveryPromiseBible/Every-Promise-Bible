---
key: Judges 7:1–8
title: Too many
updated: 2026-08-09
---
An army is assembled to fight, and God's first objection is that it is too big.

## The problem

*"**You have too many people with you** for Me to hand Midian over to them."*

Thirty-two thousand men, against a Midianite force Judges describes as *lying in the valley as
numerous as locusts*, with camels *without number, as the sand by the seashore*.

They are outnumbered several times over, and the word is *too many*.

## The reason, stated outright

*"**Israel might boast against Me and say, 'My own hand saved me.'**"*

That is the whole of it. Not tactics, not logistics, not purity. God names the sentence He
will not have said afterwards, and reduces the army until it cannot be said.

*That **no flesh should glory in His presence**… that, as it is written, "He who glories, let
him glory in the LORD."* See the note on 1 Corinthians 1:26–31.

## The first cut

*"Anyone who is **trembling with fear** may turn back."*

Twenty-two thousand go. Two-thirds of the army, released without penalty, and the provision is
straight out of Deuteronomy — *what man is there who is fearful and fainthearted? Let him go
and return to his house.* See the note on Deuteronomy 20:2–4.

Nobody is shamed for taking it.

## The second

*"Bring them down to the water, and **I will sift them for you there**."*

Three hundred out of ten thousand, on how they drank.

Commentators have argued for centuries about which group was the alert one, and the passage
never says. It offers no reason at all — *the one I point out to go with you will go; and
anyone I hold back will not.*

The selection is not a merit test. It is a number being brought down.

## What is left

Three hundred men, holding **the provisions and the trumpets** of everybody who went home.

No swords are mentioned. What they carry into the night is food, horns, and later jars and
torches. See the note on Judges 7:19–25.

## The arithmetic

Thirty-two thousand to three hundred — a hundredth of the force, against an army too large to
count.

*"**Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit**," says the LORD of hosts.*

> **The objection.** Not that they were too few. God looked at thirty-two thousand men facing
> an army like locusts and said there were too many — and named the exact sentence He was
> preventing.
