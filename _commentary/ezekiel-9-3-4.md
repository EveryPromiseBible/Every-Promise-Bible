Before anything happens to the city, one man is sent through it with a pen.

## The glory moves

*Now **the glory of the God of Israel had risen from over the cherub** where it had
rested and **moved to the threshold** of the house.*

Ezekiel tracks this through the whole book — the glory lifts from between the
cherubim, goes to the threshold, then to the east gate, then to the mountain east of
the city, and leaves.

It moves slowly, and by stages, and it is the saddest sequence in the prophets.

Here it has taken the first step.

## The man with the writing case

*The man **clothed in linen** who had **the writing case at his side**.*

Six men had come in with weapons. This one has neither armour nor a weapon.

Linen is priestly cloth. The writing case is a scribe's — an inkhorn worn at the
belt.

## The instruction

*"**Pass through the midst of the city**… and **set a mark upon the foreheads**."*

He goes first. Before the six with the weapons are told to do anything, this one goes
through and marks people.

## The mark

The Hebrew word is **tav** — the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet, and it is used
here as the name of the mark itself.

In the old paleo-Hebrew script, that letter was written as a cross.

## Who gets it

*"The people who **sigh and grieve over all the abominations** that are done within
it."*

Two verbs, and both are about grief.

Not the people who did nothing wrong. Not the ones who protested, or organised, or
left. The ones who were still grieved by what they were living in.

Whatever they had or had not done, that is what the mark is set on.

## What follows

The six are told to begin, *and do not let your eye spare, nor have any pity* — and
then: *"but **do not come near anyone on whom is the mark**."*

The instruction is explicit, and the mark is the only thing distinguishing anybody.

## Where it begins

*"**Begin at My sanctuary.**" So they began with the elders who were before the
temple.*

The judgement starts at the building and with the leadership.

Peter says the same thing: *the time has come for judgement to begin at the house of
God.*

## What Ezekiel does

*I fell on my face and cried out, "Ah, Lord GOD! **Will You destroy all the remnant
of Israel** in pouring out Your fury on Jerusalem?"*

He is the only one in the vision who intercedes.

> **Where the mark turns up again.** Revelation 7: *do not harm the earth, the sea, or
> the trees **till we have sealed the servants of our God on their foreheads**.* Same
> order — the sealing first, and then the rest.
