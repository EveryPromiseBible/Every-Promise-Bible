One verse, and it explains where the temple came from.

## The claim

*"All of this," David said, "**the LORD made clear to me in writing, His own hand upon me** —
every detail of the plan, made plain."*

The Hebrew is **bikhtav miyyad YHWH** — in writing, from the hand of the LORD.

## What "all of this" covers

The chapter before this verse lists it: the porch, the houses, the treasuries, the upper
rooms, the inner rooms, the place of the mercy seat, the courts, the chambers, the divisions
of priests and Levites — and then weights. The gold for each lampstand by weight. The silver
for each table. The gold for the cherubim.

Not an idea of a temple. A specification with numbers in it.

## Where the pattern had come from before

*"According to all that I show you, **the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its
furnishings**, just so you shall make it."*

Moses, on the mountain. And Hebrews says what those patterns were patterns **of**: *the copy
and shadow of the heavenly things*. See the note on Hebrews 8:6–7.

Two buildings, five hundred years apart, and neither architect designed anything.

## What David contributed

Materials, organisation, and the whole of his personal fortune — and not one design decision.

He is the only person in Scripture given plans for a building he is forbidden to build. See
the note on 2 Samuel 7:1–3.

## The other thing written by that hand

*And the tablets were the work of God, and **the writing was the writing of God**, engraved on
the tablets.*

The only two things Scripture says God wrote Himself are the ten commandments and the plans
for the place where they would be kept — under a lid with blood on it.

## Where the building goes

*"**Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up**"… but He was speaking of the
temple of His body.*

**Jesus** says it in the courts of the building this verse is the blueprint for.

And then: *do you not know that **you are the temple of God** and that the Spirit of God dwells
in you?*

## What that means for the blueprint

The specification was exact because it was a picture of something exact. Every cubit was
describing a Person before there was anybody to recognise Him in it.

*For **all the promises of God in Him are Yes**, and in Him Amen.*

> **The two things written by that hand.** The commandments, and the plans for the house they
> were kept in. David received the second the way Moses received the first, and neither man
> drew a line of it.
