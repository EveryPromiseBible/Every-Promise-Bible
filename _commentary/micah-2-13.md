title: "The One who breaks open the way"

Two verses of pure promise dropped into a chapter about land-grabbing, and the second one has a
title in it.

## Where it sits

Micah 2 is about men who *lie awake planning wickedness*, who *set their eyes on fields, and seize
them*, and who tell the prophet *stop your preaching*.

And then, without transition, a shepherd and a gate.

## The gathering

*"I will surely gather all of you, Jacob… **like sheep in a fold, like a flock in the middle of its
pasture**, and the sound of the crowd will fill the land."*

The Hebrew doubles the verb — **asoph e'esoph** — I will surely gather.

*"And **other sheep I have which are not of this fold**; them also I must bring, and they will hear
My voice; and there will be one flock and one shepherd."*

## The title

*"**The One who breaks open the way** will go up before them."*

The Hebrew is **happoretz** — the Breaker, the one who bursts through. It is a participle used as a
title, with the definite article, and it occurs nowhere else in Scripture in this sense.

**Paratz** is the verb for a wall being breached. The picture is a sheepfold: a dry-stone enclosure
with one narrow opening, packed with animals, and somebody going first through the gap so the flock
can follow.

## The order

*"They will **break through the gate and pass out to freedom**. Their King will pass through ahead
of them, **the LORD at their head**."*

Three times in one verse somebody goes first. The flock does not open the way; it follows through
an opening already made.

*"**I am the door of the sheep**… I am the door. If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved, and will
go in and out and find pasture."*

*Therefore, brethren, having boldness to enter the Holiest by the blood of **Jesus**, by **a new and
living way which He consecrated for us, through the veil**, that is, His flesh.*

**Hebrews** 10, and the Greek for *new* is **prosphaton** — freshly slaughtered. The way is opened
by the one going through it.

*This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and steadfast, and which enters the Presence
behind the veil, where **the forerunner has entered for us, even Jesus**.*

**Prodromos** — the one who runs ahead. It is **Hebrews** 6's word and it is Micah's title in Greek.

## The two names

*Their **King**… **the LORD** at their head.*

The verse puts both in apposition. Whoever the Breaker is, Micah has Him as king and as the LORD in
the same sentence.

## And what the chapter had just said

*"**Get up and go, for this is no place of rest.**"*

Two verses before the gate. The people being told there is no rest where they are are the ones the
Breaker leads out.

*"Come to Me, all you who labour and are heavy laden, and **I will give you rest**."*

> **The word.** *Happoretz* — the Breaker, with the article, as a title. A sheepfold has one narrow
> gap, and somebody has to go through it first.
