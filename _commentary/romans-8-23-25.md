Creation groans, and so do the people who already have the Spirit.

## The firstfruits

*"**We ourselves, who hold the first handful of the Spirit's harvest.**"*

**Aparchen tou Pneumatos** — the firstfruit of the Spirit. The sheaf waved before the harvest came
in, as a pledge that the rest was coming.

*In Him also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise, **who is the guarantee
of our inheritance** until the redemption of the purchased possession.*

**Arrabon** — the deposit paid at a betrothal. Same idea, different picture: what is held now is a
down payment, and it is on somebody else's account.

## The groaning

*"**We groan deep within as we wait.**"*

**Paul** has creation groaning in verse 22, believers groaning here, and the Spirit groaning in verse
26 — *with groanings which cannot be uttered*.

Three groans in five verses, and the third is God's.

## What is being waited for

*"**The day we are placed as sons — the redemption of our body.**"*

**Huiothesian** — adoption, the legal placing of somebody as a son. **Paul** has already said in verse
15 that they received the Spirit of adoption.

So it is both: given, and not yet delivered. The status is settled and the body has not caught up.

*Beloved, now we are children of God; and **it has not yet been revealed what we shall be**, but we
know that when He is revealed, we shall be like Him.*

## The definition of hope

*"**Hope that is seen is not hope; for why does one still hope for what he sees?**"*

*Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, **the evidence of things not seen**.*

*Now hope does not disappoint, because **the love of God has been poured out in our hearts by the Holy
Spirit** who was given to us.*

The certainty is not in the visibility. It is in who left the deposit.

*In Him also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise… **to the praise of
His glory**.*

And the redemption being waited for is the one already paid: *in Him we have **redemption through
His blood**, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of His **grace**.* What Ephesians 1
says was purchased, Romans 8 says has not yet been collected.

> **The two pictures.** A first sheaf and a betrothal deposit. Both mean the same thing — what is
> held now is a fraction of something already bought, and the buyer is not the one holding it.
