title: The oil that made somebody an anointed one
updated: 2026-08-09
---
A recipe, given by weight, for the oil that puts a man into office — and the word for what it
makes him is the word the whole Bible is waiting for.

## The spices

*"Five hundred shekels of **liquid myrrh**, two hundred fifty of **fragrant cinnamon**, two
hundred fifty of **fragrant cane**, and five hundred of **cassia**… plus a hin of olive oil."*

Four spices in an olive oil base. The weights are given exactly and the two outer ones are
double the two inner ones.

**Myrrh** is the burial spice — the one the women carried to the tomb, and one of the three
things brought to a house in Bethlehem.

## Where the same list turns up

*Your throne, O God, is forever and ever… You love righteousness and hate wickedness; therefore
God, Your God, **has anointed You with the oil of gladness** more than Your companions. **All
Your garments are scented with myrrh and aloes and cassia**.*

Myrrh and cassia, off this page, on the garments of a King the psalm addresses as God — and
Hebrews quotes the whole passage and applies it to the Son.

## The word

To pour this oil on a man is to **mashach** him.

The participle is **mashiach** — anointed one. **Messiah.** The Greek is **christos**.

Every use of the title runs back to a jar of this mixture. Priests were anointed, kings were
anointed, and the one who would be both had the word taken over as His name.

## What it is poured on

*"The Tent of Meeting, the ark of the Testimony, the table, the lampstand, the altar of
incense, the altar of burnt offering, and the basin."*

Every object in the tent, and then *Aaron and his sons too*.

*Whatever touches them becomes holy.*

The holiness travels outward by contact. It is catching.

## The two prohibitions

*"**Never pour it on just anyone's body**, and **never mix up another batch** with this same
formula."*

It cannot go on ordinary flesh, and it cannot be reproduced. There is one recipe, one supply,
and one class of person it may touch.

## Where it ran

*It is like the precious oil upon the head, running down on the beard, the beard of Aaron,
running down on the edge of his garments.* See the note on Psalms 133:2.

It is poured in one place — the head — and everything else that gets any is downstream of that.
Nobody's beard was anointed directly.

## Who has it now

*But **you have an anointing from the Holy One**, and you know all things… **the anointing which
you have received from Him abides in you**.*

The oil that could not go on ordinary flesh, and could not be duplicated, and was poured only
on the head — and John writes to ordinary people and tells them they have it.

Because it was poured on the Head, and it ran down.

> **The word the recipe makes.** **Mashach** — to anoint. **Mashiach** — anointed one. The title
> the whole Old Testament is reaching for is manufactured, by weight, in this paragraph.
