The clearest thing Jesus ever says about how the Spirit works, and it is almost
entirely about restraint.

## What Jesus withholds, and why

*There is much more I have to say to you, but you are not able to carry it now.*

The word is about bearing a weight. He is not keeping anything back for the sake
of mystery; He is not loading men who are already at their limit. It is the night
before the arrest.

That sets up everything after it. The Spirit's coming is described first as the
means by which the rest arrives — later, when they can carry it.

## The three things He does not do

- **He will not speak on His own** — He speaks what He hears
- **He will glorify Me** — not Himself
- **He will take what is Mine and make it known**

Jesus says the second one outright: the Spirit's work is to make Christ look like
what He is. That is a usable test. Something that draws attention to itself, or
to the person carrying it, is not doing what is described here.

> **"Everything the Father has is Mine."** Jesus adds this as the explanation for
> why the Spirit taking what is His counts as taking from the Father too. In four
> lines all three are named and their work is shown to be one work — the Father's
> possession, the Son's to give, the Spirit's to deliver. It is one of the
> plainest trinitarian sentences in the Gospels, and it arrives as an aside.
