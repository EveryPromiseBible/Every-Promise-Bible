The shortest summary in the letter, and it is built as a pair.

## The first

*If **the Son is yours, the life is yours**.*

The Greek is **ho echon ton huion echei ten zoen** — he who has the Son has the life.

Not *will have*. Present tense.

And the definite article: **ten zoen**, the life. A specific one.

## The second

*If **the Son of God is not yours, then neither is the life**.*

The same construction, negated.

John gives the full title in the second half — *the Son of God* — where the first half has *the
Son*.

## What is not offered

Any middle position.

The whole letter works this way. Light or darkness. Truth or a lie. Of God or of the world.
Loving the brother or abiding in death.

John does not deal in degrees, and this verse is the pattern of the book in one line.

## What the verse before it says

*And this is the testimony: that **God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His
Son**.*

**In His Son** — that is where it is located, and the next verse is the arithmetic that follows
from it.

If the life is in a person, then having the person and having the life are one transaction.

## The word

**Zoe** — John's word. The life itself, as opposed to **bios**, the span or the livelihood.

The Gospel uses it constantly: *in Him was life*, *I am the resurrection and the life*, *I have
come that they may have life*, *the words that I speak to you are spirit, and they are life*.

## What follows immediately

*These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, **that you may
know that you have eternal life**.* See the note on 1 John 5:13.

Verse 12 is the statement; verse 13 says why he wrote it.

The purpose is not to sort people into categories. It is so the people already in the first one
should know it.

## The testimony being summarised

*If we receive the witness of men, the witness of God is greater… **He who believes in the Son
of God has the witness in himself**.*

And: *he who does not believe God has made Him a liar, because he has not believed the testimony
that God has given of His Son*.

## Where the same shape appears

*He who believes in the Son has everlasting life; and he who does not believe the Son shall not
see life.*

John the Baptist, at the end of chapter 3 of the Gospel, and the construction is identical.

## What the letter says at the very end

*And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us an understanding, that we may know
Him who is true; and we are in Him who is true, in His Son Jesus Christ. **This is the true God
and eternal life**.*

The last sentence of the letter before the closing line identifies the two as the same thing.

> **The shape of it.** One sentence and its negative, and nothing in between. If the person is
> yours, so is the life — because the life is where the verse before says it is.
