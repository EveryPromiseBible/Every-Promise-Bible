title: "I will gather the lame"

Three categories of people nobody would build a nation out of, and God says He is going to.

## The three

*"I will gather **the lame**. I will bring together **those who were driven away**, and **all the ones
I have brought to grief**."*

The Hebrew is **hatzole'ah** — the limping one. It is the word used of Jacob after the wrestling at
the ford: *he limped on his hip.* See the note on Genesis 32:27–32.

**Hanniddachah** — the banished, the driven-out. And **asher hare'oti** — the ones I have treated
badly, from **ra'a**, to do harm.

The third is the hardest. God says the people He is gathering are people He afflicted.

## The shepherd's language

The three words together are a shepherd counting a damaged flock: the lame, the strays, and the
injured.

*"**I will seek what was lost and bring back what was driven away, bind up the broken and strengthen
what was sick.**"*

Ezekiel 34, and it is the same list in the same order, in the chapter that ends *I will set up over
them one shepherd, My servant David.*

*"**I am the good shepherd.** The good shepherd gives His life for the sheep."*

## What He makes of them

*"I will make **the lame into a remnant**, and **the ones who were cast far off into a strong
nation**."*

The Hebrew is **le-she'erit** and **legoy atzum** — a remnant, and a mighty nation.

The material is not upgraded first. The limping are made the remnant while limping.

*But **God has chosen the weak things of the world to put to shame the things which are mighty**…
that no flesh should glory in His presence.*

*"**Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in here the poor and the maimed
and the lame and the blind**"… "Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in,
that my house may be filled."*

**Luke** 14. **Jesus** tells a parable about a banquet whose guest list is Micah 4:6, and the reason
given is that the house must be full.

## Who the lame turned out to be

*"Go and tell John the things which you hear and see: the blind see and **the lame walk**, the lepers
are cleansed and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to
them."*

*Then the blind and the lame came to Him in the temple, and He healed them.*

**Matthew** 21, on the day after He turned over the tables. The two groups barred by custom from
the temple courts walk in, and He heals them there.

## The reign

*"And **the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion from that day and forever**."*

*"And He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and of His kingdom there will be no end."*

> **The third category.** *All the ones I have brought to grief.* God names Himself as the cause of
> some of the damage in the flock He is gathering, and gathers them anyway — and does not wait
> until the limping stops.
