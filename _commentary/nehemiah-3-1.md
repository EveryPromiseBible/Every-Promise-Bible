A building register, and the order it is written in is the point.

## What the chapter is

A list. Forty-odd work parties, named, each with the section of wall they rebuilt.

It reads like a subscription roll and it is one of the most complete pictures of a city's
social structure anywhere in the Old Testament — priests, goldsmiths, perfumers, district
rulers, and *Shallum… he and his daughters*.

## Who starts

*Then **Eliashib the high priest rose up with his fellow priests**.*

The priests take the first section. Not the governor, and not the engineers.

**Eliashib** — the name means *God restores*, or *God brings back*.

## The gate

*And **rebuilt the Sheep Gate**.*

The gate on the north side, nearest the temple, through which the sacrificial animals were
brought in.

Every lamb that died on the altar came through this door.

## Where it is in John

*Now there is in Jerusalem **by the Sheep Gate** a pool, which is called in Hebrew
Bethesda.* See the note on John 5:1–5.

The pool with the five porches and the crowd of sick people is at this gate.

**Bethesda** — house of mercy, or house of outpouring.

## The setting apart

*Setting it apart as **holy** and hanging its doors.*

The Hebrew is *they sanctified it* — **qiddeshuhu**.

It is the only gate in the chapter that is consecrated, and it is consecrated twice: this
gate and the towers beyond it.

The Fish Gate, the Valley Gate, the Dung Gate, the Fountain Gate, the Water Gate, the Horse
Gate, the East Gate — all rebuilt, none sanctified.

## The order of the chapter

The list runs anticlockwise round the city and comes back to the Sheep Gate at the end.
Verse 32: *and between the upper room at the corner, as far as the sheep gate, the goldsmiths
and the merchants made repairs.*

The circuit begins and ends at the same door.

## The two towers

*As far as **the Tower of the Hundred and the Tower of Hananel**, and these too they set
apart.*

**Hananel** means *God is gracious*.

Zechariah names the same tower in a promise about the city's future extent, and Jeremiah
does too: *the city shall be built for the LORD from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner
Gate.*

## What the wall was for

Nehemiah had heard, in Susa, that *the wall of Jerusalem is also broken down, and its gates
are burned with fire* — and *sat down and wept, and mourned for many days*.

Fifty-two days later the wall was up. *And it happened, when all our enemies heard of it…
that they were very disheartened in their own eyes; for they perceived that this work was
done by our God.*

## The gates in the list

The Sheep Gate is where the sacrifice comes in, and it is first.

The Water Gate is where Ezra reads the Law aloud to the people from morning till midday, and
they weep.

The order in the chapter is the order of the city's life.

> **Which gate was consecrated.** Only the one the lambs came through — and the pool with
> five porches and a paralysed man in them was lying right beside it.
