Two verses telling a people to stop remembering the one thing they were commanded to remember.

## The instruction

*Forget the former things. **Do not dwell on the past**.*

The Hebrew is **al-tizkeru rishonot vqadmoniyyot al-titbonanu** — do not remember former things,
and do not consider ancient things.

## Why that is startling

Israel's whole religious life was organised around remembering.

*Remember this day in which you went out of Egypt.* The Passover, the feast of unleavened bread,
the stones at Gilgal, the tassels on the garment — all of them memory devices.

## What has just been said

*Thus says the LORD, **who makes a way in the sea and a path through the mighty waters**, who brings
forth the chariot and horse, the army and the power.*

*They shall lie down together, they shall not rise; they are extinguished, quenched like a wick.*

The Red Sea, described in the four verses immediately before.

## What the instruction means

Not *forget the exodus*.

He has just recited it, and then says: stop looking at it, because something is happening now.

The former thing being set aside is the greatest thing in the national memory.

## The announcement

*See, **I am doing a new thing**.*

The Hebrew is **hineni oseh chadashah** — behold, I am doing a new thing.

Present participle. In progress.

## The timing

*Now **it springs up**.*

The Hebrew is **attah titzmach** — now it sprouts.

**Tzamach** — to sprout, to spring up, of plants breaking ground.

The related noun **tzemach** — the Branch — is a title in Isaiah, Jeremiah and Zechariah.

## The question

*Do you not perceive it?*

The Hebrew is **halo teda'uha** — will you not know it?

The new thing is already visible, and the question is whether they have noticed.

A sprout is not obvious. It is the smallest stage at which anything can be seen at all.

## The two images

*I am making **a way in the wilderness** and **rivers in the desert**.*

The Hebrew is **derek bammidbar** and **neharot bishimon**.

A road, and water.

## What both mean

The former thing was a way through water — a path opened in the sea. See the note on
Exodus 14:21–25.

The new thing is water in a road — rivers in a place with none.

The same two elements, reversed.

## Who notices

*The **wild animals honour Me, the jackals and the owls**, because I give water in the wilderness.*
See the note on Isaiah 43:20–21.

## What comes just before the chapter's turn

*"**Fear not, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine**."*

*"When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and through the rivers, they shall not
overflow you."* See the note on Isaiah 43:1–4.

## What comes after

*"You have not brought Me the sheep for your burnt offerings… **but you have burdened Me with your
sins**."*

*"**I, even I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake; and I will not remember your
sins**."*

The chapter that tells them not to remember ends with God saying what He will not remember.

## What Paul says

*Forgetting those things which are behind and reaching forward to those things which are ahead.*
See the note on Philippians 3:12.

> **The reversal.** The old thing was a road through water. The new one is water in a road — and
> the question is whether anybody has noticed a sprout.
