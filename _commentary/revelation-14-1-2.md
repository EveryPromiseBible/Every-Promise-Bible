After the darkest chapter in the book, John looks and sees somebody standing.

## What comes before

Chapter 13: the beast out of the sea, the beast out of the earth, the mark on the right
hand or the forehead, and nobody able to buy or sell without it.

Then the chapter break, and: *then I looked*.

## The posture

*There was **the Lamb, standing** on Mount Sion.*

The Greek is **hestos** — standing. The same participle used in chapter 5, where the Lamb
stands *as though it had been slain*. See the note on Revelation 5:7–8.

Not enthroned in this scene. Standing, on a hill, with a crowd.

## The place

*Mount Sion.*

The temple hill. The psalm says *beautiful in elevation, the joy of the whole earth, is
Mount Zion*, and Hebrews uses it as the destination: *you have come to Mount Zion and to
the city of the living God.* See the note on Hebrews 12:18–24.

Two mountains in that letter — one that could be touched and burned with fire, and this
one.

## The number

*And **a hundred and forty-four thousand** were with Him.*

Twelve squared, times a thousand. The number is introduced in chapter 7, sealed *out of
every tribe of the children of Israel*.

## The mark

*With **His Father's name written on their foreheads**.*

The chapter before had a mark on the forehead or the hand, without which nobody could
trade.

This is the same location and a different name. Revelation sets the two marks opposite
each other by putting them one chapter apart.

## The sound

*Then I heard a sound out of heaven **like a great weight of water falling**, like **a huge
roll of thunder**.*

The Greek is *like the voice of many waters and like the voice of loud thunder*.

Both images are already in the book: chapter 1 describes the voice of the risen Christ as
*the sound of many waters*.

## The turn

*And the sound I heard was **harpists playing their harps**.*

Something with the weight of a waterfall and the force of thunder turns out, on hearing,
to be music.

Harps are in the throne room in chapter 5, held by the elders alongside the bowls of
incense.

## The song

The next verse: *they sang **as it were a new song** before the throne… and **no one could
learn that song except the hundred and forty-four thousand** who were redeemed from the
earth.*

A song with a restricted membership. Not because it is secret — because it is theirs.

## What they are called

*These are the ones who follow the Lamb wherever He goes. These were **redeemed from among
men, being firstfruits to God and to the Lamb**.*

**Firstfruits** — **aparche**, the first sheaf, which is a guarantee rather than a sample.
Paul uses it of Christ. See the note on 1 Corinthians 15:23–28.

## Where the chapter goes next

Three angels with three announcements — the everlasting gospel to every nation, the fall of
Babylon, and a warning — and then the harvest of the earth.

## The placement

Between the two beasts and the bowls of wrath, John inserts a hill with a Lamb on it and a
sound like water.

> **What is on the two foreheads.** A mark that lets you buy and sell, and a name. The
> chapters are consecutive, and the second opens with the Lamb standing.
