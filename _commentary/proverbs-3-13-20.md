Wisdom is described as a person holding two things, and the contents of each hand
are listed.

## The verb

*Blessed is the one who **finds** wisdom.*

Finding, not manufacturing. It is the verb for coming across something.

## The comparisons

*Worth more than **silver**… yields more than **gold**… more precious than
**jewels**, and **nothing you desire compares to her**.*

Three commodities, escalating, and then a clause that removes the possibility of any
further comparison.

## The two hands

*Long life is in her **right hand**, riches and honour in her **left**.*

The right hand in that world was the hand of the primary gift.

So the arrangement is: **length of days** is what she is chiefly holding out, and
**riches and honour** are in the other hand.

Both are there. The passage does not disparage the second pair — it ranks them.

## The paths

*Her ways are **ways of pleasantness**, and all her paths are **peace**.*

The Hebrew for *pleasantness* is **noam** — agreeableness, delight, beauty. And
peace is **shalom**, wholeness.

## The tree

*She is **a tree of life** to those who take hold of her, and blessed are all who
**will not let her go**.*

The first mention of that tree since Genesis 3, where the way to it was barred by a
flaming sword.

Two verbs of grip: take hold, and do not let go.

## What wisdom built

*By **wisdom** the LORD laid the earth's foundations, by **understanding** He set the
heavens in place. By His **knowledge** the deep waters burst open.*

The same three words the passage has been recommending are used of the creation.

Whatever wisdom is, the argument runs, it is the thing the universe was made with —
which is why a man who gets it is working with the grain of things.

Proverbs 8 develops it at length: *the LORD possessed me at the beginning of His way…
when He prepared the heavens, I was there.*

## What Solomon asked for

*"Give to Your servant an understanding heart to judge Your people."*

And the answer: *because you have asked this thing, and have not asked long life for
yourself, nor riches… **I have also given you what you have not asked**: both riches
and honour.* See the note on 1 Kings 3:5–14.

He asked for what was in the right hand. What was in the left came anyway.

## Where Paul locates it

*Christ, **in whom every treasure of wisdom and knowledge lies hidden**.*

Colossians 2, and the Greek is *hidden* — stored up, concealed, the way treasure is.
See the note on Colossians 2:1–3.

> **What the chapter says four verses earlier.** *Honour the LORD with all you have,
> with the first and best of everything you gather.* And four verses later, *do not
> despise the LORD's discipline.* Proverbs 3 puts firstfruits, correction and wisdom
> on the same page. See the note on Proverbs 3:9–12.
