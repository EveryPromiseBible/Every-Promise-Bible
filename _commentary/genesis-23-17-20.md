title: A field, a cave, and the full price

The first piece of the Promised Land any Hebrew ever owned is a grave, and he paid retail for
it.

## What changed hands

*So **Ephron's field at Machpelah**, near Mamre — the field and the cave in it, **and all the
trees within its borders** — passed to Abraham as his own.*

The deed is recorded like a deed: the field, the cave, the trees, the boundaries. Genesis
gives four verses to the paperwork.

**Machpelah** means *doubled* — a double cave.

## Who watched

*Witnessed by **all the Hittites who had gathered at the city gate**.*

The gate was the courthouse. He does the transaction in public, in front of the whole town, so
that it can never be undone.

Ephron had offered it to him as a gift — *I give you the field, and the cave that is in it* —
and Abraham refused and insisted on weighing out four hundred shekels of silver.

A gift can be withdrawn. A purchase with witnesses cannot.

## What he had been promised

The whole country. *All the land which you see I will give to you and your descendants
forever.* See the note on Genesis 13:14–18.

## What he owned when he died

This.

Stephen says it at his trial: *and God gave him **no inheritance in it, not even enough to set
his foot on** — but even when Abraham had no child, He promised to give it to him.*

## Why he bought it anyway

Because burying Sarah in Canaan is a statement about what he expected to happen there.

He could have taken her body back to Haran. He buys ground in the country he had been promised
and puts her in it, and then is put in it himself, and then Isaac, Rebekah, Leah and Jacob.
Six people in a cave in a land the family did not yet hold.

*These all died in faith, **not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off**…
and confessed that they were strangers and pilgrims on the earth.*

## The pattern

A field bought at full price, publicly, with the buyer refusing every discount — so that what
is in it belongs to him beyond dispute.

Jesus told it as a parable: *the kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field, which a
man found and hid; and for joy over it **he goes and sells all that he has and buys that
field**.*

He did not need the field. He wanted what was in it, and he bought the whole thing to get it.

## Where the cave is now

Hebron. Abraham, Sarah, Isaac, Rebekah, Jacob and Leah.

Rachel is the one who is not there — she died on the road to Bethlehem and Jacob buried her
where she fell. See the note on Genesis 35:16–20.

> **What he refused.** A free field. He weighed out four hundred shekels in front of the whole
> town so that the one piece of the Promised Land his family held could never be called a
> favour.
