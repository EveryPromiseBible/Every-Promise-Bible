The instruction to husbands is one line. The rest is about what Christ did.

## The command

*Husbands, **love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself
up for her**.*

The standard is not reciprocity, deserving, or a fair division of effort. It is a
man on a cross.

Everything addressed to the husband in this passage is about what he gives. Nothing
in it authorises him to require anything.

## The three purpose clauses

Each begins *so that*:

- *so that He might **make her holy**, washing her clean*
- *so that He might **present her to Himself** a church in all her glory*
- and the state she is in when presented

Every one of them is His action, and the church is the object throughout.

## The washing

*Washing her clean **with water through the word**.*

The Greek for *word* here is **rhema** — the spoken word, the thing said, rather than
**logos**.

Two pictures are usually heard in it: the bath a bride took before a wedding in that
world, and baptism.

And the cleansing agent is named. Not effort, not time, not penance — water, and the
word.

Jesus says the same to the disciples: *you are already clean **because of the word**
which I have spoken to you.*

## What she is presented as

*In all her glory, **with no stain on her and no crease and nothing else of the
kind**, but **holy and flawless**.*

The Greek is *not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing*.

**Spot** is a mark from outside. **Wrinkle** is what age does from inside. Neither is
present.

**Flawless** is **amomos** — the sacrificial word, without blemish, the term for an
animal fit for the altar.

## Who does the presenting

*He might present her **to Himself**.*

He washes her, and He is also the one she is presented to. There is no third party in
the transaction and no inspector.

## What the passage is really about

Paul says so himself a few verses later: *this is a great mystery, **but I speak
concerning Christ and the church**.*

The marriage is the illustration. He names the subject at the end.

## What Peter adds

*Husbands, **live with her thoughtfully, with real understanding**… so that nothing
gets in the way of your prayers.* See the note on 1 Peter 3:7.

Two apostles, two letters, and neither gives a husband anything to demand.
