title: "The days of Noah and Lot"

Two examples, and what is listed as the problem in each is startlingly ordinary.

## What the people were doing

*"**People were eating and drinking, marrying and giving their daughters in marriage**, right up to
the day Noah went into the ark."*

*"They were **eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building**."*

Six activities across the two examples, and not one of them is a sin. Eating, drinking, marrying,
trading, farming, building — the whole ordinary economy of being alive.

Genesis says the generation of the flood was violent and that Sodom was worse. **Jesus** does not
mention any of it.

The point He is making is not about wickedness. It is about not noticing.

*"**And they did not know until the flood came and took them all away.**"*

**Matthew** 24 supplies the clause: they did not know.

## The two who did

Noah went into the ark. Lot went out of the city.

*By faith **Noah**, being divinely warned of things not yet seen, moved with godly fear, prepared an
ark for the saving of his household.*

*And **the LORD was merciful to him**, so the men took hold of his hand… and they brought him out and
set him outside the city.*

Genesis 19, and it is worth reading: the angels have to take Lot by the hand and pull, *while he
lingered*. He is not rescued for being alert. He is rescued because somebody grabbed him.

*The Lord knows how to **deliver the godly out of temptations**.*

**Peter** cites both examples in the same paragraph — Noah and Lot — and draws that conclusion from
them.

## The day itself

*"**On the day Noah went into the ark**"… "**on the day Lot went out of Sodom**."*

Both sentences date the disaster to a departure, and in Noah's case Genesis records who closed the
door: *and the LORD shut him in.*

## What is being warned about

*"**Remember Lot's wife.**"*

The next verse but one, and it is the shortest sentence in the chapter.

*"For as the lightning that flashes out of one part under heaven shines to the other part under
heaven, **so also the Son of Man will be in His day**."*

> **What is on the list.** Eating, drinking, marrying, buying, selling, planting, building. Not one
> of them is a sin, and Genesis had plenty of other material to choose from. What He names is not
> wickedness — it is not noticing.
