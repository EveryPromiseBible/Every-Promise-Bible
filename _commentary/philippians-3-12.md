---
key: Philippians 3:12
title: Taken hold of
updated: 2026-08-07
---

He reaches for something, and the sentence ends by saying it already has him.

## The denial

*Not that **I have already reached it**, or **been brought to completion**.*

The Greek is **ouch hoti ēdē elabon ē ēdē teteleiōmai**.

**Teleioō** — to be perfected, completed, brought to the end.

## Who is speaking

A man who has just listed everything he gave up, and called it all rubbish. See the note on
Philippians 3:7–8.

The disclaimer is not modesty. Something in Philippi was claiming arrival.

## What he wants

*The knowledge of Christ, *and* **that I may know Him and the power of His resurrection, and the
fellowship of His sufferings**.* See the note on Philippians 3:9–11.

The Greek is **gnōnai auton** — an infinitive of personal acquaintance.

## The pursuit

*But **I keep pressing on**.*

The Greek is **diōkō** — I pursue, I chase. The verb used of hunting and of persecuting.

The same word he used of himself in the previous paragraph: *concerning zeal, **persecuting** the
church*.

The energy did not change. Its object did.

## The aim

*Hoping to **take hold of** the very thing.*

The Greek is **katalabō** — to seize, to lay hold of, to overtake.

## The turn

*For which **Christ Jesus took hold of me**.*

The Greek is **kateēmphthēn hupo Christou Iēsou** — I was laid hold of by Christ Jesus.

The same verb, in the passive, with the subject and object swapped.

## What the sentence does

Starts with him chasing and ends with him already caught.

## When he was caught

On the Damascus road, by a light and a voice, mid-journey, on other business. See the note on
Acts 9:3–6.

He was not looking.

## What he says next

*Brethren, I do not count myself to have apprehended; but one thing I do, **forgetting those things
which are behind and reaching forward to those things which are ahead**.* See the note on
Philippians 3:13–14.

The Greek is **epekteinomenos** — stretching out toward, the word for a runner's body over the
line.

## What is behind

Both his record of loss and his record of achievement.

The list in this chapter includes both, and the same word covers them.

## The prize

*I press toward the goal for **the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus**.*

The Greek is **to brabeion tēs anō klēseōs**.

**Brabeion** — the award handed down at the games by the **brabeus**, the judge.

## The concession about others

*Therefore let us, **as many as are mature**, have this mind.*

The Greek is **teleioi** — the adjective from the verb he just denied of himself.

He says he is not **teteleiōmai** and then addresses the **teleioi**. The wordplay is deliberate, and
the point of it is that maturity consists in knowing you have not arrived.

## The last clause

*And if in anything you think otherwise, **God will reveal even this to you**.*

He does not argue the point further.

> **The reversal.** The sentence begins with him laying hold and ends with him having been laid hold
> of — the same verb, the voice flipped.
