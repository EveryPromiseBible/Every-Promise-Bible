The letter ends with Paul taking the pen from his secretary — *see what large letters I have written
with my own hand* — and writing the summary himself.

## Where boasting is allowed

*May I never boast in anything **except the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ**.*

**Kauchasthai** is Paul's word for glorying, and this whole letter has been about men who wanted
something to boast in. The circumcision party's motive is named two verses earlier: *that they may
boast in your flesh.*

So Paul does not tell them to stop boasting. He tells them where the only safe place to do it is —
the one event in the universe to which he contributed nothing but the need.

## A cross that cuts both ways

*Through which **the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world**.*

Two crucifixions in one clause, and the cross stands between them. The world is dead to Paul: it
can no longer offer him anything he wants. Paul is dead to the world: he can no longer offer it
anything it wants.

Either half alone would be a half-freedom. A man who has lost his appetite for the world's approval
but still wants its attention is not free.

## The category that stopped existing

*For in Christ Jesus **neither circumcision counts for anything, nor uncircumcision**, but a new
creation.*

The argument that split the churches of Galatia, and Paul settles it by abolishing both sides. The
question was never which answer was right. It was that the category itself has ceased to carry
weight.

**Kaine ktisis** — a new creation. The same phrase he uses to the Corinthians: *if anyone is in
Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away.* Not a repaired man. A new one.

## The rule, and the peace

*And to all who **keep in step with this rule**, peace be upon them, and mercy.*

**Kanon** — the measuring rod, the straight edge. In a letter about a false standard, Paul supplies
the true one, and it is the new creation rather than any mark on a body.

And read the order of the blessing: **peace** first, then **mercy**. Peace is the thing already
made — *having made peace through the blood of His cross* — and mercy is what keeps arriving on top
of it, morning by morning, for people who are already at peace.

> **Boast in what He did.** Every other ground of boasting requires maintenance, and any of them can
> be taken from you. This one cannot, because it was finished before you heard of it and none of it
> was yours. *God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ.*
