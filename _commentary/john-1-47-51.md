A man is recognised before he arrives, and the promise he gets is a staircase.

## What Jesus says about him

*"Here is **a true son of Israel, a man with nothing false in him**."*

**En ho dolos ouk estin** — in whom is no guile.

**Dolos** is the word used of Jacob taking the blessing: *your brother came with deceit*. The man is
called an Israelite with none of Jacob in him, and the promise two verses later is Jacob's dream.

## The question

*"**How do You know anything about me?**"*

## The answer

*"**Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.**"*

Nothing is said about what he was doing there. Rabbinic custom had the fig tree as the place a man
sat to study, and Micah and Zechariah both use *under his vine and under his fig tree* for peace.

Whatever it was, it is enough. Nathanael has just said *can anything good come out of Nazareth?* and
he answers a single sentence with a confession:

*"**Rabbi, You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!**"*

## What He promises

*"**You will see greater things than these.**"*

*"Most assuredly, I say to you, hereafter you shall see **heaven open, and the angels of God
ascending and descending upon the Son of Man**."*

## Jacob's ladder

*Then he dreamed, and behold, **a ladder was set up on the earth, and its top reached to heaven; and
there the angels of God were ascending and descending on it***… and Jacob woke and said *surely the
LORD is in this place, and I did not know it.*

Genesis 28, and **Jesus** quotes the traffic exactly — up first, then down — and puts Himself where
the ladder was.

Nathanael is told he will see the connection between earth and heaven, and that it is a Person
rather than a structure.

*"**I am the way**, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me."*

*For there is one God and **one Mediator between God and men, the Man Christ Jesus**.*

## The change of pronoun

*"**You** will see greater things"* — singular, to Nathanael. And then *"**you** shall see heaven
open"* — plural, to everybody in earshot.

The promise widens mid-sentence.

> **What the man with no guile is given.** Jacob's dream, with the ladder replaced. Genesis has the
> angels going up and down a staircase; John has them going up and down on the Son of Man.
