---
key: Revelation 21:3–4
title: God moves in
updated: 2026-08-07
---

The direction of the whole book reverses here. Nobody goes up.

## The voice

*And I heard **a voice out of heaven, loud**.*

The Greek is **phōnēs megalēs ek tou thronou** in most manuscripts — a great voice from the throne.

## The announcement

*Look — **God has pitched His tent among people**.*

The Greek is **hē skēnē tou Theou meta tōn anthrōpōn** — the tent of God with men.

## The word

**Skēnē** — a tent, a tabernacle.

The Septuagint's word for the tabernacle in the wilderness, the portable structure God gave
instructions for so that *I may dwell among them*.

## The other place John uses it

*And the Word became flesh and **dwelt among us**.*

The Greek is **eskēnōsen** — tented, pitched His tent. See the note on John 1:14–18.

The Gospel opens with the verb and the Apocalypse closes with the noun.

## The direction

*Out of heaven.*

The city descends. Nothing in the passage climbs.

## The formula

*They will be **His people**, and **God Himself will be there with them**, and **He will be their God**.*

The covenant formula, running through the prophets: *I will be their God, and they shall be My
people*.

Repeated to Abraham, at Sinai, through Jeremiah and Ezekiel. It arrives here as a completed
statement.

## The plural

Some manuscripts read **laoi** — peoples.

If so, the promise made to one nation is stated at the end over many.

## The tears

*God will **dry the tears on every face**.*

The Greek is **exaleipsei pan dakruon** — He will wipe away every tear.

Isaiah: *the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from all faces*. See the note on Isaiah 25:8.

## Who does it

Not an angel.

The sentence has God performing an act that requires being within reach of the face.

## The four abolished things

*Death will be over. So will **grief, and crying, and pain** — all of it.*

The Greek is **ho thanatos ouk estai eti** — death shall be no more.

**Penthos** — mourning. **Kraugē** — outcry, the sound made at a death. **Ponos** — toil, pain.

## The order

Death first, and then the three things death produces.

## The reason

*The things that used to be are gone.*

The Greek is **ta prōta apēlthan** — the first things went away.

## What is said next

*"Behold, **I make all things new**." And He said to me, "Write, for these words are true and
faithful."* See the note on Revelation 21:5–6.

**Kaina** — new in kind. Not a replacement set; a renewed one.

## What is not in the city

*But **I saw no temple in it**, for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple.* See the note
on Revelation 21:22–23.

The tent that was the whole point of the tabernacle turns out not to need a building.

## The curse

*And there shall be **no more curse**.* See the note on Revelation 22:3–4.

The word from Genesis 3, cancelled on the last page.

## The last of the four

*They shall see His face.*

What Job asked for. See the note on Job 19:25–27.

> **The direction.** Every religion climbs. Here the city comes down, and the sentence is that God
> has pitched His tent where the people already are.
