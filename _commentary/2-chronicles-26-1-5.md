Fifty-two years on the throne, and the chronicler puts the whole reign under one
condition.

## Sixteen years old

He is a boy when they make him king. His father Amaziah had been murdered by
conspirators, and the people take the son and put him on the throne.

Nothing in his position was earned.

## The man beside him

*He set himself to seek God **in the days of Zechariah, who taught him to fear
God**.*

Not the prophet who wrote the book — an otherwise unknown man, named here only,
and named because Uzziah's seeking is tied to him.

A young king with someone teaching him. The chronicler thought that worth
recording.

## The condition

*And **as long as he sought the LORD**, God gave him success.*

The Hebrew for *gave him success* is to make prosper, to cause to advance — the
same verb used of Joseph in Potiphar's house.

The chapter goes on to list what that looked like: victories over the
Philistines, tribute from the Ammonites, towers built in the desert, cisterns dug,
vineyards in the hills, an army of over three hundred thousand, and engines of
war on the walls of Jerusalem.

*And his fame spread far, for he was marvellously helped, till he was strong.*

## "Till he was strong"

That clause is the pivot of the whole chapter, and it is doing two jobs. It marks
how far the help went — and it marks where the trouble starts.

The next verse: *but when he had grown strong, his heart became proud, and it led
to his ruin.* See the note on 2 Chronicles 26:16–18.

## What lifted him

Not the east, not the west, not the desert.

*It is God who judges. He brings one down and lifts another up.* The psalm says
promotion has one source, and this chapter is a fifty-two-year illustration of
it — including the end. See the note on Psalms 75:6–8.

> **Where the chapter's phrase lands elsewhere in Scripture.** *In the year that
> King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord.* Isaiah dates his vision of the throne by the
> death of this man — the king who went into the sanctuary he had no business in,
> and the prophet who saw the true throne the year that king was buried.
