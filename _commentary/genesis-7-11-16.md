The last line of this passage is five words long in Hebrew and it is the point of
the whole chapter.

## The date

*In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, on **the seventeenth day of the second
month**.*

Genesis dates it to the day. Whatever else this account is, it is written as a
record rather than a legend.

## Where the water came from

*The **great deep burst open** and the **floodgates of the heavens were flung
wide**.*

Both directions at once — up from below and down from above. The Hebrew for the
first is that the fountains of the great deep were split open.

The rain is mentioned second, and it is the lesser half.

## Who went in

*Noah, his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth, his wife, and his three sons' wives.*

Eight. And every kind of creature *coming to Noah two by two* — the animals come to
him; he does not go after them.

## The sentence

*Then **the LORD shut him in** — sealed the door with His own hand, safe inside.*

The Hebrew is *and the LORD shut behind him*. Noah did not close the door.

He built the ark, gathered the food, loaded the animals, and walked in. The last
act — the one that made it watertight — was done from outside by somebody else.

## Which name is used

The text switches. Through the building instructions it is **Elohim**, God — the
name of power and creation.

Here it is **YHWH**, the LORD, the covenant name given at the burning bush. The
shutting of the door is done under the personal name.

## What a shut door means

Nobody inside could open it, and nobody outside could either.

The security of everyone in that boat had nothing to do with how tightly they were
holding on. The door was sealed by the One who told them to come in.

Jesus uses the same picture: *neither shall anyone snatch them out of My hand.* See
the note on John 10:27–30.

## What the word for the boat is

**Tebah**. It appears in only one other place in Scripture — the basket of papyrus,
sealed with tar and pitch, that Moses' mother laid among the reeds. See the note on
Exodus 2:1–10.

Two vessels, both waterproofed, both carrying a life through water that killed
everything else.

## What the ark had no way of doing

There is no rudder in the specification, no sail, no oar. Once the door was shut,
nobody aboard could influence where it went.

For a hundred and fifty days they were carried.

> **The word Jesus used about that week.** *They knew nothing about it **until the
> flood came** and swept them all away.* Matthew 24 — eating, drinking, marrying,
> right up to the day Noah entered the ark. The judgement is not what He describes
> as the surprise. The ordinariness is.
