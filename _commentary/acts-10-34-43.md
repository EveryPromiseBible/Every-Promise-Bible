Peter's sermon in a Roman officer's house, and the summary of Jesus' ministry in
it is the shortest in the New Testament.

## How it opens

*I see now, truly, that God shows no favouritism.*

Peter says *I see now* — he had not seen it before that week. It took a vision on
a roof, three men at the gate, and a walk to Caesarea.

The Greek is vivid: God is *no taker of faces*, no respecter of appearances.

## The summary

*How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, and how
He went about **doing good** and **healing everyone under the oppression of the
devil**, because God was with Him.*

One sentence for three years. Two verbs: doing good, and healing.

Peter is speaking to people who had heard the reports — *you know what has
happened all through Judea.* He does not need to persuade them it occurred.

## What the healing is called

*Everyone under the oppression of the devil.* The word means being tyrannised,
held down by force.

Peter characterises sickness there as an oppression being lifted, and healing as
release rather than reward.

## "Because God was with Him"

The reason given for the whole ministry. Peter puts the anointing, the power, and
the presence together and hangs everything on it.

## Where it ends

*Every one of the prophets bears witness to Him, that through His name **everyone
who believes** in Him receives forgiveness of sins.*

That is the last sentence before the Spirit interrupts him. The word *everyone*
is what he is saying when it happens — in a Gentile house, to people he would not
have entered a room with a week earlier. See the note on Acts 10:44–48.
