A man wakes up on a rock and discovers where he had been sleeping.

## What he says

*"**Surely the LORD is in this place — and I never knew it.**"*

Both halves are true and the second is the point.

It had been true all night. He had chosen the spot because the sun set, not because
it was anything. See the note on Genesis 28:10–15.

## The awe

*A **holy awe** came over him.*

The Hebrew is that he was afraid — the fear that comes with realising what you have
been standing next to.

## What he calls it

*"**How full of wonder this place is.** This is nothing less than **the house of
God**. This is **the very gate of heaven**."*

Two names for it, and neither is what it looked like. It was open ground with stones
on it.

**House of God** — a building, on a site with no building. **Gate of heaven** — a
doorway, in a field.

## The stone

*He took **the stone he had rested his head on**, and he set it up as a pillar, and he
**poured oil over the top of it**.*

The pillow becomes a pillar.

The same stone, moved from horizontal to vertical, and marked with oil.

It is the first anointing with oil in Scripture, and it is performed by a man on the
run, on a rock, alone.

## Where the oil came from

Genesis does not say, and he had left home in a hurry with nothing recorded in his
hands. Oil was ordinary in that world — carried for lamps, for cooking, for skin.

He uses what he had on a stone he had slept on.

## The name

*He named that place **Bethel** — the house of God — though the town had **once been
called Luz**.*

**Luz** means almond, or a turning aside.

The town had a name already. He renames it after what he had not known was there.

## What he does next

*Then Jacob made a vow: "If God will be with me, and keep me in this way… and give me
bread to eat and clothing to put on, so that I come back to my father's house in
peace, **then the LORD shall be my God**."*

Given the whole covenant unconditionally in his sleep, he wakes up and negotiates.

That is Jacob throughout, and Genesis records it without comment.

## Where he ends up

Twenty years later, God tells him: *arise, go up to Bethel and dwell there, and make
an altar there to God, who appeared to you when you fled.*

He goes back with two wives, eleven sons, a limp and a new name, to the rock he slept
on when he had nothing.

> **What Jesus does with the vision.** *"You will see heaven standing wide open, and
> the angels of God going up and coming down **on the Son of Man**."* Said to
> Nathanael, in John 1. Jacob called the place the gate of heaven. Jesus says He is
> the stairway. See the note on John 1:47–51.
