One man missed the meeting, and the whole church remembers him for it.

## The absence

*But Thomas, one of the Twelve… **was not with them when Jesus came**.*

John states it plainly and does not say why.

The others were behind locked doors *for fear of the Jews*. Thomas was somewhere else.

## The name

*The one called **the Twin**.*

The Greek is **Didumos**, translating the Hebrew **Te'om** — twin.

John gives the translation three times. His twin is never mentioned.

## What he had said before

*Then Thomas said to his fellow disciples, "**Let us also go, that we may die with Him**."*

Said when Jesus announced He was going back to Judea, where they had just tried to stone Him. See
the note on John 11:11–16.

The man remembered for doubt is the one who volunteered to die.

## And again

*"**Lord, we do not know where You are going, and how can we know the way?**"*

Which produced *I am the way, the truth, and the life*. See the note on
John 14:5–7.

Both of his recorded questions get answers the church has never stopped using.

## The report

*So the other disciples **kept telling him**, "**We have seen the Lord**."*

The Greek is **elegon** — imperfect. They kept saying it.

Repeatedly, over a week.

## The refusal

*"**Unless I see the mark of the nails in His hands, and put my finger where the nails went, and put
my hand into His side, I will not believe it**."*

The Greek is **ou me pisteuso** — a double negative. The strongest refusal Greek permits.

## What he asks for

Three things: to see the nail marks, to put a finger in them, and to put a hand in the side.

## What is telling about the list

He knows about the spear.

The soldier's thrust happened at the cross, and John records it: *one of the soldiers pierced His
side with a spear*. See the note on John 19:31–34.

Thomas either saw it or was told. He names the wound nobody would invent.

## What he is asking for

The same evidence the others already had.

*When He had said this, **He showed them His hands and His side**. Then the disciples were glad when
they saw the Lord.*

They believed after seeing. He asks for what they were given.

## The week

*And after eight days His disciples were again inside, and **Thomas with them**.* See the note on
John 20:26–29.

He does not leave. He stays with people whose testimony he has rejected, for a week.

## What happens

*Then He said to Thomas, "**Reach your finger here, and look at My hands; and reach your hand here,
and put it into My side**."*

The exact three items, in the order he named them, offered by somebody who was not in the room
when he said it.

## What he does not do

Touch.

The text does not record him accepting the offer. It records what he says.

## What he says

*"**My Lord and my God!**"*

The Greek is **ho Kurios mou kai ho Theos mou**.

The highest confession in the Gospel, and it comes from the last man to make one.

> **The three items.** He names the nail marks and the spear wound, and a week later they are
> offered back to him in the same order by somebody who was not there when he listed them.
