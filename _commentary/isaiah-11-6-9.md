Six pairs of animals that should not be together, and a toddler in charge of all of them.

## The pairs

Wolf and lamb. Leopard and young goat. Calf and lion. Cow and bear. Nursing child and cobra.
Weaned child and viper.

In every pair one eats the other. Isaiah does not remove the predators — he lists them by name
and puts them down beside the prey.

*And **the lion will eat straw like the ox**.*

The digestive system, not just the temperament.

## Who is leading

*And **a little child will lead them**.*

The Hebrew is **na'ar qaton** — a small boy. The word for the person driving the herd is the word
for someone too young to be trusted with one.

*For **unto us a Child is born**, unto us a Son is given; and the government will be upon His
shoulder.*

*"Assuredly, I say to you, unless you are converted and **become as little children**, you will by
no means enter the kingdom of heaven."*

## Where the chapter starts

*There shall come forth **a Rod from the stem of Jesse**, and a Branch shall grow out of his
roots.*

A stump, not a tree. The dynasty is already cut down when the shoot appears.

*And again, Isaiah says: "**There shall be a root of Jesse; and He who shall rise to reign over
the Gentiles, in Him the Gentiles shall hope.**"*

**Paul** quotes Isaiah 11:10 at the end of **Romans** 15 and applies the whole chapter to
**Christ**.

## The lion and the lamb

*"Do not weep. Behold, **the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has prevailed**"…
And I looked, and behold, in the midst of the throne… stood **a Lamb as though it had been
slain**.*

**John** is told to look at a Lion and turns round to see a Lamb. It is the same figure, and it
is the reason the wolf can lie down with the lamb in Isaiah 11 — the Lion in the passage became
the prey.

*For **He Himself is our peace**, who has made both one, and **has broken down the middle wall of
separation**.*

**Paul** applies the same logic to two nations who had spent centuries eating each other.

## The reason given

*For **the earth will be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea**.*

Not because the animals were retrained. Isaiah gives one cause, and it is knowledge — saturating,
like water, with no dry patch left in it.

*"For **all shall know Me, from the least of them to the greatest of them**," says the LORD. "For
I will forgive their iniquity, and their sin I will remember no more."*

**Hebrews** 8 quotes Jeremiah's version, and it ends on forgiveness. The knowledge that fills the
earth in Isaiah 11 is, in the new covenant, knowledge of somebody who has stopped counting.

> **What is not removed.** The wolf, the leopard, the lion, the bear, the cobra. Isaiah names
> every predator and leaves them all in the field — and the one thing that changes is what the
> earth is full of.
