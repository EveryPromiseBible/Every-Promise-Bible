One line, about a coastline, and it was said two hundred years before the land was
divided.

## The blessing

*"Zebulun will **live by the seashore**. He will be **a harbour for ships**, and
his border will reach toward Sidon."*

Jacob is in Egypt, dying, with twelve sons and no territory anywhere. Nobody had
surveyed anything.

Two centuries later Joshua divides the land by lot, and Zebulun's portion runs up
into the north country toward the coast.

## The name

**Zebulun** means dwelling. Leah named him: *now my husband will dwell with me.*

## Where He lived

*He left Nazareth and went to live in **Capernaum**, a town beside the sea in **the
territory of Zebulun and Naphtali**.*

Matthew says it deliberately, and then quotes Isaiah over it:

*Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali… **the people sitting in darkness have seen a
great light**, and on those living in the land where death casts its shadow, light
has dawned.*

Nazareth is in Zebulun. Capernaum is on the border of Zebulun and Naphtali. He grew
up in one and made His home in the other, and both are in the tribe whose name
means dwelling. See the note on Matthew 4:12–17.

## The harbour

Zebulun is *a harbour for ships*, and the lake it sits on is where the boats were.

Simon's boat, pushed out a little from the land, used as a pulpit and then filled
until it settled low in the water. The storm crossing. The walking on the sea.
Levi's tax booth at the harbour of Capernaum.

## And after the resurrection

*Simon Peter said, "I am going fishing"… and all that night they caught nothing at
all.*

Back on the same water, in the same trade, after everything. And He is on the beach
at daybreak with a charcoal fire, bread and fish already on it, asking whether they
have any food.

He fills their net, feeds them breakfast, and then restores Peter three times over
a charcoal fire — the only other charcoal fire in John's Gospel is the one Peter
warmed himself at while he denied Him. See the note on John 21:1–3.

Livelihood, a meal, and the thing Peter could not say. All three, in the tribe
called dwelling.

> **What Moses added over Zebulun.** *Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out… they
> shall call the peoples to the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of
> righteousness, for they shall partake of the abundance of the seas.* A tribe on
> the trade routes, calling the nations in.
