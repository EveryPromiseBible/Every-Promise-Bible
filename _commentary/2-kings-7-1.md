key: 2 Kings 7:1
title: About this time tomorrow
updated: 2026-08-09
---
A city is starving under siege, and the prophet gives a price list for the next morning.

## What the siege had done

*There was a great famine in Samaria… **a donkey's head was sold for eighty shekels of
silver**.*

And worse, in the verses just before this: two women had agreed to eat their own children, and
the king tore his clothes when he heard it and was wearing sackcloth underneath.

## What Elisha says

*"About this time tomorrow, at the gate of Samaria, **a seah of the finest flour will sell for
a shekel, and two seahs of barley for a shekel**."*

Not *God will help*. A commodity price, a location, and a deadline.

It is normal market rate. He is promising that by tomorrow the famine will not merely be
lifted — the price will be ordinary.

## Why tomorrow matters

*"Therefore **do not worry about tomorrow**, for tomorrow will worry about its own things."*

The city has no food tonight and no prospect of any. What is promised is that a day is coming
which the present has no way of producing.

## Who does not believe it

*So an officer on whose hand the king leaned answered the man of God and said, "**Look, if the
LORD would make windows in heaven, could this thing be?**"*

And Elisha: *"In fact, you shall see it with your eyes, but you shall not eat of it."*

He is trampled in the gate the next day by the crowd going out for the food.

## How it arrives

Four lepers, sitting outside the gate because they are not allowed in, reasoning that they
will die either way and might as well surrender to the Arameans. See the note on
2 Kings 7:3–4.

They walk into an empty camp. The army had heard a sound in the night and fled, leaving
everything.

## What the city is fed by

Men who were not permitted inside it.

*"Why are we sitting here until we die?"… Then they said to one another, "**We are not doing
right. This day is a day of good news, and we remain silent**."*

The Hebrew is **yom besorah** — a day of good tidings. The Greek Old Testament renders that
root **euangelia**.

Four outcasts find a supply nobody in the city had earned, and their conscience will not let
them keep quiet about it.

*How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him who **brings good news**.*

> **What was promised.** Not relief. A price — the ordinary market rate for flour, at a named
> gate, by a named hour, spoken over a city where people were eating their children.
