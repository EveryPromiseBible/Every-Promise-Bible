The third of the offerings, and the only one shared three ways.

## The name

*A **peace offering** — a gift that celebrates peace with God.*

The Hebrew is **shelamim**, plural, from **shalom**.

Not peace as the absence of hostility. **Shalom** is wholeness, completeness, nothing
missing. The plural form makes it emphatic.

The older name for it is the *sacrifice of peace offerings*, and Leviticus 7 divides it into
three occasions: thanksgiving, a vow, and simple freewill.

## What is unusual about it

*Whether **male or female**.*

The burnt offering had to be male and unblemished. See the note on Leviticus 1:3–9.

This one may be either. It is the only sacrifice in the list where the sex of the animal is
left open.

## The condition kept

*Let it be **without flaw** before the LORD.*

The Hebrew is **tamim**. The one requirement that is never relaxed anywhere in Leviticus.

## The hand

*Lay your hand on its head.*

The same **samakh** — pressing down with weight. Identification, before anything is killed.

## The place

*It is put to death **at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting**, and Aaron's sons **sprinkle
its blood** against the sides of the altar.*

Same door, same blood on the same sides. The procedure up to this point is identical to the
burnt offering.

## What goes on the fire

*The **fat that covers the inner parts**, both **kidneys with the fat around them**, and the
**covering of the liver**.*

The Hebrew for fat is **chelev**. In Hebrew idiom it stands for the best of a thing — *the
fat of the land*, *the finest of the wheat* is literally *the fat of the wheat*.

Only the interior fat goes up. Nothing on the outside of the animal.

The rule is stated flatly a few verses later: *all the fat is the LORD's*, and *you shall eat
neither fat nor blood*.

## Where it is placed

*On the altar, **on top of the burnt offering that is on the wood above the fire**.*

The peace offering is laid on the burnt offering.

The order is not negotiable, and it is the same order as the days: the burnt offering
underneath, everything else stacked on it.

## The result

*A gift by fire, **a fragrance that pleases the LORD**.*

The same phrase as the burnt offering. **Reach nichoach** — a restful smell.

## Who eats it

God gets the fat.

The priest gets the breast and the right thigh — *the wave breast and the heave shoulder*.

And the man who brought it eats the rest, with his household, in front of the tabernacle.
Leviticus 7 says a thanksgiving peace offering must be eaten the same day, and nothing left
till morning.

Which means it was a meal, for a family, that had to be finished, so people were invited.

The only offering where the worshipper eats any of it.

## The three parties at the table

God, the priest, and the man. All three receive from the same animal.

That is what makes it the peace offering, and the reason it is the one with a feast attached.

## Where the peace comes from

*Therefore, having been **justified by faith, we have peace with God** through our Lord Jesus
Christ.* See the note on Romans 5:1–2.

And *having made peace through the blood of His cross*.

Righteousness first, then peace. The same order as Melchizedek's two titles. See the note on
Hebrews 7:1–2.

> **The three-way split.** God takes the fat, the priest takes the breast and thigh, and the
> man who brought it eats the rest — the only sacrifice the worshipper gets a meal out of.
