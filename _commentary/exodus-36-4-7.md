The only time in the Bible that people have to be told to stop giving.

## The interruption

*Finally **all the skilled workers doing the work of the sanctuary set down their tools and came to
Moses**.*

The Hebrew is **vayyavo'u kol-hachakhamim** — all the wise ones came.

They stop work. Every one of them, at once, to raise the same point.

## The report

*"**The people are bringing far more than we need** to do the work the LORD commanded."*

The Hebrew is **marbim ha'am lehavi middei ha'avodah** — the people multiply to bring, beyond
the requirement of the work.

## Who is complaining

The builders. Not the treasurer, not Moses, not a committee.

The people using the material are the ones who say there is too much of it.

## The order

*So Moses **sent word all through the camp**: "**No man or woman is to make anything more as an
offering for the sanctuary**."*

The Hebrew is **vayya'aviru qol bammachaneh** — they caused a voice to pass through the camp.

A proclamation, broadcast to everybody.

## What it forbids

Making anything more.

Which tells you what a lot of the giving was. Not surplus wealth handed over. Work done — spinning,
weaving, tanning. See the note on Exodus 35:25–29.

The instruction has to stop production, not just donation.

## The restraint

*And so **the people were held back from giving**.*

The Hebrew is **vayyikkale ha'am mehavi** — the people were restrained from bringing.

## Why

*Because **what was already in hand was more than enough** to do all the work, **with plenty to
spare**.*

The Hebrew is **dayyam… vehoter** — sufficient for them, and over.

## Where this material came from

Ex-slaves in a desert, four hundred years unpaid, carrying out what the Egyptians had handed them
on the night they left.

*Thus they plundered the Egyptians.*

Everything on the tabernacle inventory came out of Egypt in the hands of people who owned nothing
the week before.

## What the same gold had done

*And he… made a moulded calf.* See the note on Exodus 32:1–4.

Chapter 32 and chapter 36 are the same category of material, from the same ears, going two
directions.

The camp that had to be stopped from giving is the camp that had melted its jewellery into a
calf.

## The accounting afterwards

Exodus 38 lists it: twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and thirty shekels of gold, a hundred
talents of silver, seventy talents of bronze.

*This is the inventory of the tabernacle… **as counted according to the commandment of Moses**.*

Moses had it audited.

## What was built

*Then the tabernacle was erected… **and the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory
of the LORD filled the tabernacle***.

*And Moses was not able to enter the tabernacle of meeting, because the cloud rested above it.*

The man who supervised the whole build cannot get in when it is finished.

## What Paul says about giving

*So let each one give as he purposes in his heart, not grudgingly or of necessity; for **God loves a
cheerful giver**.* See the note on 2 Corinthians 9:6–7.

> **What had to be stopped.** Not donations of surplus — *making anything more*. The order had to
> halt production, because most of what was coming in was work.
