key: Ruth 3:10–13
title: There is a nearer kinsman
updated: 2026-08-09
---
He agrees to everything and then names the one obstacle, and the obstacle is a man with a
better claim.

## What he calls it

*"**This kindness of yours is greater even than the kindness you showed before.**"*

The Hebrew is **chesed** both times — covenant loyalty, the word used of God all through
Scripture. He uses it of a Moabite widow, twice in a sentence, at midnight.

The earlier kindness was staying with Naomi. This one is asking a redeemer rather than a
younger man.

## The first thing he says

*"**Don't be afraid.** I'll do everything you ask."*

Before the complication, before the legal position, before anything about the other man.

## What the town already thinks

*"**All the people of my town know you are a woman of noble character.**"*

The Hebrew is **eshet chayil** — a woman of valour, of strength.

It is the phrase that opens the poem at the end of Proverbs 31, and in the Hebrew ordering of
the Bible the book of Ruth stands immediately after Proverbs. The question *who can find a
virtuous woman?* is answered on the next page by a Moabite gleaning in somebody else's field.

## The complication

*"Yes, it's true I am a redeemer for your family — **but there is another man who is a closer
relative than I am**."*

There is a nearer kinsman with the prior right, and Boaz will not go round him.

## What the nearer kinsman does

He is willing to buy the field. Then he is told the widow comes with it, and he withdraws:
*"I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I ruin my own inheritance."*

He had the closer claim and could not carry it through. The one who could was not first in
line and had to wait for the other to fail.

Paul: *for **what the law could not do** in that it was weak through the flesh, God did by
sending **His own Son** in the likeness of sinful flesh.*

## The oath

*"**As surely as the LORD lives, I will redeem you myself.**"*

He swears it in the dark, with nobody present, to a woman with no standing to enforce
anything.

## The last two words

*"**Lie down until morning.**"*

He has just sworn to settle it. Her instruction is to sleep. See the note on
Ruth 3:16–18.

> **The order.** *Don't be afraid — I'll do everything you ask* comes first. The legal
> difficulty comes second, and it is his problem, not hers.
