The only recorded episode between infancy and the Jordan, and the first recorded
words of Jesus.

## Where they found Him

*In the temple, sitting right among the teachers, listening to them and asking
them questions.*

That is what a student did — the rabbinic method was question and answer, and a
learner sat among the teachers rather than in front of them.

Luke is careful about the posture. He is not lecturing; He is sitting in the
circle, listening first, then asking.

## What astonished them

*Everyone who heard Him was astonished at how much He understood and at the
answers He gave.*

Both are named: what He grasped, and what He said back. A twelve-year-old holding
His end of a discussion with the temple teachers for three days.

## Mary's question and His answer

*"Child, why have You treated us like this? Your father and I have been searching
everywhere for You, sick with worry."*

*"Why were you searching for Me? Did you not know that I had to be taken up with
My Father's business?"*

She says *your father*. He answers *My Father*, and the contrast is unmistakable
in the Greek. It is the first thing He is recorded as saying, and it distinguishes
between the two.

The phrase can be rendered *in My Father's house* or *about My Father's business*
— the Greek is elliptical, and both are defensible.

## The line that follows

*But they did not understand what He was telling them.*

Luke says it plainly about the two people who knew most about who He was — who
had both had angelic visits. Neither the annunciation nor the shepherds nor
Simeon had prepared them for this sentence.

> **What He does next.** He goes home to Nazareth and lives under their care for
> eighteen more years. The boy who has just said He must be about His Father's
> business spends the next two decades in a carpenter's household. See the note
> on Luke 2:51–52.
