The third day, and it happens twice.

## The gathering

*"**Let the waters be gathered into one place, and let dry ground appear**." And it was so.*

The Hebrew for *dry ground* is **yabbashah**.

The same word for what Israel walks across at the Red Sea, and again at the Jordan. See the
note on Joshua 4:19–24.

Both crossings are dry land appearing out of gathered water, and both are described in
Genesis 1's vocabulary.

## The naming

*He named the dry ground **"land,"** and the gathered waters He named **"seas."***

Third and fourth of the namings. Day and night, heaven, and now land and seas.

After this God stops naming things, and in chapter 2 Adam starts.

## The verdict

*And God saw that it was good.*

Said twice on this day — once after the land appears and once after it turns green.

The third day is the only one that gets the verdict twice.

## The second command

*"Now let the land **grow green**," He said — **and it did**.*

The Hebrew is *let the earth bring forth grass* — **tadshe ha-arets deshe**, a verb and noun
from the same root.

The land is told to produce, and it produces. The command and the compliance are one clause
apart.

## The three kinds

*Grass, and **seed-bearing plants**, and **trees heavy with fruit**.*

Ascending: ground cover, plants that seed, trees whose fruit contains the seed.

## The clause

*Each carrying **its own seed to make more of its kind**.*

The Hebrew is *whose seed is in itself, according to its kind* — **zera** and **min**.

**After its kind** is the phrase of the whole chapter. It occurs ten times across the third,
fifth and sixth days.

The reproductive capacity is built into the thing on the day it is made. Nothing has to be
resupplied.

## What is not there yet

The sun.

Light was made on day one; the sun, moon and stars arrive on day four. Vegetation exists for
a day without the star it depends on.

## The evening and the morning

*Evening came, then morning — the third day.*

Evening first, in every one of the six. Which is why the Jewish day still starts at sunset.

## The third day

Dry land out of water, and then life coming out of the ground.

*Now after two days He will revive us; **on the third day He will raise us up**, that we may
live in His sight.* Hosea.

*He was buried, and He rose again the third day according to the Scriptures.*

## Where the seed image goes

*Unless a grain of wheat falls into the ground and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it
produces much grain.* See the note on John 12:23–26.

And *the seed is the word of God*. See the note on Matthew 13:18–23.

And Peter: *born again, not of corruptible seed but incorruptible*. See the note on
1 Peter 1:23.

Every one of them is drawing on the clause in Genesis: the seed is inside the thing already.

## The first promise

*I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her Seed.*

The word from day three becomes the word of the first promise after the fall.

> **What was built in on the third day.** Not a supply of seed. *Its own seed, in itself* —
> so that nothing after that day needed restocking.
