title: "The whole of a person"

The last two verses of the book, and the Hebrew of the famous line is shorter than the English.

## What is missing from it

*Fear God, and keep His commandments, **for this is the whole of a person**.*

The Hebrew is **ki-zeh kol-ha'adam** — for this is all of the man. There is no word for *duty*
in it.

Most English versions supply one, and it changes the sentence. *This is the whole duty of man*
reads as a job description. *This is the whole of man* reads as a definition — what a person
actually amounts to.

## Where the book has been

Twelve chapters of a man with unlimited means testing every avenue and reporting *hevel* —
vapour — nearly forty times. Wisdom, pleasure, building, wealth, work, and each one breath on a
cold morning.

The conclusion is two clauses long, and it is the first thing in the book that is not called
vapour.

## The judgement

*For God will bring **every deed into judgment, together with every hidden thing**, whether good
or evil.*

The Hebrew is **kol-ne'lam** — everything concealed. Ecclesiastes ends by saying the audit is
total.

*In the day when **God will judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ**, according to my gospel.*

**Paul** keeps the sentence and adds the Judge's name. The secrets are still on the list; the
bench is occupied by somebody specific.

## What was done about the verdict before the day

*There is therefore now **no condemnation** to those who are in **Christ Jesus**.*

*Love has been perfected among us in this: that **we may have boldness in the day of
judgment**, because as He is, so are we in this world.*

**John**'s word is **parresia** — freedom of speech, the confidence of somebody with standing in
the room. He does not say the day is cancelled. He says the outcome is settled early, and gives
the reason: *as He is, so are we*.

*Who shall bring a charge against God's elect? **It is God who justifies.***

## And what the commandment turned out to be

*Fear God, and **keep His commandments**.*

*And **this is His commandment: that we should believe on the name of His Son Jesus Christ**, and
love one another, as He gave us commandment.*

**John** reduces the plural to one and puts believing at the front of it.

*"This is the work of God, **that you believe in Him whom He sent**."*

**Jesus** is asked directly — *what shall we do, that we may work the works of God?* — and He
answers with a singular noun. The Preacher's last instruction is not abolished; it is told what
it is for.

## Why the fear is not terror

*The fear of the LORD is **the beginning of wisdom**.* See the note on Proverbs 9:10–12.

Ecclesiastes ends where Proverbs began, having spent twelve chapters proving by experiment that
everywhere else is vapour.

> **The word that is not in the Hebrew.** *Duty.* The line reads *this is the whole of a
> person* — a definition rather than an assignment. And John names the commandment in the
> singular: *that we should believe on the name of His Son.*
