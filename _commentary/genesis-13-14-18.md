God speaks to Abram at the moment he has just given away the best land.

## The timing

*After **Lot had gone**.*

Abram had offered his nephew first choice: *if you take the left, then I will go to the
right*. Lot *lifted his eyes and saw all the plain of Jordan, that it was well watered
everywhere* and took it.

Abram is left with the hill country, and the narrator adds a note about where Lot went: *the
men of Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful against the LORD.*

God speaks after the choice has been made and cannot be revisited.

## The instruction

*"**Look around you, from where you are**."*

The Hebrew is *lift up your eyes now and look from the place where you are*.

**From where you are.** Not from a better vantage point, and not after moving.

Lot had lifted his eyes and chosen. Abram is told to lift his and look at what is left.

## The four directions

*"To the **north and south**, the **east and west**."*

## The promise

*"**All the land you see I will give to you and your offspring forever**."*

The whole of it, including the plain Lot took.

## The offspring

*"I will make your offspring **like the dust of the earth** — **if anyone could count the
dust, then your descendants could be counted**."*

Dust here; stars in chapter 15, when he is taken outside at night; sand on the seashore in
chapter 22, after the mountain.

Three images, each attached to a different moment.

Dust is what is underfoot — and God had said in the previous chapter that Abram would walk
this land.

## The walking

*"**Go, walk through the length and breadth of the land**, for **I am giving it to you**."*

Present participle: *I am giving*.

He is told to walk over ground he does not own, on the strength of a sentence.

The same construction runs through Joshua: *every place your foot touches, I am handing over
to you*. See the note on Joshua 1:1–5.

## How much he owned when he died

One field with a cave in it, at Hebron, bought for four hundred shekels of silver to bury
Sarah.

Stephen says it at his trial: *and God gave him **no inheritance in it, not even enough to
set his foot on**. But even when Abraham had no child, He promised to give it to him for a
possession.*

## What he did

*So Abram **moved his tent** and went to live near **the great trees of Mamre at Hebron**,
and there **he built an altar to the LORD**.*

He moves, and builds. Genesis marks his route by altars — Shechem, Bethel, and now Hebron.

## Where Hebron is

Machpelah is there. The cave he later buys, where Sarah, Abraham, Isaac, Rebekah, Leah and
Jacob are all buried.

He pitches his tent beside the only piece of the country his family would ever hold.

## What Hebrews says about the tents

*By faith he dwelt in the land of promise as in a foreign country, **dwelling in tents**…
for he waited for the city which has foundations, whose builder and maker is God.* See the
note on Hebrews 11:8–12.

> **When it is said.** After the choice went the other way. Lot lifted his eyes and picked
> the watered plain; Abram is told to lift his and look at everything from where he is
> standing.
