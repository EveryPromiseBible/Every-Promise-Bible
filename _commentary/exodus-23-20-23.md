---
key: Exodus 23:20–23
title: My name is in him
updated: 2026-08-09
---
An angel is promised, and one clause in the middle makes clear this is not an ordinary one.

## The sending

*"Look — **I am sending an angel ahead of you** to guard you on the way and **to bring you to
the place I have prepared**."*

Two jobs: keep them on the road, and get them to the destination.

**The place I have prepared** — the Hebrew is *the place which I have established*.

*"In My Father's house are many mansions… **I go to prepare a place for you**. And if I go and
prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you to Myself."*

## The clause

*"Do not rebel against him, for he will not overlook your rebellion, **since My name is in
him**."*

That is not said of any other angel in Scripture.

The Name is not a label. In Hebrew thought it is the person — *the name of the LORD is a
strong tower*. To say the Name is *in* him is to say something about identity, not about rank.

Which is why the same figure keeps doing things angels do not do. At the bush, *the Angel of
the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire* — and two verses later it is *God called to him
out of the midst of the bush*, and Moses hides his face. See the note on Exodus 3:1–3.

## What he cannot do here

*"**He will not overlook your rebellion.**"*

The Hebrew is *he will not lift up your transgression* — **lo yissa lephish'achem**. The idiom
for pardon is to *lift* or *carry away* a sin.

Under this arrangement, the Angel who carries the Name does not carry the sin.

## Where that changes

*But **this Man**, after He had offered one sacrifice for sins forever, sat down at the right
hand of God.* See the note on Hebrews 9:11–12.

The lifting that could not be done on the road to Canaan is the whole of what He did.

And the Name comes back with it: *nor is there salvation in any other, for **there is no other
name** under heaven given among men by which we must be saved.*

## The escort

*"Then I will be an **enemy to your enemies** and an adversary to your adversaries."*

## The rest of the sentence

*"**But if you truly listen to his voice** and do all that I say."*

The whole promise runs on a condition, and the book of Judges is the record of how that went:
*Then the Angel of the LORD came up from Gilgal to Bochim, and said: "I led you up from Egypt…
and I said, I will never break My covenant with you. **But you have not obeyed My voice.**"*

The place he was sent to bring them to was reached anyway, generations later, and not because
the condition was met.

> **The clause that gives it away.** *My name is in him.* An angel who guards a road, who
> cannot be rebelled against, and who does not pardon — and the reason given for all three is
> whose name he carries.
