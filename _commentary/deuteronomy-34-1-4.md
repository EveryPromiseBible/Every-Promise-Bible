The last thing an old man does is climb a mountain to look at something he will not walk in.

## The climb

**34:1** *Then Moses **climbed up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah**, across from
Jericho.*

He is a hundred and twenty. The chapter says two verses later, **34:7**: that *his eyes were not dim nor his
natural vigour diminished*.

Nobody carries him. He goes up.

## Why he is not crossing

At Meribah, told to speak to the rock, he struck it twice and said, **Numbers 20:10**: *must we bring water for you
out of this rock?*

*Because you did not believe Me, to hallow Me in the eyes of the children of Israel, therefore you
shall not bring this assembly into the land.* See the note on Numbers 20:7–9.

He had asked to be let off, **Deuteronomy 3:25**: *I pray, let me cross over and see the good land*. The answer was,
**Deuteronomy 3:26**: *enough of that! Speak no more to Me of this matter*.

## The showing

**34:1** again: *And there **the LORD showed him the whole land**.*

The Hebrew is **vayarehu YHWH et-kol-ha'aretz**.

It is God who does the showing. Nebo has a view, but not this one.

## The sweep

**34:1–3**: *Gilead as far as Dan, all of Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all of Judah as far as
the western sea, the Negev, and the whole plain of the Valley of Jericho, the City of Palms, all
the way to Zoar.*

North to south, and then the coast.

The list runs anticlockwise from the far north around to the Dead Sea. It is a survey, not a
glance.

## The tribal names

Dan, Naphtali, Ephraim, Manasseh, Judah.

The land is described by the allotments it will have, which have not been made yet. Joshua will
divide it years later.

Moses sees the map with the names already on it.

## The word

**34:4** *"**This is the land I promised on oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob** when I said, 'I will give it
to your descendants.'"*

The oath is Genesis 12, repeated to Isaac and to Jacob.

Moses is being shown that a promise made six hundred years earlier is about to be kept.

## The last sentence

**34:4** again: *"**I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you will not cross over into it**."*

The Hebrew is **her'iticha ve'einecha** — I have caused you to see it with your eyes.

No softening. The sentence stands exactly as it was given.

## What happens next

**34:5**: *So **Moses the servant of the LORD died there** in the land of Moab, according to the word of the
LORD.*

**34:6**: *And **He buried him** in a valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth Peor; **but no one knows his
grave to this day**.*

The Hebrew has God doing the burying. There is no one else on the mountain.

## The epitaph

**34:10**: *But **since then there has not arisen in Israel a prophet like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to
face**.*

## Who did cross over

**34:9**: *Now Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him.*

## Where Moses finally stood in it

*And behold, two men talked with Him, who were **Moses and Elijah**, who appeared in glory and
spoke of His decease which He was about to accomplish at Jerusalem.* See the note on
Luke 9:28–36.

On a mountain, inside the land, talking about an exodus.

The Greek word Luke uses for *decease* is **exodos**.

## What Hebrews says about all of them

*These all died in faith, **not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off** were
assured of them, embraced them.* See the note on Hebrews 11:13–16.

The phrase fits this chapter exactly.

> **Who does the showing.** **34:1** again: *The LORD showed him the whole land* — north to south, by the tribal
> names that had not been assigned yet.
