The reversal arrives one verse after the sentence, and the New Testament quotes it twice.

## What has just been said

Three children with three names: Jezreel, **Lo-Ruhamah** — Not-Pitied — and **Lo-Ammi**, Not-My-
People. See the note on Hosea 1:8–9.

*"For you are not My people, and I will not be your God."*

That is the end of verse 9, and it is as final a sentence as the Old Testament contains. It
cancels the covenant formula — *I will be your God and you shall be My people* — clause by
clause.

## The next word

*"**Yet** the day is coming."*

Nothing intervenes. No repentance is recorded between the verses, no condition is stated, and
nobody asks.

## The promise

*"When the people of Israel will be **like the sand of the sea, past counting, past measure**."*

That is the promise to Abraham, quoted back — *I will multiply your descendants as the stars of
the heaven and as the sand which is on the seashore.*

The children have just been named Not-My-People, and the answer is the Abrahamic covenant.

## The place

*"And **in the very place where it was said to them, 'You are not My people,' there they will be
called children of the living God**."*

Not somewhere else. The same ground.

## Who quotes it

*Even us whom He called, not of the Jews only, but also of the Gentiles. As He says also in
Hosea: "**I will call them My people, who were not My people**, and her beloved, who was not
beloved." And it shall come to pass in the place where it was said to them, "You are not My
people," **there they will be called sons of the living God**.*

**Romans** 9, and **Paul** applies both of Hosea's cancelled names to the Gentiles — people who had
never been in the covenant at all.

*Who once were not a people but are now **the people of God**, who had not obtained mercy but now
**have obtained mercy**.*

**Peter** writes it to churches scattered across five Roman provinces, and he uses both names too.

## The living God

*"Children of **the living God**."*

*Simon Peter answered and said, "You are the Christ, **the Son of the living God**."*

*Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that **we should be called children of
God**! And that is what we are.*

**John** uses the same verb — **klethomen**, that we should be *called* — and adds a clause that
is not in Hosea: *and that is what we are.*

## The next verse

*"Then the people of Judah and the people of Israel will be **gathered together as one**. They will
choose **a single head** for themselves."* See the note on Hosea 1:11.

> **The word between the sentence and the promise.** *Yet.* Nothing happens in the gap. Nobody
> repents, no terms are offered, and the covenant formula that was cancelled in verse 9 is
> reissued in verse 10 on the strength of nothing but the word at the front of it.
