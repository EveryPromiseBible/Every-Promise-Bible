title: The stone the builders threw away

The most quoted psalm verse in the New Testament, and Jesus applies it to Himself.

## The gate

*This is **the gate of the LORD**. The just walk through it.*

One gate, and a description of who goes in.

*"**I am the door.** If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved."* See the note on
John 10:7–10.

## The sentence in the middle

*I will thank You, for You answered me. **You have become my salvation.**"*

The Hebrew is **lishuah** — for salvation. The noun behind the Name.

## The stone

*The stone the builders threw away **has become the cornerstone**.*

**Jesus** quotes it at the end of the parable of the vineyard, to the chief priests, having just
told them a story about tenants who killed the owner's son:

*"Have you never read in the Scriptures: '**The stone which the builders rejected has become the
chief cornerstone. This was the LORD's doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes**'?"*

And **Peter**, standing in front of the same council after healing a lame man: *this is the
stone which was rejected by you builders, which has become the chief cornerstone. **Nor is
there salvation in any other**.*

And again in his letter, to scattered churches: *therefore, to you who believe, He is precious;
but to those who are disobedient, the stone which the builders rejected has become the chief
cornerstone.*

Three of the four gospels, Acts and 1 Peter. No other verse in the Psalter is used so often, and
every use is about the same Person.

## Who the builders were

The professionals. Men whose job was to know which stones would carry weight, examining the one
the whole structure would turn on and setting it aside.

*He came to His own, and **His own did not receive Him**.*

## What the psalm says about it

*This is **the LORD's doing**, and it is a wonder in our eyes.*

Not a tragedy repaired. The rejection and the placing are described as one act, and the actor
is God.

*Him, being delivered by **the determined purpose and foreknowledge of God**, you have taken by
lawless hands, have crucified, and put to death.*

> **What Jesus did with this verse.** Quoted it at the men who were about to reject Him, in a
> parable about a son who is killed — and told them the stone they threw away was going in the
> corner.
