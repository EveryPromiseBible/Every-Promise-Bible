Two craftsmen are named in front of the whole nation, and one of the gifts listed is teaching.

## The announcement

*"Look — **the LORD has singled out Bezalel** son of Uri, grandson of Hur, of the tribe of Judah."*

The Hebrew is **qara YHWH beshem** — the LORD has called by name.

The same idiom used of God calling Cyrus, and of the Servant: *the LORD has called Me from the
womb*.

## The name

**Betzal'el** — *in the shadow of God*.

His grandfather **Hur** is the man who held up Moses' other arm at Rephidim while Aaron held the
first.

## The filling

*"**He has filled him with the Spirit of God** — with **wisdom, understanding, and know-how** in
every kind of craft."*

The Hebrew is **chokmah, tevunah, da'at** — wisdom, understanding, knowledge.

The same three nouns used of God at creation in Proverbs: *the LORD by wisdom founded the earth,
by understanding He established the heavens, by His knowledge the depths were broken up*. See the
note on Proverbs 3:19–20.

## What that means here

The first person in Scripture said to be filled with the Spirit of God is a craftsman, and the
filling is for metalwork.

Not for prophecy, leadership or war. For making things well.

## The trades

*"To **design artful works in gold, silver, and bronze**, to **cut and set stones**, to **carve
wood**, to master every skilled craft there is."*

Four disciplines: metal, gem-cutting, joinery, and design.

## The second gift

*"And **He has put it in the hearts** of both Bezalel and **Oholiab** son of Ahisamach, of the tribe
of Dan, **to teach others too**."*

The Hebrew is **ulehorot natan belibbo** — and to teach He has put in his heart.

**Yarah** — to instruct. The root behind **torah**.

## Why that matters

The skill is given, and so is the ability to hand it on.

Without the second gift the tabernacle is built by two men. With it, the camp is trained.

## The two tribes

Judah and Dan.

Judah is the leading tribe, the one Jacob blessed with the sceptre. Dan is the last-mentioned in
most lists and later the most troublesome.

The two chief craftsmen come from the top and the bottom of the standing.

## The other trades

*"He has filled them with the skill to do all kinds of work — as **engravers, designers,
embroiderers** in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen, and **weavers**."*

Textile work as well as metal. The colours are the tabernacle's own palette.

## The verdict

*"They are **masters of every craft and gifted designers**."*

The Hebrew is **choshevei machashavot** — devisers of designs, thinkers of thoughts.

## The constraint

Everything they make is to a pattern shown on the mountain: *see that you make all things
according to the pattern shown you*.

Complete skill, and no freedom of design. The gift is for execution.

## Who spins

*Every woman with skill spun with her own hands and brought what she had spun.* See the note on
Exodus 35:25–29.

The same word, **chakhmat-lev** — wise of heart — used of them.

## What Paul says about gifts

*Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit… **but one and the same Spirit works all
these things, distributing to each one individually as He wills**.* See the note on
1 Corinthians 12:8–11.

## What the whole book ends with

*So Moses finished the work. Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory of the
LORD filled the tabernacle.*

Made by hand, by named men, out of donated jewellery — and filled.

> **The second gift.** Not only the skill, but *He has put it in his heart to teach*. Without that
> line the tabernacle is built by two men instead of a camp.
