James opens his chapter on the tongue by warning people off a job, and two sentences later he has
disqualified everybody in the room, including himself.

## A heavier scale

*Those of us who teach get **weighed on a heavier scale**.*

**Meizon krima** — a greater judgement. And notice the pronoun: **lempsometha**, *we* shall receive.
He is inside the warning, not delivering it from outside.

The stricter assessment is not about whether a teacher is saved. Paul's version of it is explicit:
work that does not survive the fire is lost *but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire*.
It is a question of stewardship, and stewardship is what you do with what you were given, not what
you did to be accepted.

## "And every one of us slips"

*And **every one of us slips, and slips often**.*

**Ptaiomen hapantes** — we all stumble, and the *all* is emphatic. James, the brother of the Lord,
the head of the Jerusalem church, writing Scripture, says *we* — and then adds **polla**, in many
things.

That is his method throughout the letter, and it is worth learning: he raises the standard until
nobody is left standing under it. He does it with the law in chapter two — trip over one point and
you are guilty of the lot — and he does it here with the mouth.

## The perfect man

*Show me a man who never gets it wrong with his mouth, and you are looking at **somebody fully
grown** — a man who can hold the reins on the whole rest of him too.*

**Teleios aner** — a complete man. And James's own next paragraph says flatly that *no man can tame
the tongue*.

So he describes a person and then tells you the person does not exist. Except that one does:

*Who committed no sin, **nor was deceit found in His mouth**; who, when He was reviled, did not
revile in return.*

Peter is quoting Isaiah 53 about Jesus, and it is the only place in Scripture where the description
James sets up is actually met. The perfect man who never stumbled in word, and who therefore had the
whole body under control, walked out of a tomb.

> **Why the bar is set that high.** Not to make teachers anxious — to stop anybody standing on his
> own record. James will let nobody claim a clean mouth, and the one Person who could claim it never
> used it against us. *He was reviled and did not revile in return… who Himself bore our sins in His
> own body on the tree.*
