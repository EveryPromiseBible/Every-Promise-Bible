key: Psalms 42:9–10
title: Where is your God?
updated: 2026-08-09

The lowest point in the psalm, and the taunt in it is a question.

## What he says to God

*I say to God, my Rock, "**Why have You forgotten me? Why must I walk in shadow**, crushed by
the enemy's weight?"*

He calls Him *my Rock* in the same sentence as accusing Him of forgetting. The psalm does not
treat those as incompatible.

## The taunt

*It cuts to the bone when my foes taunt me, saying all day long, "**So where is this God of
yours?**"*

The Hebrew is **ayyeh Elohecha** — where is your God? It is asked twice in the psalm, and once
more in Psalm 79.

## Where the same question is asked again

*"**He trusted in God; let Him deliver Him now if He will have Him**, for He said, 'I am the Son
of God.'"*

**Matthew** records it said at the foot of a cross, by chief priests and elders, to a Man who
had been abandoned by everybody who could have answered them.

*"My God, My God, **why have You forsaken Me**?"* — **Jesus**, quoting another psalm from the
cross, with the same verb the taunters used.

The psalmist asks *why have You forgotten me* and is wrong about the fact. There was one
occasion when the question was accurate, and it was not asked by a man in the wrong.

## What he does with it

*Why are you cast down, O my soul? And why are you disquieted within me? **Hope in God, for I
shall yet praise Him**.*

Three times in two psalms he interrogates his own soul rather than answering the taunt. He does
not argue with the enemies; he talks to himself about God.

*Casting all your care upon Him, **for He cares for you**.*

## The verse in between

*Yet the LORD will command His lovingkindness in the daytime, and **in the night His song shall
be with me**.*

**Chesed** by day and a song by night, written by a man who has just said he is walking in
shadow.

> **What is not answered.** The enemies asked *where is your God* and the psalm never tells
> them. It turns instead and asks the same question of its own soul, with a different verb —
> *why are you cast down?* — and answers that one.
