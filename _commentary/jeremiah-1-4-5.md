The call of a prophet, and every verb in it is in the past.

## The opening

*The word of the LORD came to me.*

The Hebrew is **vayehi devar-YHWH elai** — and the word of the LORD was to me.

The formula runs through the book more than fifty times.

## The first clause

*"**Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you**."*

The Hebrew for *formed* is **etzorcha**, from **yatsar** — the potter's word.

The same verb as *the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground*, and the same root behind
the potter's house Jeremiah is later sent to watch. See the note on Jeremiah 18:1–6.

## The word for knew

**Yada** — the Hebrew word for knowing by relationship rather than by information.

It is the word used of Adam and Eve, of God knowing Abraham, and of *you only have I known of
all the families of the earth*.

Not *I knew about you*. Knew you.

## The second clause

*"**Before you drew your first breath, I set you apart**."*

The Hebrew is **hiqdashticha** — I sanctified you, made you holy.

The verb belongs to the priesthood and the vessels of the tabernacle. Jeremiah was of a priestly
family at Anathoth, and the word for consecrating a priest is applied to him before birth.

## The third clause

*"**I appointed you as a prophet to the nations**."*

The Hebrew is **nathaticha** — I gave you, I appointed you.

**Lagoyim** — to the nations. Not to Judah. A prophet who will spend his life speaking to one
small kingdom is commissioned to the nations in his first sentence.

## The tenses

Knew, set apart, appointed. Three completed actions, all of them before he existed.

Nothing in the call is conditional on anything Jeremiah does.

## What he says back

*"Ah, Lord GOD! Behold, **I cannot speak, for I am a youth**."*

The Hebrew is **na'ar** — a boy, a young man.

He is not the first. Moses said *I am slow of speech*. Gideon said *my clan is the weakest*.
Isaiah said *I am a man of unclean lips*.

## The answer

*"**Do not say, 'I am a youth,'** for you shall go to all to whom I send you… **Do not be afraid
of their faces**, for I am with you to deliver you."*

*Then the LORD put forth His hand and touched my mouth.*

The objection is about speaking, and the answer is a hand on the mouth.

## What the life turned out to be

Forty years of preaching that nobody accepted. A scroll cut up and burned by a king. A cistern
he was lowered into with ropes and left in the mud. And Jerusalem falling anyway, exactly as he
had said.

The call promised he was known, not that he would be heard.

## Where else this language goes

*He said to me, "You are My servant, O Israel"… **the LORD has called Me from the womb**.* Of the
servant in Isaiah.

*He who separated me from my mother's womb and called me through His grace.* Paul, of himself.
See the note on Galatians 1:15–17.

Both take Jeremiah's construction and apply it elsewhere.

## What the psalm says about the forming

*For You formed my inward parts; You covered me in my mother's womb… **and in Your book they all
were written**, the days fashioned for me, when as yet there were none of them.* See the note on
Psalms 139:13–16.

> **The tenses.** Knew, set apart, appointed — three finished actions, all of them completed
> before there was anybody to do them to.
