The ark is finally in Jerusalem, and the staffing arrangements name a familiar man.

## The day

The ark has come up from Obed-edom's house on the shoulders of consecrated Levites, with
sacrifices, singers and a linen ephod. See the note on 1 Chronicles 15:25–28.

David has given out bread, meat and raisin cakes to the whole crowd, and delivered a psalm —
*oh, give thanks to the LORD! Call upon His name*.

Then the roster.

## The first appointment

*David left **Asaph and his kinsmen** there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD, **to
minister there regularly, according to each day's need**.*

The Hebrew is *for the day's matter in its day*.

Continuous. Not a festival arrangement — a daily one.

Asaph is the chief musician, and twelve psalms carry his name, including *truly God is good to
Israel, to such as are pure in heart. But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled*.

## The second

*And he left **Obed-edom and his sixty-eight relatives** to serve with them.*

The man whose house the ark had been parked at for three months. See the note on
1 Chronicles 13:12–14.

He does not go home.

## What the sixty-eight are

His household, and the number is worth setting beside chapter 26, where his descendants are
counted at sixty-two, *capable men, strong for the work*. See the note on
1 Chronicles 26:6–11.

## The post

*While **Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah kept the gates**.*

Gatekeeper.

Not a musician, not a priest. The man who opens up and locks the door.

## What is happening six miles away

The verses immediately after this:

*And he left Zadok the priest and his brethren the priests **before the tabernacle of the LORD
at the high place that was at Gibeon**, to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of
burnt offering regularly morning and evening.*

Two centres, at once.

The tabernacle of Moses at Gibeon with the altar and the sacrifices and no ark. And a tent in
Jerusalem with the ark, singers, and no veil.

That arrangement runs until Solomon's temple, and it is what Amos means by *the tabernacle of
David that is fallen*. See the note on Acts 15:13–18.

## What Obed-edom chose

The tent with the ark in it.

He had three months of it in his house. He appears to have decided that was the arrangement he
wanted to keep.

## Where the psalm they were given goes

*Seek the LORD and His strength; **seek His face evermore**.*

*Oh, give thanks to the LORD, for He is good! For His mercy endures forever.*

The last line becomes the refrain of Psalm 136, twenty-six times over.

## What the chapter ends with

*Then all the people departed, every man to his house; and David returned to bless his house.*

Everybody goes home, and the singers stay.

> **What he did with the three months.** Kept them. The ark went up to Jerusalem and the man
> whose house it had been in went with it, and took a job on the door.
