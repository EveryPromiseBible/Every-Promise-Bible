David is not allowed to build the temple, so he does everything except build it.

## What he hands over

*David handed Solomon **the plans** — the design for the porch, the buildings, the
storerooms, the upper rooms, the inner rooms, and **the room for the mercy seat**.*

The Hebrew for *plans* is **tabnith** — a pattern, a model, an architectural
drawing.

It is the same word used of what Moses was shown on the mountain: *see that you make
all things according to the **pattern** shown you on the mountain.* See the note on
Exodus 25:31–40.

Both buildings were copied from something.

## Where they came from

*He gave him the plan for everything **the Spirit had laid on his heart**.*

The Hebrew reads *the pattern of all that he had by the Spirit.*

David worked out the specification, and the chapter says where the specification came
from.

## The detail

Read the list, and then the verses following: the courses of priests, the weight of
gold for each lampstand, the weight of silver for each table, the gold for the forks
and basins by weight, the refined gold for the altar of incense.

Nothing is left to Solomon's judgement. The design is complete down to the cutlery.

## The claim

*"All of this," David said, "**the LORD made clear to me in writing, His own hand upon
me** — every detail of the plan, made plain."*

The Hebrew is *the LORD made me understand in writing by His hand upon me.*

David wrote it down, and says the understanding was given while he wrote.

The same combination as Moses at Sinai — a pattern shown, and a man producing the
drawing.

## Why he could not build

Earlier in the chapter: *"You shall not build a house for My name, **because you have
been a man of war** and have shed blood."*

He is refused the job and not refused the involvement.

## What he did instead

He gathered the materials: gold, silver, bronze, iron, timber, onyx, marble — and
then gave his own private treasure on top of it.

*"Because I have set my affection on the house of my God, I have given to the house of
my God, **over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house, my own special
treasure**."*

A man builds a house he will never see, out of money he did not have to give, to a
design he wrote down and handed to somebody else.

## What he says to Solomon

*"Be strong and of good courage, and do it; **do not fear nor be dismayed**, for the
LORD God — my God — will be with you. **He will not leave you nor forsake you**, until
you have finished all the work."*

## What is handed over

*The plans for the vestibule, its houses, its treasuries, its upper chambers, its inner
chambers… **and the plans for all that he had by the Spirit**.*

Not sketches. A complete specification, room by room, with weights given for the gold of each
lampstand and each dish.

## Who drew them

*"**All of this the LORD made clear to me in writing, His own hand upon me** — every detail of
the plan, made plain."* See the note on 1 Chronicles 28:19.

David did not design it. He received it, the way Moses received the tabernacle pattern on the
mountain.

*Who serve **the copy and shadow of the heavenly things**… "See that you make all things
according to the pattern shown you on the mountain."* See the note on Hebrews 8:6–7.

## Who does not get to build it

David.

He gathered the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the timber and the stone; he drew up
the divisions of priests and the courses of singers; he handed over the plans. And he was told
plainly he would not lay a stone of it. See the note on 2 Samuel 7:1–3.

*Whoever desires to become great among you, let him be your servant.*

## What he says to his son

*"**Be strong and of good courage, and do it; do not fear nor be dismayed, for the LORD God —
my God — will be with you.** He will not leave you nor forsake you."*

That last sentence is the one **Hebrews** lifts and hands to ordinary readers as the ground
for contentment: *for He Himself has said, "I will never leave you nor forsake you."*

> **What he told the assembly.** *All things come from You, and of Your own we have
> given You.* He raised the largest building fund in Israel's history and then said
> nothing in it had been theirs to begin with.
