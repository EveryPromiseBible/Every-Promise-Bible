The verse Paul builds *the spirit of faith* on, and it is not spoken from a
comfortable place.

## The three rescues

*You rescued **my life from death**, **my eyes from tears**, **my feet from
stumbling**.*

Three, and they cover three different things: the body, the emotions, the walk.

The Hebrew for the middle one is that He delivered his eye from tears. Not that the
tears were forbidden — that they were stopped.

## What he does with it

*I will **walk before the LORD in the land of the living**.*

Not in the temple, not in retreat. In the land of the living — ordinary daylight,
among people.

## The line Paul quotes

*I believed, even when I said, "**I am badly shaken**."*

Both halves are in the sentence at once. He believed **and** he said he was in
pieces.

The Hebrew is *I was greatly afflicted*. Nothing is being minimised.

Paul takes this exact line into 2 Corinthians — *we have the same spirit of faith
as the one who wrote, "I believed, and so I spoke"* — while listing beatings,
shipwrecks and being hard pressed on every side. See the note on
2 Corinthians 4:13–15.

So whatever the spirit of faith is, both men exercised it while stating plainly how
bad things were.

## The next line

*In my alarm I said, "**Every person is a liar.**"*

The Hebrew is *in my haste* — said hurriedly, in panic. He is quoting himself at
his worst and flagging that it was hasty.

The psalm keeps his panic on the record rather than editing it out.

## What surrounds it

Read the psalm from the top and it opens with *the pains of death surrounded me,
and the pangs of Sheol laid hold of me; I found trouble and sorrow.*

And *I love the LORD, because He has heard my voice.*

## Where it ends up

Further down the same psalm: *what shall I render to the LORD for all His benefits
toward me? **I will take up the cup of salvation** and call upon the name of the
LORD.*

Psalm 116 is one of the Hallel psalms, sung at Passover. The cup of salvation is in
it.

Jesus sang this psalm with the disciples in the upper room, on the night He took a
cup and said *this is My blood of the new covenant.*

> **The verse in the middle of it.** *Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death
> of His saints.* Written by a man who has just said God rescued his life from
> death — and who still calls the other outcome precious.
