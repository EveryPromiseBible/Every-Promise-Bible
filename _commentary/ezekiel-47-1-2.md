Ezekiel has been shown a temple in enormous detail for eight chapters. Then he is
brought back to the front door and shown water.

## Where it starts

*Water flowing out **from under the threshold of the house**.*

Not from a spring, a well or a river. From under the doorstep of the building
itself.

The source is the house.

## Which direction

*Toward the east, **for the house faced east**.*

East is the direction of the entrance, and the direction of the Dead Sea — which the
chapter goes on to say the water heals. *The waters shall be healed… and everything
will live wherever the river goes.*

## The detail he adds

*The water was coming down **from beneath the south side of the house, south of the
altar**.*

He locates it precisely. It passes the altar on its way out, and it comes from the
side.

The burnt offering was killed *on the north side of the altar*. The water runs out
past the south side. See the note on Leviticus 1:10–13.

## "Trickling"

*And there **the water was trickling out** from the south side.*

The Hebrew is a small flow — seeping, oozing. At the threshold it is barely
anything.

What follows in the chapter is the measuring: a thousand cubits, and it is ankle
deep. Another thousand, knee deep. Another thousand, up to the waist. Another
thousand, and *a river that could not be crossed, for the water was too deep, water
in which one must swim.*

Four measurements, and the further it gets from the door the deeper it is.

## What John does with it

*"**Out of his innermost being will flow rivers of living water**." He was speaking
about the Spirit.*

Jesus said it standing up in the temple courts at the Feast of Tabernacles, on the
day the water-pouring ceremony did not happen. See the note on John 7:37–39.

Ezekiel's water comes from under the threshold of the house. John's comes from
inside a person — and Paul says *you are the temple of God, and the Spirit of God
dwells in you.*

## Where the river ends

*Along the bank of the river, on this side and that, will grow all kinds of trees for
food. Their leaves will not wither, and their fruit will not fail. They will bear
fruit every month… **their fruit will be for food, and their leaves for medicine**.*

Revelation 22 closes the Bible with the same picture: a pure river of water of life,
proceeding from the throne, with the tree of life on either side, *and the leaves of
the tree were for the healing of the nations.*

> **What the man with the measuring line does.** He walks Ezekiel out into it four
> times and asks him each time how deep it is. The prophet is not shown the river
> from the bank. He is put in it, up to the waist, and then told to swim.
