Moses does three things he was not told to do, and the water comes anyway.

## The gathering

*Moses and Aaron **gathered the assembly in front of the rock**.*

That part was the instruction.

## What he says

*"**Listen now, you rebels** — **must we bring you water out of this rock?**"*

Two things in one sentence, and neither was in the instruction.

**You rebels** — the Hebrew is **hammorim**. It is the only recorded time Moses addresses
the nation like that, and the man described in Numbers 12 as *very humble, more than all
men who were on the face of the earth* is the one saying it.

**Must we** — the pronoun is first person plural. God had said *you will bring water out
of the rock for them*, and Moses says *must **we***.

## The striking

*Then Moses **lifted his hand and struck the rock twice** with his staff.*

He was told to speak to it. He hits it, and then hits it again.

The doubling is recorded deliberately. Numbers could have said he struck it.

## What comes out

*And **water came gushing out, more than enough**, and **the people drank, and their
animals too**.*

The Hebrew is *water came out abundantly*.

Nothing is withheld. The people get everything that was promised, the animals are
included as specified, and the supply is described as more than sufficient.

The failure is Moses'. The provision is unaffected by it.

## What the earlier instruction had been

At Rephidim, thirty-eight years before: *strike the rock, and water will pour out.* See
the note on Exodus 17:4–7.

He is doing what worked last time. The instruction had changed. See the note on
Numbers 20:7–9.

## What the striking said

The verdict in verse 12 names the problem as failing to *honor Me as holy in the sight of
the people of Israel*.

The nation was watching. What they saw was a furious man calling them rebels and beating
a rock, and what they were told to see was a word spoken and water coming.

## The name of the place

*This was the water of **Meribah** — contention — because the children of Israel contended
with the LORD, and He was hallowed among them.*

The same name as Rephidim carried. Two places called Quarrelling, thirty-eight years
apart, over the same commodity.

## What Paul says the rock was

*They drank from a spiritual Rock that went with them, and **that Rock was Christ**.* See
the note on 1 Corinthians 10:1–5.

## What Moses says about it later

In Deuteronomy he mentions it three times, and each time he attaches it to the people:
*the LORD was angry with me for your sakes*, and *because of you*.

He never disputes the ruling.

> **What is not affected.** The water. Numbers puts the disobedience and the abundance in
> the same sentence — struck twice, and it came out more than enough.
