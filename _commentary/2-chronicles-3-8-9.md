The chronicler gives the weight of the gold in the holiest room, and then the weight
of the nails.

## The room

*Its length ran **the full width of the house, twenty cubits, and twenty cubits
across** as well.*

A perfect square in plan, and 1 Kings adds the third dimension — twenty high. A cube,
about thirty feet each way.

Revelation describes the new Jerusalem the same way: *its length, breadth and height
are equal.*

## The gold

*He overlaid it with fine gold — **six hundred talents of it**.*

A talent is somewhere around seventy-five pounds. Six hundred of them is upward of
twenty tons of gold, in one room.

## The nails

*The nails alone weighed **fifty shekels of gold**.*

The chronicler weighs the nails.

It is the smallest item in the building account, and he records it because the point
of the whole passage is that nothing in that room was ordinary — down to the
fastenings holding the sheets to the wall.

## What is in the room

The next verses: two cherubim, made and overlaid with gold, with a combined wingspan
of twenty cubits — wing tip to wall on both sides, and the inner wings meeting in the
middle.

*They stood on their feet, and **they faced inward**.*

The chronicler notes which way they were looking, and it is the one detail 1 Kings
does not give.

## The cherubim over the ark

Separate from these, and much smaller: the two beaten out of the mercy seat itself,
facing each other with their faces toward it.

*"There I will meet with you, and I will speak with you **from above the mercy seat,
from between the two cherubim**."* See the note on Exodus 25:17–22.

## Where cherubim appear first

At the gate of Eden, with a flaming sword, keeping the way to the tree of life. See
the note on Genesis 3:20–24.

They are guards at the beginning of the Bible, and by Exodus they are hammered into
the lid of a box where God says He will meet somebody.

## What Solomon said about the room

*"But will God indeed dwell on the earth? Behold, heaven and the heaven of heavens
cannot contain You. **How much less this temple which I have built!**"*

Twenty tons of gold, gold nails, and the man who commissioned it says at the
dedication that it will not do.

> **What is in front of the throne in Revelation.** Not a cube of gold. *A sea of
> glass, like crystal* — and around the throne, four living creatures full of eyes,
> who *do not rest day or night, saying, "Holy, holy, holy."*
