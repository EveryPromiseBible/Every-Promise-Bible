---
key: 2 Corinthians 7:5–7
title: "Fightings without, fears within"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
The most transparent paragraph the apostle ever wrote about his own state, and the answer God sent
him was a man walking up a road.

## He does not tidy it up

*Our bodies got no rest at all; we were pressed hard from every side — **fighting on the outside,
fears on the inside**.*

Outside, opposition. Inside, **phoboi** — fears, plural. This is the man who wrote *be anxious for
nothing*, and he does not pretend he was not afraid.

Two chapters earlier he tells us what the fear was about: he had an open door for the gospel at
Troas and *no rest in my spirit, because I did not find Titus my brother* — so he left. He walked
away from an open door because he was worried about a friend and a church that had fallen out with
him.

That is in the Bible, written by the author, about himself.

## Who God comforts

*But God, **who comforts those who have been brought low**, comforted us.*

**Tous tapeinous** — the low, the flattened, the ones down at ground level. It is the only
qualification named. Not the faithful, not the strong, not the ones handling it well.

The verb is **parakaleo** — to call alongside — and its noun is **parakletos**, which is the name
Jesus gave the Holy Spirit the night before He died. The comfort of God is not a mood He sends. It
is a Person He gives.

## How it arrived

*Comforted us **by the arrival of Titus**.*

No vision. No angel. No voice in the night. God's comfort for the apostle to the Gentiles came up
the road with luggage.

And then it doubles: *not only by his arrival, but by the comfort he had received from you.* Titus
had been comforted by the Corinthians; Paul is comforted by Titus carrying it. Comfort travels from
person to person and loses nothing on the way — *the God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our
tribulation, that we may be able to comfort those who are in any trouble*.

> **Low is the qualification.** If you have ever thought your fear disqualifies you from
> encouragement, read the list again: fightings, fears, no rest — and then *God, who comforts the
> downcast, comforted us*. He does not wait for you to rally. And He very often sends it with a
> face and a name, because He knows what He is dealing with. Christ Himself was strengthened by an
> angel in a garden; nobody is above being comforted.
