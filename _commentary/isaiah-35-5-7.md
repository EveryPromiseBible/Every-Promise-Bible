---
key: Isaiah 35:5–7
title: "Then the lame will leap"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
Four disabilities named and reversed, and **Jesus** sends this list to a man in prison.

## The four

*Then the eyes of the blind will be opened, and the ears of the deaf unstopped. Then the lame will
leap like a deer, and the tongue that could not speak will sing.*

Sight, hearing, walking, speech.

The Hebrew for *leap* is **dalag** — to spring, to bound. And the fourth is not that the mute
speaks. It is that he **sings**.

## Who was sent this list

*And John, calling two of his disciples to him, sent them to Jesus, saying, "Are You the Coming
One, or do we look for another?"*

**John** the Baptist is in Herod's prison and is not sure.

*"Go and tell John the things you have seen and heard: **that the blind see, the lame walk**, the
lepers are cleansed, **the deaf hear**, the dead are raised, the poor have the gospel preached to
them."*

**Jesus** does not answer the question. He recites Isaiah 35 back, adds two items, and lets the
man in the cell do the arithmetic.

## The two added

*The dead are raised. The poor have the gospel preached to them.*

Neither is in Isaiah 35. Both are elsewhere in Isaiah, and the second one — **euangelizontai** —
is put last, in a list of miracles, as though it were the largest of them.

## The water

*Water will break open in the wilderness, and streams will run through the desert. The burning
sand will become a pool, the thirsty ground bubbling springs.*

The Hebrew for *burning sand* is **sharav** — the shimmer of heat over ground, the mirage.

What looked like water becomes water.

*"**If anyone thirsts, let him come to Me and drink.** He who believes in Me, as the Scripture has
said, **out of his heart will flow rivers of living water**."*

## The jackals

*Where jackals once curled up to rest, **grass and reeds and papyrus will grow**.*

The lair becomes a marsh. Isaiah does not relocate the place; he changes what it produces.

## What the chapter is for

*Strengthen the weak hands, and make firm the feeble knees. Say to those who are **fearful-hearted,
"Be strong, do not fear! Behold, your God will come"**… He will come and save you.*

The verses immediately before, and **Hebrews** picks up the same two body parts: *therefore
strengthen the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees.*

## And the road

*A highway shall be there, and a road, and it shall be called **the Highway of Holiness**… whoever
walks the road, although a fool, shall not go astray.*

Isaiah 35 ends with a road so plain that a fool cannot lose it.

> **What Jesus did with this passage.** Quoted it to a man in a cell who had asked whether He was
> the one — not as an argument, but as an inventory. And He added a line Isaiah did not have:
> *the poor have the gospel preached to them.*
