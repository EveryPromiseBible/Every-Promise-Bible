The only source of light in the holy place, and it is described as one object
rather than seven.

## Hammered

*A lampstand of pure gold, **hammered out** — base, shaft, cups… **all of one solid
piece**.*

The Hebrew is **miqshah** — beaten work. It was not cast in a mould or assembled
from parts. A talent of solid gold, worked with a hammer until it became this.

*Buds and branches and shaft — **all one seamless piece**.*

The stress on its oneness is repeated three times in the passage. Six branches
coming out of a central shaft, and the whole thing is one piece of metal.

## No wood

Everything else in the tabernacle is acacia wood overlaid with gold — the ark, the
table, the altar. The lampstand alone is gold all the way through, with no other
substance in it.

## Almond blossoms

*Cups shaped like **almond blossoms**, buds and petals.*

Twenty-two of them in total, up the shaft and along the branches.

The almond is the first tree in that country to flower, before its own leaves and
before anything else. Jeremiah's first vision is an almond branch, and God tells him
*I am watching over My word to perform it* — a play on the Hebrew, since the word
for almond is *shaqed*, the watcher.

And Aaron's rod that budded was an almond rod. It was dead wood that blossomed
overnight, and it was kept in the ark.

## The light goes forward

*Set them up so their **light spills forward**.*

The Hebrew is that the lamps give light *over against* the lampstand — the flames
are angled to illuminate what is in front, not the stand itself.

The lampstand exists to light the room and the table of showbread opposite it, not
to be looked at.

## What was in that room

There were no windows in the holy place. No natural light reached it at all.

Whatever a priest did in there — the bread, the incense — he did by this light and no
other.

## The talent of gold

*Use **a full talent of pure gold** for the lampstand and all its tools.*

Around seventy-five pounds of gold, worked by hand into one object.

## Where it turns up again

*I turned around… and there they were: **seven lampstands, all of them gold**. In
among them stood **a figure like the Son of man**.*

Revelation 1. Seven separate lampstands now — the seven churches — and He is
standing in the middle of them. See the note on Revelation 1:12–13.

The one that was beaten out of a single piece has become seven, with Him among
them.

> **The last line of the instructions.** *Be sure you make it all **to the pattern
> you were shown on the mountain**.* Said twice in this chapter. Hebrews picks it up:
> the priests serve *the copy and shadow of the heavenly things*. Moses was shown
> something and told to reproduce it.
