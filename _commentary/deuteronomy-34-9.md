One verse, near the last page of the Torah, about what transferred.

## Where it sits

Moses has died on Nebo, shown the whole land from Gilead to the Western Sea, and *He buried
him in a valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth Peor, **but no one knows his grave to
this day**.*

Then thirty days of weeping, and then this.

## The filling

*Now **Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom**.*

The Hebrew is **ruach chokmah** — spirit of wisdom.

The same phrase used of Bezalel, who built the tabernacle. See the note on
Exodus 31:1–5.

## The reason

*Because **Moses had laid his hands on him**.*

The Hebrew is *for Moses had laid his hands upon him*.

The verse gives a cause, and it is a gesture.

## Where the laying on happened

Numbers 27. Moses had asked God for a successor — *let the LORD set a man over the
congregation… **that the congregation of the LORD may not be like sheep which have no
shepherd***.

And the instruction: *take Joshua the son of Nun with you, a man in whom is the Spirit, and
**lay your hand on him**… **and you shall give some of your authority to him**, that all the
congregation of the children of Israel may be obedient.*

*Some of your authority.* The Hebrew is *of your honour*.

Note the order: Joshua is already described as *a man in whom is the Spirit* before the
hands go on.

## The result

*So **the Israelites listened to him** and did what the LORD had commanded Moses.*

The purpose stated in Numbers — that the congregation would obey — is recorded as having
happened.

## Who Joshua was

Moses' attendant for forty years. He had led the fight against Amalek, gone partway up
Sinai, stayed in the tent of meeting when Moses left it, and been one of the two spies who
said go. See the notes on Exodus 17:8–13 and Numbers 14:5–10.

## What the chapter says next

*But **since then there has not arisen in Israel a prophet like Moses, whom the LORD knew
face to face**.*

The Torah ends by saying the succession was not a replacement.

Joshua gets the wisdom and the authority. The face-to-face is not transferred, and Numbers
12 had already marked it as unique. See the note on Numbers 12:4–9.

## Where the gesture goes

Paul to Timothy: *do not neglect **the gift that is in you, which was given to you by
prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the eldership**.* See the note on
1 Timothy 4:14–16.

And in the second letter: *stir up the gift of God which is in you **through the laying on
of my hands***.

The same act, and Paul attributes the gift to it twice.

## What Joshua is told at the start of his own book

*"**Moses my servant is gone. So now it's your turn**."*

And: *"**The way I was with Moses, that's how I will be with you**."* See the note on
Joshua 1:1–5.

> **What the verse attributes it to.** Not experience, and not the forty years. Two hands
> on a head — and the nation listened to him afterward.
