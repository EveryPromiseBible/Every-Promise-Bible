title: Carried by the fullness of Your kindness

The one verse in the psalm about coming in, and the ground it names is not the worshipper's.

## The contrast being drawn

The verses before this are about people who cannot come in — *the boastful shall not stand in
Your sight… You destroy those who speak falsehood.*

Then: ***But I**, carried by the fullness of Your kindness, will come into Your house.*

The Hebrew opens **va'ani** — *but as for me*. The turn is the whole point of the verse.

## What carries him in

*The fullness of Your kindness.*

**Berov chasdekha** — in the abundance of Your **chesed**. Not by right, not by record, and not
by invitation earned. The preposition does the work: he comes in *in* God's covenant love, as
in a vehicle.

*But God, who is rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us… **made us
alive together with Christ** — by grace you have been saved.*

**Paul**'s sentence has the same structure: a *but*, and then a reason located entirely in God.

## Where he is going

*I will bow toward Your holy temple **in reverence of You**.*

The fear and the confidence are in one clause. He is not casual about the house he has just
said he is entitled to enter on somebody else's account.

*Let us therefore **come boldly to the throne of grace**.*

## What he asks for next

*Lead me, LORD, **in Your rightness**, for my watchers are all around. **Make Your way level and
straight** before me.*

Not *in my rightness*. The Hebrew is **betzidqatekha** — in Your righteousness.

*And be found in Him, **not having my own righteousness**, which is from the law, but that which
is through faith in **Christ**.*

The psalm asks to be led in a righteousness that belongs to God, and **Paul** says how a man
comes to stand in one.

## The word for the enemies

**Shorerai** — my watchers. People observing him, waiting for a stumble.

> **The two words the verse turns on.** *But I.* Everybody in the preceding verses is shut out,
> and the reason the psalmist is not is stated immediately and is not about him.
