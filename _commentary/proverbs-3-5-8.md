The instruction is not to throw the understanding away. It is not to put weight on it.

## The first line

*Trust in the LORD **with all your heart**.*

The Hebrew is **betach el-YHWH bekol-libbeka**.

**Batach** — to lie helpless on, to fling oneself down on something. The root sense is of leaning
one's full weight.

## The second

*And **do not lean on your own understanding**.*

The Hebrew is **ve'el-binateka al-tisha'en**.

**Sha'an** — to lean, to prop oneself. The word used of a man leaning on a staff.

## What is not said

Not: do not use your understanding.

The verb is about what you rest your weight on when the ground gives way.

## The word

**Binah** — discernment, the ability to distinguish between things. A good word everywhere else in
Proverbs.

*Get wisdom, get **understanding**.* The book commands the acquiring of the very thing this verse
says not to lean on.

## The third line

*In all your ways **acknowledge Him**.*

The Hebrew is **da'ehu** — know Him.

**Yada** — the intimate verb. Not *consult* Him, and not *acknowledge* in the sense of a nod.

## The scope

**Bekol-derakeika** — in all your ways.

Not the religious ones. All of them.

## The promise

*And **He will make your paths straight**.*

The Hebrew is **yeyasher orchoteika**.

**Yashar** — to make straight, level, smooth. Not necessarily short — a straight road can be long.

## The warning

*Do not be **wise in your own eyes**.*

The Hebrew is **al-tehi chakam be'eineika**.

The phrase reappears at the end of the book, in its bluntest form: *do you see a man wise in his own
eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for him*.

## The pair

*Fear the LORD and **turn from evil**.*

Job is described in exactly this pair: *one who feared God and shunned evil*.

## The result

*This will be **healing to your body**, **refreshment to your very bones**.*

The Hebrew is **rif'ut leshorreka** — healing to your navel, or sinew. And **shiqqui le'atzmoteika**
— drink, moisture, to your bones.

## The image

Bones going dry is the book's picture of what worry does.

*A sound heart is life to the body, but **envy is rottenness to the bones**.* And *a broken spirit
dries the bones*. See the note on Proverbs 17:22.

The promise here is the reverse: irrigation.

## What comes next

*Honour the LORD with your possessions, and with the firstfruits of all your increase.* See the
note on Proverbs 3:9–10.

And then: *my son, **do not despise the chastening of the LORD**… for whom the LORD loves He
corrects*. See the note on Proverbs 3:11–12.

Quoted in full in Hebrews 12, immediately after *looking unto Jesus*.

## The chapter's other famous line

*Happy is the man who finds wisdom… **She is a tree of life to those who take hold of her**.* See the
note on Proverbs 3:13–18.

The only tree of life mentioned between Genesis 3 and Revelation 22 is in this book.

> **The verb.** Not *do not use* your understanding — *do not lean on it*. The word is about where a
> man puts his weight when the ground moves.
