key: Ruth 3:1–4
title: Wash, anoint, dress, and go down
updated: 2026-09-10
---
Naomi sends Ruth to the threshing floor with four instructions and one about timing.

## What she is looking for

**3:1** *"My daughter, shouldn't I look for **a home for you** — a place where you'll be **settled and
cared for**?"*

The Hebrew is **manoach** — a resting place. It is the noun used of the dove in Genesis, who
found no place to set her foot down and had to be reached for and drawn back in. See the note
on Genesis 8:6–12.

Naomi is not looking for a husband in the abstract. She is looking for somewhere her
daughter-in-law can stop.

## The four

**3:3** *"**Wash** yourself, **put on perfume**, **dress in your finest**, and **go down** to the
floor."*

Washing, anointing, clothing — and then descending.

They are the three things done to a priest at his consecration, in that order: washed with
water, clothed in the garments, anointed with oil. See the note on Leviticus 8:10–13.

## What she takes off

Her widow's clothes. She has worn them since Moab, through the barley harvest and the wheat
harvest.

**Isaiah 61:10**: *He has clothed me with **the garments of salvation**, He has covered me with **the robe of
righteousness**, as a bridegroom decks himself with ornaments.*

And Paul, of what a believer is dressed in, **Galatians 3:27**: *for as many of you as were baptized into Christ
**have put on Christ**.*

## The timing

**3:3** again: *"But **don't let him see you until he has finished eating and drinking**."*

She waits until the work of the harvest is done and the man is at rest. Nothing is asked of
him while he is still threshing.

## Where she goes

*Down* to the floor, and then to his feet.

The threshing floor is where grain is separated from chaff, and Boaz is, **3:2**: *winnowing barley*
when she arrives — the same barley she had been gleaning behind him. See the note on
Ruth 2:17–18.

## What she is told to say

Nothing. **3:4**: *"He'll tell you what to do."*

She goes with no speech prepared, and what she says when the moment comes is her own — and it
is not what Naomi told her. See the note on Ruth 3:8–9.

> **The word Naomi uses.** **Manoach** — a resting place. She sends her out to find one, and
> the chapter ends with *sit still, my daughter*.
