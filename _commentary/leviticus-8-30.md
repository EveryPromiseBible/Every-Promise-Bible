---
key: Leviticus 8:30
title: Oil and blood together
updated: 2026-08-09
---
One verse, and it is the only place in the ritual where the two are applied at the same moment.

## What is taken

*Moses took some of **the anointing oil** and some of **the blood from the altar**.*

Both, in one hand.

Up to this point they have been kept apart. The oil was poured on Aaron's head at the start of
the chapter. See the note on Leviticus 8:10–13. The blood was put on the ear, the thumb and the
toe. See the note on Leviticus 8:22–24.

Now they are mixed and used together.

## Who gets it

*And sprinkled it **on Aaron and his clothes, and on his sons and their clothes**.*

This is where the sons come in. Nothing was poured on them earlier — they were dressed and stood
there. Here they are sprinkled, and it is oil and blood at once.

## The order that never reverses

The blood is always first.

Ear, thumb and toe are marked with blood in verse 24, and only then does the oil come. It is the
same at the cleansing of a leper: blood on the ear, thumb and toe, and then *the priest puts some
of the oil on the same ear, thumb, and toe, **over the blood already there***. See the note on
Leviticus 14:10–20.

The oil never touches a man where the blood has not been first. Leviticus is careful about it in
every ceremony where both are used.

## What that means

The Spirit does not come to a person on the strength of their improvement. He comes where the
blood has been applied, and He comes onto that exact spot.

*In Him you also trusted, after you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation; in whom
also, **having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise**.*

Believed first, sealed second, and the sealing is not a separate achievement.

## The garments too

*On Aaron and **his clothes**, and on his sons and **their clothes**.*

Not only the men. What they were wearing was sprinkled as well, and those garments were *for
glory and for beauty*, made by hand out of blue, purple, scarlet and fine linen.

## The three that agree

*And there are three that bear witness on earth: **the Spirit, the water, and the blood**; and
these three agree as one.*

Washing, blood, oil — the three things done to Aaron and his sons in this chapter, in that order.

> **The one verse where they are mixed.** Oil and blood, taken together and sprinkled on the men
> and on what they were wearing — and in every ceremony in the book, the blood goes on first.
