key: Deuteronomy 7:20–24
title: Little by little
updated: 2026-08-09
---
God explains why the conquest will be slow, and the reason is not about the enemy.

## The promise

*"The LORD your God will **clear away these nations before you little by little**."*

The Hebrew is **me'at me'at** — little little. The word doubled, which is how Hebrew says
*gradually*.

## The reason given

*"You may not finish them all at once, **or the wild animals would grow too many for you**."*

Not because the enemy is strong, and not because Israel is failing. Because a country emptied
faster than it can be occupied goes back to the wild.

The slowness is protective, and it is explained rather than merely imposed. See the note on
Exodus 23:27–30.

## What is not slow

*"**He will hand them over to you** and throw them into great confusion until they are
destroyed. He will give their kings into your hand."*

The outcome is not in question anywhere in the paragraph. Only the pace.

## The instruction in the middle

*"**Do not be terrified by them**, for the LORD your God is right there in your midst — a
great and awesome God."*

## What grows at the same rate

*"Until **you have increased**, and you inherit the land."*

The clearing is paced to the growing. They receive ground as fast as they become able to hold
it, and not faster.

*But **grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ**.*

*Being confident of this very thing, that **He who has begun a good work in you will complete
it** until the day of Jesus Christ.*

## What the hornet is

*"The LORD your God will **send the hornet** among them."*

Mentioned three times in the Pentateuch, and never explained. Joshua says it plainly at the
end: *I sent the hornet before you which drove them out… **not with your sword or with your
bow***.

Something went ahead of them that they did not send and could not claim credit for.

> **Why it is slow.** Not resistance, and not discipline. A land cleared faster than it can be
> lived in turns wild — so the pace is set by how much of it they can actually hold.
