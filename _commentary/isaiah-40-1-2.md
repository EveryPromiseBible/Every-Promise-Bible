key: Isaiah 40:1–2
title: Speak to her heart
updated: 2026-08-07

The command is doubled, and it is given to somebody unnamed.

## The word

*Comfort My people. **Comfort them**, says your God.*

The Hebrew is **nachamu nachamu ammi**.

**Nacham** — to comfort, to console. The verb is repeated for emphasis and given in the plural
imperative.

## Who is addressed

Unspecified. Plural.

The chapter opens with an order and does not say who is being ordered.

## The possessive

*My people… **your God**.*

Both halves of the covenant formula appear in the first line, after thirty-nine chapters largely
concerned with judgment.

## The turn in the book

Chapter 39 ends with Hezekiah showing the Babylonian envoys his treasury, and Isaiah telling him
*all that is in your house shall be carried to Babylon*.

Chapter 40 begins here.

## The manner

*Speak tenderly to Jerusalem. **Lean close** and tell her.*

The Hebrew is **dabberu al-lev Yerushalayim** — speak upon the heart of Jerusalem.

## The idiom

**Al-lev** — to the heart.

Used of Shechem speaking to Dinah, of Boaz to Ruth, of a man wooing. It is intimate speech, not
proclamation.

Hosea uses it of God taking Israel to the wilderness *and speaking comfortably to her*.

## The three messages

*That **the hard season is over**.*

The Hebrew is **male tzeva'ah** — her warfare, her hard service, is fulfilled.

**Tzava** — an army, a term of forced service. The word used of the Levites' term of duty.

## The second

*That **her debt is paid in full**.*

The Hebrew is **nirtzah avonah** — her iniquity is accepted, paid off.

**Ratzah** — the same root as **ratzon**, favour. Used of a debt discharged and of a sacrifice
accepted.

## The third

*That she has received from the hand of the LORD **double for all her sins**.*

The Hebrew is **kiflayim** — double, or a folding-over.

## The difficulty in it

Read as punishment, double is more than justice allows.

**Kefel** is also the word for a garment folded in two — the two matching halves. Read that way, it
is not excess but completeness: the payment matches the debt exactly, folded against it.

## What follows

*The voice of one crying in the wilderness: "**Prepare the way of the LORD; make straight in the
desert a highway for our God**."* See the note on Isaiah 40:3–5.

All four Gospels apply that line to John the Baptist, which puts this chapter at the front of every
account of Jesus.

## The rest of the chapter

*All flesh is grass… **but the word of our God stands forever**.* See the note on Isaiah 40:6–8.

*Behold, the Lord GOD comes with power… **He will feed His flock like a shepherd**.* See the note on
Isaiah 40:9–11.

And *those who wait on the LORD shall renew their strength*. See the note on Isaiah 40:29–31.

## Who does the comforting

*For the LORD **has comforted His people**, and He will be tender with His afflicted ones.* See the
note on Isaiah 49:8–13.

The same verb, **nicham**, nine chapters later, in the perfect.

> **The register.** *Speak to her heart* — the Hebrew idiom for how a man speaks to a woman he is
> courting. It is not the vocabulary of a proclamation.
