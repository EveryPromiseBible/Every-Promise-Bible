key: John 2:13–17
title: "The temple cleared"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
The first Passover of the ministry, and He begins it by emptying the courts.

## What He found

*"**Men selling oxen and sheep and doves, and money changers sitting at their tables.**"*

Both trades were necessary and both were legitimate in principle. Pilgrims could not walk animals
from Galilee, and the temple tax had to be paid in Tyrian coin.

The trouble is where they were doing it: the Court of the Gentiles, the only part of the building a
non-Jew was permitted to stand in.

*"Is it not written, '**My house shall be called a house of prayer for all nations**'? But you have
made it a den of thieves."*

**Mark** keeps the last three words of Isaiah's sentence. The one space set aside for the nations was
being used as a market.

## The whip

*"**So He made a whip out of small cords.**"*

**John** alone records it, and records Him making it — which takes time. This is not a flash of
temper.

*"**Take these things away! Do not make My Father's house a house of merchandise!**"*

## What the disciples remembered

*"**Zeal for Your house has eaten Me up.**"*

Psalm 69:9, and **John** notes they thought of it afterwards.

Psalm 69 is quoted more often about the crucifixion than any psalm except 22: the vinegar, the
hatred without cause, the reproaches falling on Him. The disciples reach for a verse from the psalm
of the passion to explain the whip.

*For **even Christ did not please Himself; but as it is written, "The reproaches of those who
reproached You fell on Me."***

**Paul** quotes the next line of the same verse.

## The sign He gives

*"**Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.**"*

They answer that the building took forty-six years, and **John** adds the explanation: *but He was
speaking of the temple of His body.*

The first thing He does at the first Passover is clear a building and then announce His own death and
resurrection as its replacement.

*Do you not know that **you are the temple of God** and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?*

*And **I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple**.*

> **Which court it was.** The one the Gentiles were allowed into — the only part of the building open
> to them — and it had been given over to livestock and currency. Mark keeps the clause the others
> drop: *a house of prayer for all nations*.
