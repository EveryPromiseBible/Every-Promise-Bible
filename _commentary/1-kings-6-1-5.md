The one place the Old Testament dates itself from the exodus, and what it dates is a
foundation being laid.

## The date

*It was **four hundred and eighty years after the people of Israel came up out of the land of
Egypt** — in the fourth year of Solomon's reign — that he began to build the house of the
LORD.*

Scripture almost never counts from the exodus. It counts by kings' reigns, by generations, by
the founding of cities. Here it counts from the night the blood went on the doorposts.

Everything from the Passover to the temple is measured as one span.

## The month

**Ziv** — the second month, the month of brightness, when the spring flowers are out.

## The shape

Sixty cubits by twenty by thirty, with a porch twenty across, and *side rooms ringing the
house on every side*.

Exactly twice the dimensions of the tabernacle. It is the same building, doubled — the tent
Moses was shown on the mountain, in stone.

*Who serve **the copy and shadow of the heavenly things**… "See that you make all things
according to the pattern shown you on the mountain."* See the note on Hebrews 8:6–7.

## What David had wanted to do

Build it. And been told *the LORD will make you a house* instead. See the note on
2 Samuel 7:1–3.

The plans were given to David and the building was done by his son. *All this,* David said,
*the LORD made me understand in writing, by His hand upon me.*

## What is not heard

*The house, when it was being built, was built with stone finished at the quarry, so that
**no hammer or chisel or any iron tool was heard in the house** while it was being built.* See
the note on 1 Kings 5:13–18.

## The house that replaced it

*Jesus answered and said to them, "**Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it
up.**" Then the Jews said, "It has taken forty-six years to build this temple, and will You
raise it up in three days?" **But He was speaking of the temple of His body.***

## And the one being built now

*Do you not know that **you are the temple of God** and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?*

*In whom you also are being built together for **a dwelling place of God in the Spirit**.*

Seven years to build the first. It burned. The second was forty-six years in progress and came
down in a generation. The third is still going up.

> **What the number counts.** Not from a coronation or a conquest. Four hundred and eighty
> years **from the exodus** — from a lamb, a doorpost and a night — to the day the foundation
> of the house was laid.
