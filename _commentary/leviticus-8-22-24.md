Three points of blood on a priest, and each one marks a place where a man makes contact with the
world.

## The animal

*Then Moses presented **the second ram, the ram of ordination**.*

The Hebrew is **eil hammillu'im** — the ram of fillings.

**Millu'im** — from **male**, to fill. The idiom for ordination is *filling the hands*.

## What has come before it

A bull for a sin offering, then a first ram wholly burnt.

This is the third animal of the day, and the only one whose blood goes on a person.

## The identification

*Aaron and his sons **laid their hands on its head**.*

The Hebrew is **samak** — to lean, to press down with weight.

Not a touch. The full weight of the man goes onto the animal.

## The first mark

*Moses took some of its blood and put it on **the lobe of Aaron's right ear**.*

The Hebrew is **tenuk ozen** — the lobe, the extremity.

## What an ear does

Hears. The first faculty consecrated in a priest is the one he receives with.

*Faith comes by hearing.* And before any service is required, the sense that takes instruction in.

## The second

*On **the thumb of his right hand**.*

**Bohen yado** — the thumb.

The digit that makes a hand able to grip. Without it, the hand cannot work.

## The third

*And on **the big toe of his right foot**.*

**Bohen raglo** — the same word for thumb, applied to the foot.

The point a man's weight goes through when he walks and turns.

## The three together

What he listens to, what he handles, where he goes.

Not the heart, not the mind. Three physical extremities, and blood on each.

## The right side

All three are the right. The side of strength and precedence.

## The sons

*He brought Aaron's sons close and **did the same for each of them**.*

The Hebrew is **vayaqrev** — he brought near.

No difference between the high priest and the rest. Each man gets the same three marks.

## The rest of the blood

*Then he **splashed the rest of the blood against the altar on every side**.*

Most of it does not go on the men.

## Where the same three marks appear again

*This shall be the law of the leper for the day of his cleansing… the priest shall put it **on the tip
of the right ear of him who is to be cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of
his right foot***. See the note on Leviticus 14:14–18.

The identical procedure, on a man being brought back from outside the camp.

Blood first, and then oil on top of the blood, in exactly the same three places.

## What that pairing says

A priest being installed and a leper being restored are marked the same way.

## The seven days

*You shall not go outside the door of the tabernacle of meeting for seven days.*

The ordination takes a week, and on the eighth day the glory appears and fire comes out from before
the LORD. See the note on Leviticus 9:23–24.

## Where the priesthood goes

*You are a chosen generation, **a royal priesthood**, a holy nation.* See the note on 1 Peter 2:9.

And *having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience*. See the note on Hebrews 10:22.

The blood in the letter goes on the conscience. In Leviticus it goes on the ear, the hand and the
foot.

> **The three places.** What he hears, what he handles, where he walks. Not one of them is an inward
> faculty — the marks go on the points where a man touches the world.
