They ask for a date, and He tells them the category is wrong.

## The question

*The Pharisees asked Him **when the kingdom of God was going to come**.*

It is the question everybody was asking, and it assumes an event with a date.

## What they were expecting

A visible restoration: Rome out, David's line back, Jerusalem at the centre. The expectation was
not unreasonable — the prophets had said a great deal about it.

## The answer

*"The kingdom of God is **not the kind of thing you can watch for and measure**."*

The Greek is **meta parateraseos** — with observation. The noun belongs to the vocabulary of
watching for omens and signs, and to medical observation of symptoms.

It is a rare word, used once in the New Testament.

## What it rules out

*"**Nobody will be able to say, 'Here it is,' or 'There it is'**."*

The same construction He uses three verses later of false claims: *look here, or look there — do
not go after them*.

Pointing at a place is not how this is located.

## The reason

*"Because the kingdom of God is **already here among you**."*

The Greek is **entos humon estin**.

**Entos** can mean *within* or *among, in the midst of*. The translators have divided over it for
centuries.

## Why *among* fits the sentence

He is speaking to Pharisees, and the same men are told a few chapters later *you are like
whitewashed tombs*.

*Within you* would put the kingdom inside people He has just been in conflict with.

*Among you* points at Himself, standing in front of them, and that is the sense the context
carries.

## What is being claimed

That the thing they are watching the horizon for is in the room.

## What comes immediately after

*"The days will come when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and **you will
not see it**."*

*"For as the lightning that flashes out of one part under heaven shines to the other part… **so
also the Son of Man will be in His day**."*

To the Pharisees: it is here and you cannot point at it. To the disciples: when it comes, nobody
will need to.

Two answers, two audiences, one paragraph.

## Then Noah and Lot

*As it was in the days of Noah… they ate, they drank, they married*, and *likewise as it was in
the days of Lot*.

*Remember Lot's wife.* See the note on Luke 17:26–30.

## What the kingdom looked like in Luke

*The blind see, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed… and **the poor have the gospel preached to
them**.*

And *if I cast out demons with the finger of God, surely the kingdom of God has come upon you*.

Luke keeps attaching the word to things happening in front of people.

## What was said at the start

*"**The Spirit of the LORD is upon Me**, because He has anointed Me to preach the gospel to the
poor."*

*And He began to say to them, "**Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing**."* See the
note on Luke 4:18–21.

**Today**, in the synagogue at Nazareth. The same claim, made at the beginning of the ministry.

## What Paul says it consists of

*For the kingdom of God is **not eating and drinking, but righteousness and peace and joy in the
Holy Spirit**.* See the note on Romans 14:13–16.

Three things that cannot be pointed at either.

> **The word.** **Parateresis** — the watching that looks for signs and symptoms. He tells them
> the kingdom will not show up under that kind of observation, while standing in front of them.
