She asked where he was. The answer she gets is: look at the ground.

## Who is speaking

The chorus — the daughters of Jerusalem, who appear throughout the book as a third voice.

She had asked Him directly. Someone else answers.

## The condition

*If you **do not know**.*

The Hebrew is *if you do not know for yourself*.

It concedes her ignorance without correcting it. Nobody tells her she should have known.

## The title

*Loveliest of women.*

The Hebrew is *the fairest among women* — **hayyafah bannashim**.

She has just said of herself, three verses earlier, *I am dark, but lovely… do not look upon
me, because I am dark, because the sun has tanned me. My mother's sons were angry with me;
they made me the keeper of the vineyards, but my own vineyard I have not kept.*

The name she is given contradicts the one she gave herself.

## The instruction

*Then **follow the tracks of the flock**.*

The Hebrew is *go out by the footsteps of the flock* — **iqvei ha-tson**, the heel-prints.

She wanted a location. She is given a trail.

The flock has been where the shepherd is. The prints in the dust are where they walked
back and forth, and they lead to him because they came from him.

## The second half

*And **pasture your young goats beside the shepherds' tents**.*

She had complained about being *among the flocks of your companions*. See the note on
Song of Songs 1:7.

The answer sends her to exactly that place — the shepherds' encampment — with her own
animals, to graze them there.

Not away from the other flocks. Beside them.

## What she is not told

Where he is.

Nobody in the verse answers the question she asked. She is told what to do while she does
not know.

## What happens next

He speaks. The very next verse is his: *I have compared you, my love, to my filly among
Pharaoh's chariots.*

She takes the instruction and he arrives.

## The pattern in the book

She loses him repeatedly and finds him every time, and never once by working out where he
went.

*I sought him, but I did not find him… the watchmen found me… scarcely had I passed by them,
when I found the one I love.*

## The tracks elsewhere

*He leads me in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.* See the note on
Psalms 23:1–3.

And Peter, of the sufferings of Christ: *leaving us an example, that you should **follow
His steps**.* See the note on 1 Peter 2:21–23.

The Greek there is **ichnesin** — footprints. The same image, the same instruction.

> **The answer to a question about location.** Footprints. She is not told where he is; she
> is told what the ground already shows, and to bring her goats to where the tents are.
