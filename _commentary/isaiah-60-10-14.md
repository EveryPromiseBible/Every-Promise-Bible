A city under reconstruction is told its walls will be built by the people who knocked them down.

## The builders

*Foreigners will **rebuild your walls**, and **their kings will serve you**.*

The Hebrew is **benei-nekar** — sons of the stranger.

The people who broke the walls will build them.

Cyrus, who funded the return. Artaxerxes, who gave Nehemiah timber from the royal forest.

## The two clauses

*In My anger **I struck you**, but in My favor **I have shown you mercy**.*

The Hebrew is **beqitzpi hikkiticha uvirtzoni richamticha**.

Both first person. The prophet does not attribute the destruction to Babylon and the mercy to
God.

## The gates

*Your gates will **stand open always. They will never be shut, day or night**.*

The Hebrew is **yomam valaylah lo yissageru**.

## What a shut gate meant

Security. Every walled city closed its gates at sunset, and a gate left open at night meant either
peace so complete it was unnecessary, or a city that had fallen.

## Why they are open

*So that people may **bring you the wealth of the nations, with their kings led in procession**.*

The Hebrew is **chel goyim** — the strength, the wealth of nations.

The traffic never stops, so the gates cannot.

## The warning

*Any nation that will not serve you will perish.*

The Hebrew is **charov yecheravu** — shall be utterly wasted.

The passage does not soften the alternative.

## The timber

*The glory of Lebanon will come to you, the **pine, the fir, and the cypress together**, to beautify
the place of My sanctuary.*

Three trees. The same forests that supplied Solomon's temple, floated down the coast as rafts.

## The footstool

*I will make **the place of My feet glorious**.*

The Hebrew is **meqom raglai**.

The temple as God's footstool: *the LORD is in His holy temple*, and *let us worship at His
footstool*.

## The reversal

*The children of those who oppressed you **will come and bow before you**. All who once despised you
will **fall down at your feet**.*

## The name

*They will call you **the City of the LORD, Zion of the Holy One of Israel**.*

A city whose name had been *forsaken* and *desolate* gets a new one.

*You shall no longer be termed Forsaken… but you shall be called **Hephzibah** — My delight is in
her.*

## Where the chapter started

*Arise, shine; for your light has come! And the glory of the LORD is risen upon you.*

*For behold, **the darkness shall cover the earth, and deep darkness the people**; but the LORD will
arise over you.* See the note on Isaiah 60:1–3.

## Where it ends

*Instead of bronze I will bring you gold… **you will call your walls Salvation and your gates
Praise**.* See the note on Isaiah 60:15–18.

*The sun shall no longer be your light by day, nor for brightness shall the moon give light to
you; **but the LORD will be to you an everlasting light**.*

## Where the gates end up

*Its gates shall not be shut at all by day (**there shall be no night there**).*

*And they shall bring the glory and the honour of the nations into it.* See the note on
Revelation 21:24–27.

John takes the sentence almost word for word, and adds the reason: there is no night.

> **The gates.** Open day and night because the traffic never stops — said to a city whose walls
> were rubble, about builders who would be foreigners.
