title: "Holiness travelling the other way"

A believer married to an unbeliever, in a city where becoming a Christian could end a marriage. And
Paul's answer contains a sentence that turns the entire Levitical system around.

## "I, not the Lord"

*To the rest I say this — **I, not the Lord**.*

Paul is not downgrading his own authority; two verses from the end of the chapter he says *I think
I also have the Spirit of God*. He is being scrupulous about attribution: Jesus had spoken on
divorce, and Jesus had not spoken on this case, so Paul will not put words in His mouth.

The apostle who wrote most of the New Testament is careful to tell you which sentence is a quotation.

## Do not be the one who leaves

*If any brother has an unbelieving wife and she is willing to go on living with him, **he is not to
divorce her**.*

The instinct in a new convert's household is to separate — to get clean, to remove the compromise.
Paul forbids it. If the unbeliever is content to stay, the believer stays.

Note what he assumes: the danger of contamination runs the way everybody expects, and he is not
worried about it.

## The verb that reverses the law

*For the unbelieving husband is **set apart** through his wife, and the unbelieving wife is set
apart through her husband.*

**Hegiastai** — perfect passive of **hagiazo**, the sanctuary word. Has been made holy, and remains
so.

Now put that beside Haggai, where God has the priests examine exactly this question. *If a man
carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and touches bread or stew with it, does it become
holy?* The priests answer: **no**. Then: *if one who is unclean because of a dead body touches any
of these, does it become unclean?* And the priests answer: **yes**.

That is the law's direction of travel. Holiness does not transmit. Defilement does. Which is why
the clean thing in Israel spent its life avoiding contact.

Then a Man reached out and touched a leper — the one act guaranteed to make Him unclean — and *the
leprosy left him immediately*. A woman with a twelve-year haemorrhage, unclean by every rule and
disqualifying everything she touched, took hold of His hem and *the fountain of her blood was dried
up*. He put His hand on a coffin and the boy sat up.

Every time, contact happened. Every time, it went the wrong way for the rules and the right way for
the person.

> **Which way it flows now.** In your house you are not the vulnerable one. What you carry is more
> contagious than what they carry, because the holiness in you is not yours — *of Him you are in
> Christ Jesus, who became for us wisdom from God, and righteousness and sanctification and
> redemption*. That is why the children of such a marriage are called holy, and why Paul's counsel
> to the believing spouse is simply: stay.
