A set of building instructions, and almost every detail in them is doing two jobs.

## The pitch

*Coat it with **pitch, inside and out**.*

The Hebrew is **kaphar** — and this is the only place in Scripture it is used of
tar. Everywhere else it is the word for **atonement**: to cover, to make
propitiation.

It is the root of **kippur**, as in Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement. The noun
**kopher** is the ransom price — the word Elihu uses in Job 33: *I have found a
ransom.* See the note on Job 33:23–24.

The one thing keeping the water out is called covering, inside and out.

## The measurements

*300 cubits long, 50 wide, and 30 high.*

Roughly 450 feet by 75 by 45 — a displacement of some 43,000 tons, and proportions
close to what modern naval architecture uses for stability in heavy seas. It is not
built to sail anywhere. It is built to stay upright and float.

*Lower, middle, and upper decks.* Three levels.

## The door

*Set **a door in its side**.*

One. Not one per deck, not one at each end. A single door, and it is in the side.

*"I am the door,"* Jesus said. *"If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved."* See
the note on John 10:7–10.

And what was opened in His side was opened with a spear, after He was dead —
*immediately blood and water came out.*

## The window

*Put a roof on it, **finished to a cubit from the top**.*

An opening at the very top, running round under the roof line. The only light comes
from above, and there is no way to see the water from inside.

Whoever was in that boat could look up and could not look out.

## "But with you I will establish my covenant"

The first occurrence of **covenant** in the Bible.

It comes before the flood, not after — while the ark is still timber and the sky is
still dry.

## Who comes in

*"You and your sons, your wife, and your sons' wives."*

Eight people, on the strength of one man's standing with God. Nothing is recorded
about the faith of the other seven.

And the animals *will come to you* — Noah is not told to go and catch them.

## The last line

*Noah did everything just as God commanded him — **every last thing**.*

The Hebrew simply repeats it: *thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded
him, so he did.*

A hundred and twenty years of building, on a warning about weather that had never
happened.

> **What is not in the instructions.** A rudder, a sail, an oar, or a destination.
> The ark has no means of steering and nowhere to go. It was built to be shut into
> and carried.
