The shortest sentence He ever said about Himself, and the crowd reached for stones
before He finished.

## What they asked

*"**You are not yet fifty years old**, and You have seen Abraham?"*

He had just said *Abraham rejoiced to see My day, and he saw it and was glad.*

Their objection is about arithmetic. Two thousand years and one lifetime do not
overlap.

## The answer

*"I tell you the truth, **before Abraham was, I am**."*

The tenses do not agree, and that is the whole sentence. Not *I was*. Not *I
existed*. **I am.**

Before Abraham came into being — the Greek verb is one of becoming, of coming to
exist — I **am**.

## What He used

*Ego eimi.* It is the Greek of Exodus 3, where God tells Moses to say *I AM has
sent me to you.*

The name given at the burning bush, in the present tense, applied to Himself in the
temple courts.

See the note on Exodus 3:13–15.

## What they did about it

*At that they **picked up stones** to throw at Him.*

There was no misunderstanding. Stoning was the penalty for blasphemy, and nobody in
that court had to have it explained.

The chapter's earlier claims — that He was sent, that He does the Father's will,
that they would die in their sins — had not produced this. This did.

## The seven "I am"s in John

Bread of life. Light of the world. The door. The good shepherd. The resurrection
and the life. The way, the truth and the life. The true vine.

Seven with something after them, and this one, with nothing after it at all.

## What He walked into

*But Jesus **hid Himself and went out of the temple, passing right through the
middle of them**.*

Through the crowd holding the stones. John does not explain it and does not
comment.

He does the same in Luke 4, when Nazareth takes Him to the brow of the hill to
throw Him off — *passing through the midst of them, He went His way.*

They could not lay a hand on Him until the night He let them, and then in the
garden, when the soldiers said they were looking for Jesus of Nazareth and He
answered *I am*, they *drew back and fell to the ground.*

> **The name and what it does not specify.** *I AM WHO I AM* — the sentence has no
> predicate. It is not *I am strong* or *I am merciful*. It is the verb standing
> alone, and whatever the need in front of it, the name is large enough.
