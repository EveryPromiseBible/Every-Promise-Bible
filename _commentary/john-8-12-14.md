The second of the seven **I am** sayings, and the setting supplies the picture.

## When He says it

*"**Then Jesus spoke to them again.**"*

The preceding scene is the woman taken in adultery — *neither do I condemn you* — and before that,
the Feast of Tabernacles.

During Tabernacles, four enormous lampstands were lit in the Court of the Women, each with four
golden bowls, and the rabbis said the light reached every courtyard in Jerusalem. The treasury,
where **John** says this was spoken, is in that court.

He stands under the extinguished lamps of the feast and says it.

## The claim

*"**I am the light of the world.**"*

**Ego eimi to phos tou kosmou.**

The **ego eimi** is the name from the burning bush, and **John** records Him using it seven times with
a predicate and several times without — *before Abraham was, I AM*, which is the sentence they pick
up stones for in the same chapter.

## The two clauses attached

*"**Whoever follows Me will never walk in the dark, but will have the light of life.**"*

Not *will see the light*. Will **have** it.

*In Him was life, and **the life was the light of men**.*

*For it is the God who commanded light to shine out of darkness who has shone in our hearts to give
**the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ**.*

## The world

**Tou kosmou** — not of Israel.

*"A light to bring revelation to the Gentiles, and the glory of Your people Israel."*

Simeon, in **Luke** 2, holding a baby.

*Arise, shine; for your light has come!… **The Gentiles shall come to your light**, and kings to the
brightness of your rising.*

## The objection

*"**You are testifying about Yourself. Your testimony is not valid.**"*

A fair point under the law: *by the mouth of two or three witnesses the matter shall be
established.*

*"**Even if I bear witness of Myself, My witness is true, for I know where I came from and where I am
going.**"*

And then, four verses on, He supplies the second witness: *I am One who bears witness of Myself, and
the Father who sent Me bears witness of Me.*

## And the sequel

*Now as **Jesus** passed by, He saw a man who was blind from birth… "**As long as I am in the world,
I am the light of the world.**" When He had said these things, He spat on the ground.* See the note
on John 9:1–5.

**John** puts the claim and then, in the next chapter, has Him give a man his eyes.

> **Where He was standing.** In the Court of the Women at Tabernacles, under four huge lampstands
> that were said to light every courtyard in the city — and He said *I am the light of the world*.
