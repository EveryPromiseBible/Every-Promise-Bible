The fifth day, and two new words appear for the first time.

## The command

*God said, "**Let the waters swarm with living things**, and let birds fly across the sky."*

The Hebrew is **yishretzu hammayim sheretz nephesh chayyah** — let the waters swarm with swarms of
living soul.

**Sharatz** — to teem, to swarm. The verb and its noun together, which Hebrew does for emphasis.

## The new phrase

**Nephesh chayyah** — living soul, living creature.

Its first appearance in the Bible.

The plants of day three were not given it. From here on it is used of animals, and in chapter 2 of
the man: *and man became a **living soul***.

## The new verb

*So **God made the great creatures of the sea**.*

The Hebrew is **vayyivra** — and God created.

**Bara** — to create. Used three times in the chapter: at the beginning, here, and of humanity.

Everything else is **asah** — made, or **amar** — said.

The three uses mark the three thresholds: matter, animal life, and human life.

## The great creatures

*The **great creatures of the sea**.*

The Hebrew is **hattanninim haggedolim** — the great sea monsters.

**Tannin** — the word used elsewhere of Leviathan and of Rahab, the chaos monsters of surrounding
mythology.

Israel's neighbours told stories in which the gods fought these creatures to make the world.
Genesis lists them as things God made on a Thursday.

## The verdict

*And **God saw that it was good**.*

## The blessing

*Then **He blessed them**: "Have young, multiply, fill the seas and the skies."*

The Hebrew is **vayvarech otam Elohim** — and God blessed them.

The first blessing in the Bible, and it is pronounced over fish and birds.

## What the blessing consists of

**Peru urevu umil'u** — be fruitful and multiply and fill.

The same three verbs given to humanity two days later, and to Noah after the flood.

## What is blessed

Not the light, not the sky, not the dry land, not the plants.

The first thing God blesses is the first thing that can reproduce itself.

## The order of the days

Day one: light. Day four: lights.

Day two: sea and sky. Day five: fish and birds.

Day three: dry land and plants. Day six: land animals and man.

Three days of forming, three days of filling, and the pairs line up.

## The refrain

*Evening came, then morning — **the fifth day**.*

The Hebrew is **vayehi-erev vayehi-voqer** — and there was evening and there was morning.

Evening first. The Jewish day still begins at sunset.

## What day six adds

*Then God said, "**Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness**."*

*So God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; **male and female He
created them**.* See the note on Genesis 1:26–31.

**Bara** three times in one verse.

## The blessing repeated

*Then God blessed them, and God said to them, "**Be fruitful and multiply; fill the earth and
subdue it**."*

The same words as the fish, with two additions.

> **The first blessing in the Bible.** Not over light, or land, or the sky — over fish and birds,
> the first things made that could make more of themselves.
