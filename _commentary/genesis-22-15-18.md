The voice comes a second time, and this one is an oath.

## The second call

*The angel of the LORD called to Abraham from heaven **a second time**.*

The first stopped the knife. See the note on Genesis 22:11–12.

This one comes after the ram has been found in the thicket and offered, and after the
place has been named.

## The oath

*"**I swear by myself**, declares the LORD."*

The only time in Genesis God does this.

He has nothing higher to swear by, so He stakes Himself. Hebrews makes the whole
argument out of that one clause. See the note on Hebrews 6:13–14.

## The reason given

*"**Because you have done this** and **have not held back your son, your only son**."*

The same phrase as verse 12, repeated word for word.

**Have not held back** — the Hebrew is **chasak**, to withhold, to spare.

Paul turns it around in Romans: *He who **did not spare** His own Son, but delivered Him
up for us all.*

## The blessing

*"**I will surely bless you**."*

The Hebrew doubles the verb — *blessing I will bless you*. Hebrews carries it straight
over into Greek. See the note on Hebrews 6:13–14.

## The two measures

*"I will make your descendants **as many as the stars in the sky** and **as the sand on
the seashore**."*

Both had been used before — the stars in Genesis 15, when Abram was taken outside at
night, and the dust of the earth in Genesis 13.

Here both, together, at the end of the hardest thing he ever did.

## The gates

*"Your descendants will **possess the gates of their enemies**."*

The gate was where a city's business was done and where it was defended. To hold the gate
is to hold the city.

## The last promise

*"And **through your offspring all nations on earth will be blessed**."*

This is the clause that reaches past Israel. It was in the first call, in Genesis 12, and
it is repeated here at the end.

Paul reads it precisely: *He does not say, "And to seeds," as of many, but as of one,
"And to your Seed," **who is Christ**.*

## What it rests on

*"**Because you have obeyed my voice**."*

Stated twice in the paragraph — *because you have done this*, and *because you have
obeyed*.

The covenant was given free in Genesis 12 and 15, and Genesis 22 attaches an oath to it,
after a mountain.

## Where the mountain was

*The land of Moriah.* 2 Chronicles: *Solomon began to build the house of the LORD at
Jerusalem **on Mount Moriah**.*

## The name Abraham gave the place

**Jehovah Jireh** — *the LORD will provide*. And Moses adds the saying still current when
he wrote: *in the Mount of the LORD it shall be provided.*

Future tense, in a place where provision had just happened.

> **What is sworn and what it is sworn on.** Descendants, gates, and blessing for all
> nations — and God puts His own name behind it, on a hill, next to an altar with a ram
> on it.
