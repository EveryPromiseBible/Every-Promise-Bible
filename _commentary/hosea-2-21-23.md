A chain of answers running from heaven down to a name, and the last link is a renaming.

## The chain

*"I will answer, says the LORD. **I will answer the skies, and the skies will answer the earth,
and the earth will answer with grain and wine and oil, and all of it will answer Jezreel**."*

The Hebrew verb **anah** — to answer, to respond — occurs five times in one sentence.

Each thing in the chain answers the one above it. God answers the sky; the sky answers the ground;
the ground answers with harvest; the harvest answers a person's name.

Nothing in the chain is asked to produce anything. Each is described as replying.

## What that reverses

Earlier in the chapter God had said *I will take back My grain in its time, and My new wine in
its season*, and *she never understood that it was I who gave her the grain, the new wine, the
oil.*

The three commodities she credited to her lovers are the three that come down the chain.

## The name

*"I will **plant her for Myself in the land**."*

The Hebrew is **uzra'tiha li** — and I will sow her for Myself. It is the verb inside the name
**Jezreel**, which means *God sows*.

The child named as a judgement about a massacre becomes the word for planting a people. See the
note on Hosea 1:11.

## The two names undone

*"**I will love the one called Not-Loved.** I will say to the one called Not-My-People, '**You are
My people**.'"*

**Lo-Ruhamah** and **Lo-Ammi**, the second and third children. Both names are cancelled by having
the negative removed.

## Who quotes it

*As He says also in Hosea: "**I will call them My people, who were not My people, and her beloved,
who was not beloved**."*

**Romans** 9, and **Paul** applies it to Gentiles — people to whom the covenant formula had never
been said in the first place.

*Who once were not a people but are now the people of God, **who had not obtained mercy but now
have obtained mercy**.*

**Peter** takes both names too, in a letter to churches scattered across Asia Minor, and puts them
in a paragraph that begins *you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood.*

## The reply

*"And **he will say, 'You are my God.'**"*

The last link in the chain. The renaming produces an answer, and it is the other half of the
covenant formula — the clause verse 9 had cancelled.

*And **because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying out,
"Abba, Father!"***

**Galatians** 4, and the crying out is not the ground of the sonship. It is the evidence of it,
and the Spirit is the one doing it.

> **The direction of the chain.** God answers the sky, the sky answers the earth, the earth answers
> with harvest, the harvest answers a name — and only at the very bottom, after five answers
> coming down, does a person say anything back.
