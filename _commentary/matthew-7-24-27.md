---
key: Matthew 7:24–27
title: "Two houses, one storm"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
The end of the Sermon on the Mount, and the two men differ in exactly one respect.

## What is identical

Both hear the same words. Both build. Both houses stand until the weather arrives. Both get the
same storm — *the rain came down, the rivers rose, the winds blew*, twice, in the same words.

**Matthew** repeats the weather clause verbatim so that nobody can locate the difference there.

## What differs

*"Everyone who hears these words of Mine **and does them**."*

*"Everyone who hears these words of Mine **and does not do them**."*

And underneath: **rock**, or **sand**.

## The foundation

*For **no other foundation can anyone lay than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ**.*

**1 Corinthians** 3, and **Paul** says the foundation is not chosen by the builder. It is already
laid, and the only question is what goes on it.

*Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: "**Behold, I lay in Zion a stone for a foundation, a tried stone,
a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation**. Whoever believes will not act hastily."*

Isaiah 28, quoted by **Paul** and by **Peter**, and **Peter** adds: *he who believes on Him will by
no means be put to shame.*

## The doing

The parable turns on doing, and the sermon has just finished saying that mighty works do not
qualify anybody. See the note on Matthew 7:21–23.

So the question is what the doing is.

*Then they said to Him, "What shall we do, that we may work the works of God?" **Jesus** answered
and said to them, "**This is the work of God, that you believe in Him whom He sent**."*

*"**This is the will of Him who sent Me, that everyone who sees the Son and believes in Him may have
everlasting life.**"*

**John** 6 asks the same question twice and gets a singular noun both times.

## The digging

*"He is like a man building a house, who **dug deep and laid the foundation on the rock**."*

**Luke**'s version adds the excavation. The rock is not on the surface; the difference between the
two men is invisible from the road, and both houses look finished.

## The collapse

*"And down it went — and **its collapse was complete**."*

The Greek is **kai en he ptosis autes megale** — and great was its fall.

**Matthew** ends the Sermon on that word, and the next sentence is the crowd's reaction:

*And so it was, when Jesus had ended these sayings, that **the people were astonished at His
teaching, for He taught them as one having authority**, and not as the scribes.*

## What the storm is not

A test of construction quality. Both men built, both worked, and the rain is not sent at one of
them.

*Therefore, since we are receiving **a kingdom which cannot be shaken**, let us have grace.*

> **What the parable will not let you locate the difference in.** Not the hearing, not the
> building, not the weather — Matthew repeats the storm clause word for word. It is under the
> house, where nobody can see it from outside.
