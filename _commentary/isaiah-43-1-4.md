Four hazards are named, and none of them is cancelled.

## The opening

*But now.*

The Hebrew is **ve'attah**.

The chapter before ended in judgment: *so He poured on him the fury of His anger… it burned him,
yet he did not take it to heart*.

The two words are a turn.

## The two verbs

*He who **created** you, O Jacob, He who **formed** you, O Israel.*

The Hebrew is **bora'aka** and **yotzerka**.

**Bara** — the Genesis 1 word, used only of God. **Yatzar** — the potter's word, the one used of
Adam being shaped from dust.

## The two names

Jacob and Israel. The name he was born with and the one he was given at the Jabbok. See the note on
Genesis 32:27–32.

Both are used, in the same breath.

## The first command

*Do not be afraid, **for I have redeemed you**.*

The Hebrew is **ge'altika** — I have acted as **go'el**, the kinsman-redeemer.

## The claim

*I have **called you by name**. **You are Mine**.*

The Hebrew is **li-attah** — to Me, you.

Two words.

## The waters

*When you **pass through the waters**, I will be with you.*

The Hebrew is **ki-ta'avor** — when, not if.

## What is promised

Not that there are no waters. Company in them.

## The rivers

*When you cross the rivers, **they will not sweep over you**.*

The Hebrew is **lo yishtefuka** — they will not overflow you.

The Red Sea, and the Jordan. Both crossed on foot.

## The fire

*When you walk through the fire, **you will not be burned**. The flames will not set you ablaze.*

The Hebrew is **lo tikkaveh** — you will not be scorched.

## The men who did

*"I see four men, loose, walking about in the middle of the fire, **and they are not harmed**."* See
the note on Daniel 3:24–25.

*The smell of fire was not on them.*

## The word repeated four times

**Through.**

Not around, not instead of. The waters, the rivers, the fire and the flame are all still there.

## The reason

*For I am the LORD your God, **the Holy One of Israel, your Savior**.*

## The exchange

*I gave **Egypt for your ransom**, Cush and Seba in your place.*

The Hebrew is **kofreka** — your ransom, your covering price.

## What that refers to

Nations given in place of one people.

Whatever the historical reference, the sentence sets a scale: whole countries priced against them.

## The reason underneath the reason

*Because you are **precious in My eyes**, and **honored**, and **I love you**.*

The Hebrew is **yaqarta be'einai** — you are costly in My eyes. **Nikbadta** — you are weighty,
honoured, from **kavod**, glory.

## The word

**Yaqar** — precious, rare, costly.

The same adjective in Psalm 116: *precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of His saints*.

## The repetition

*I will give **people in exchange for you**, nations in exchange for your life.*

The exchange is stated twice, before and after the reason.

## What the chapter goes on to say

*"I, even I, am He who **blots out your transgressions** for My own sake; and I will not remember
your sins."* See the note on Isaiah 43:25.

And *"behold, I will do a new thing… **I will even make a road in the wilderness and rivers in the
desert**."* See the note on Isaiah 43:18–19.

> **The preposition.** Through the waters, through the rivers, through the fire. Nothing in the
> promise removes any of the four.
