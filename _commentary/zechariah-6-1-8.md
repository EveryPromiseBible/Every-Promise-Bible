The eighth and last of Zechariah's night visions, and it answers the first one.

## Where it sits

Zechariah 1 to 6 is a single night — eight visions in sequence, given to a prophet in Jerusalem
among people rebuilding a ruined temple.

The first vision was a man among myrtle trees with horses behind him — red, sorrel, and white —
who report: *we have walked to and fro throughout the earth, and behold, all the earth is
resting quietly*.

And the angel's protest: *O LORD of hosts, how long will You not have mercy on Jerusalem?*

This is the last vision, and the horses come back.

## The gate

*Four chariots coming out **from between two mountains, mountains of bronze**.*

The Hebrew is **harei nechoshet**.

Bronze in the tabernacle is the metal of the altar and of judgment. Two mountains of it make a
gateway that nothing gets through uninvited.

Some take the two mountains as Zion and Olivet with the Kidron between them.

## The colours

*The first chariot had **red** horses, the second **black**, the third **white**, and the fourth
**dappled**, all of them powerful.*

Four teams, and the colours overlap with Revelation 6 without matching. Red, black, white — and
then dappled where John has the corpse-coloured horse. See the note on
Revelation 6:1–2.

## The question

*What are these, my lord?*

Zechariah asks it repeatedly through the eight visions, and the angel always answers. It is the
pattern of the whole section.

## The answer

*These are **the four spirits of heaven**, going out **from standing before the Lord of the
whole earth**.*

The Hebrew is **arba ruchot hashamayim** — and **ruach** is spirit, wind, or breath.

Most translations choose *spirits*; some choose *winds*, and the four winds of heaven is a
standing phrase.

**From standing before the Lord** — they have been waiting in attendance. They are dispatched,
not loose.

## The directions

*The black horses go **toward the north country**, the white horses **toward the west**, and the
dappled horses **toward the south country**.*

The north is Babylon. Every invasion came down that way, and the exile went out that way.

The south is Egypt.

The red team's destination is not given, which has puzzled readers for centuries.

## The eagerness

*When the powerful horses went out, they were **eager to range back and forth across the
earth**.*

The Hebrew is **vayevaqshu** — they sought, they were impatient to go.

*And he said, "**Go, walk through the earth**." So they walked through the earth.*

They wait for the word, and then go.

## The report

*Look, those going toward the north country have **given My Spirit rest in the land of the
north**.*

The Hebrew is **heniychu et-ruchi** — caused My Spirit to rest.

The verb is the one behind **menuchah**, rest, and behind Noah's name.

Babylon is dealt with, and the word for it is that something has settled.

## What answers what

Vision one: the horses report the whole earth at rest, and the angel asks *how long?*

Vision eight: the chariots go out, and the report is that God's own Spirit is at rest in the
north.

The night that began with an unanswered *how long* ends with a quieting.

## What immediately follows

The crowning of Joshua the high priest — *behold, the Man whose name is the BRANCH… He shall
build the temple of the LORD, and **He shall be a priest on His throne**.* See the note on
Zechariah 6:9–15.

A priest on a throne, which the law kept separate.

## The other night vision in the set

Joshua the high priest in filthy garments, with Satan at his right hand to oppose him, and the
order: *take away the filthy garments from him… I have removed your iniquity from you, and I
will clothe you with rich robes*. See the note on Zechariah 3:1–5.

> **Where the chariots come from.** Out from between two mountains of bronze, after standing in
> attendance — and the last word of the vision is that something has been put at rest.
