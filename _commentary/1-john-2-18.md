John tells them the hour is late and there are counterfeits about — and then tells them why they are
in no danger from either fact.

## Little children

*Little children, we are in **the final hour**.*

**Paidia** — small children. John is an old man by now, the last of the Twelve, and this is how he
speaks to grown congregations for most of the letter.

**Eschate hora** — the last hour. It has been running for two thousand years, which tells you what
John means by it. He is not measuring a duration; he is naming a character. The final period of
history is defined by what is in it, not by how long it takes.

## The word he coined

**Antichristos** occurs nowhere in the Bible except John's letters, and the prefix carries two senses
at once. **Anti** means *against*, and it means *in place of*.

Both are true of the thing he is describing. It opposes Christ, and it offers itself as a
substitute — which is always the more dangerous half, because opposition is visible and replacement
is not.

And John immediately democratises the term: *right now there are **many** antichrists*. Not one
figure at the end. A spirit already at work, and his test for it comes four chapters later — anyone
who will not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh.

## The wordplay two verses later

Here is the thing that makes this paragraph a comfort rather than a warning. Against the
**antichristoi**, John sets the **chrisma**:

*But **you have an anointing** from the Holy One, and you know all things… the anointing which you
have received from Him abides in you, and you do not need that anyone teach you.*

The same root. **Christos** means the Anointed One; **chrisma** is the anointing itself. Those who
are against the Anointed are met by people who have the anointing.

## What he does not tell them to do

He does not tell them to study the counterfeits, watch for them, or become expert in what is wrong.
He tells them what they already have and says it abides.

*They went out from us, but they were not of us* — and John's confidence about who belongs is not
based on a screening process. The ones who are His stay because of what is in them, not because they
identified every fraud in time.

> **The last hour, and you are not on your own in it.** John's answer to a world full of substitutes
> is not vigilance but possession: an anointing from the Holy One, received and abiding. You are not
> asked to out-argue the antichrists. You are told that the Anointed One has already put His
> anointing in you, and it stays.
