The last clause of a long signed agreement, and it is the sentence everything else in the list
is for.

## What this document is

A written covenant, sealed by the priests, the Levites and the leaders after the reading of
the Law and the great confession of chapter 9 — *and because of all this, we make a sure
covenant and write it*.

It is voluntary. Nobody imposes it, and the signatures are listed by name.

## What they undertake

The first of the ground meal, the contributions, the fruit of every tree, the new wine and
oil, the tithe of the soil — brought to the storerooms of the house of God.

Firsts, not leftovers.

## The tithe of the tithe

*And **the Levites will bring up a tenth of the tithes** to the house of our God.*

The tribe with no land, living on what everybody else brings, tithes on that in turn — and the
law says which tenth: *the best of it*.

Nobody in the arrangement is exempt from giving out of what they were given.

## Why it is spelled out in such detail

Because of what happens in chapter 13. Nehemiah goes back to Babylon, comes home, and finds
the storerooms let to Tobiah and the Levites gone back to their fields because nothing was
coming in. See the note on Nehemiah 13:10–14.

The agreement here is exactly the one broken there, and the book records both.

## The last sentence

*"**We will not neglect the house of our God.**"*

The Hebrew is **lo na'azov** — we will not forsake, will not abandon, will not leave it.

## Where the house is now

*Do you not know that **you are the temple of God** and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?*

*In whom the whole building, being fitted together, grows into **a holy temple in the Lord**.*

**Paul** relocates the house into people, and then uses the same verb Nehemiah's covenant
uses: *not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as is the manner of some.*

## What the giving is answering

*And **God is able to make all grace abound toward you**, that you, always having all
sufficiency in all things, may have an abundance for every good work.*

*So let each one give as he purposes in his heart, not grudgingly or of necessity, for **God
loves a cheerful giver**.* See the note on 2 Corinthians 9:6–7.

Both of **Paul**'s sentences put the supply before the giving, which is the order Nehemiah's
document assumes without arguing for it — every item on the list is a **first** of something
already received.

> **The clause it ends on.** Not a total, a rate or a schedule. *We will not neglect the house
> of our God* — and chapter 13 is the record of what it looked like when they did.
