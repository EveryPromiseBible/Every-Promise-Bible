The first creatures to acknowledge the new thing are the ones nobody would choose.

## Who honours Him

*The **wild animals honour Me, the jackals and the owls**.*

The Hebrew is **chayyat hassadeh tannim uvenot ya'anah** — the beast of the field, jackals, and
daughters of the ostrich.

## The two named

**Tannim** — jackals. Scavengers, associated in the prophets with ruins and desolation: *Hazor
shall be a dwelling for jackals*.

**Benot ya'anah** — literally *daughters of greed*, usually translated ostriches or owls. Listed
among the unclean birds in Leviticus.

## Why them

Both are creatures of waste places, and both are ceremonially unclean.

Of everything that could be named as giving God honour, the passage picks the animals that live
where nothing else does.

## The reason

*Because **I give water in the wilderness and rivers in the desert**.*

Repeating the previous verse word for word. See the note on Isaiah 43:18–19.

The jackals honour Him because water arrived in the one place they live.

## What they do that Israel does not

The chapter goes on: *but you have not called upon Me, O Jacob… you have not brought Me the sheep
for your burnt offerings*.

The unclean animals acknowledge it; the people who were given the covenant have not.

The comparison is not stated, and the ordering makes it.

## The purpose

*To **give drink to My people, My chosen**.*

The Hebrew is **ammi vechiri** — My people, My chosen one.

The water was not for the jackals. They benefit from something aimed elsewhere.

## The forming

*The people **I formed for Myself**.*

The Hebrew is **am-zu yatzarti li** — this people I formed for Myself.

**Yatzar** — the potter's verb. The word used of God forming man from the dust, and of forming
Jeremiah in the womb. See the note on Jeremiah 1:4–5.

## The purpose clause

*That they might **declare My praise**.*

The Hebrew is **tehillati yesapperu** — My praise they shall recount.

**Tehillah** — praise. The root behind **Tehillim**, the Hebrew name for the book of Psalms.

**Saphar** — to count, to recount, to tell.

## What that says about the forming

The people were formed for a purpose, and the purpose is stated as speech.

Not service, obedience, or conquest. Telling.

## What Peter does with it

*But you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, His own special people, **that
you may proclaim the praises of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvellous light**.*
See the note on 1 Peter 2:9–10.

Peter assembles the phrase out of Isaiah and Exodus and applies it to scattered Gentile
congregations.

## What the chapter opened with

*"Fear not, for I have redeemed you; **I have called you by your name; you are Mine**."*

*"Since you were precious in My sight, you have been honoured, and **I have loved you**."* See the
note on Isaiah 43:1–3.

## What the chapter ends with

*"**I, even I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake**; and I will not remember
your sins."*

The Hebrew is **lema'ani** — for My own sake.

The stated reason for the blotting out is not their worthiness.

> **The order.** Jackals and owls honour Him for the water; the people it was drawn for have not
> called on Him. The passage puts the two facts next to each other and says nothing.
