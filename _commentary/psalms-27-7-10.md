The middle of the psalm, where the confidence of the opening turns into asking.

## What the heart says

*My heart says of You, **"Seek His face." Your face, O LORD, I will seek.**"*

The invitation and the answer in one verse. He reports the prompting and then agrees with it
out loud.

*"**No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him.**"*

**Jesus** locates the first move where this verse does — the seeking begins as something said
to him.

## The three requests

*Do not hide Your face. Do not turn Your servant away in anger. **Do not cast me off, do not
forsake me**.*

All three are negatives. He is not asking for anything to be added; he is asking for nothing to
be taken away.

## The reason he gives

*"**For You have been my help.**"*

Past tense. The whole ground of the request is what has already happened.

*He who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, **how shall He not with Him
also freely give us all things?***

**Paul** reasons in the same direction — from the larger thing already done to the smaller thing
being asked.

## The verse that gets quoted on its own

*Though **my father and mother leave me**, the LORD will gather me in.*

The Hebrew for *gather* is **asaph** — to take in, to bring into the house. It is what you do
with a harvest, and with a traveller at nightfall.

*"**I will not leave you orphans; I will come to you.**"*

**Jesus** says it in the upper room, hours before leaving. The Greek is **orphanous**.

*For you did not receive the spirit of bondage again to fear, but **you received the Spirit of
adoption by whom we cry out, "Abba, Father."***

## What the psalm ends on

*Wait on the LORD; **be of good courage, and He shall strengthen your heart**. Wait, I say, on
the LORD.*

Said twice in one verse. The psalm that opens *the LORD is my light and my salvation, whom
shall I fear* ends by telling itself to wait.

> **The word for what He does.** **Asaph** — gathers in, the way a harvest is brought under
> cover before dark. The verse is not about replacement. It is about somewhere to be taken.
