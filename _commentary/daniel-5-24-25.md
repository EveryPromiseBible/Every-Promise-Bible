Four words on a wall, and the second of them is the one everybody remembers.

## The hand

*"So **the hand was sent from Him**, and this writing was set down."*

The Aramaic is **min-qodamohi shelich pissa di-yeda** — from before Him was sent the palm of a
hand.

Not a message delivered. A hand, on its own, writing on plaster in a banqueting hall while a
thousand nobles watch.

## What the king was doing

Drinking from the vessels taken out of the temple in Jerusalem, and *they praised the gods of gold
and silver, bronze and iron, wood and stone.*

The metals are the statue's, in order. Belshazzar is toasting Nebuchadnezzar's dream.

## The words

*"**MENE, MENE, TEKEL, PARSIN.**"*

Three of them are weights — a mina, a shekel, and a half. It reads like a price list, which is
part of why nobody in the room could do anything with it.

Daniel reads them as verbs.

## Numbered

*"**MENE**: God has numbered your kingdom, and finished it."*

**Menah** — counted, reckoned. Said twice.

## Weighed

*"**TEKEL**: you have been **weighed in the balances, and found wanting**."*

**Teqiltah** — you were weighed. **Chassir** — lacking, short.

*For **all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God**.*

**Paul**'s verb is **hysterountai** — they come up short, they are lacking. It is Daniel's word in
Greek, applied to everybody.

And then, without a break in the sentence: *being **justified freely by His grace** through the
redemption that is in **Christ Jesus**.*

Belshazzar is weighed and found short, and there is nothing after it. **Romans** 3 weighs the whole
world and finds it short, and the next clause is *freely*.

## Divided

*"**PERES**: your kingdom has been divided and given to the Medes and Persians."*

A pun. **Peres** is *divided*, and it is also *Persia*.

## What the chapter says he knew

*"And you, Belshazzar, his son, **have not humbled your heart, though you knew all this**."*

Nebuchadnezzar published his own madness as a public proclamation. The document existed. Daniel's
charge is not ignorance.

## The other time a finger writes about sin

*But **Jesus stooped down and wrote on the ground with His finger**, as though He did not hear
them… And again He stooped down and wrote on the ground.*

*"He who is without sin among you, let him throw a stone at her first."… **Being convicted by their
conscience, they went out one by one**, beginning with the oldest.*

**John** 8. A finger writing, an unrecorded text, and a room emptying — and the person the writing
was about is the one left standing.

*"**Neither do I condemn you**; go and sin no more."*

Two occasions in Scripture where writing appears from a hand nobody expected. In one, a king is
weighed and found short that night. In the other, the accusers leave and the accused is told she
is not condemned.

*There is therefore now **no condemnation** to those who are in Christ Jesus.*

## What Daniel refuses first

*"Let your gifts be for yourself, and give your rewards to another; **yet I will read the writing
to the king**."*

He turns down the purple, the gold chain and the third place in the kingdom, and then reads it
anyway. He is given all three afterwards, and that night the king is killed.

> **The word.** *Tekel* — weighed, and found lacking. Romans 3 uses the Greek of it about
> everybody, and puts *justified freely by His grace* in the same sentence. Belshazzar's verse has
> no such clause after it.
