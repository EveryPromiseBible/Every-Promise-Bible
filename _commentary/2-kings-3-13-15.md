Three kings with an army and no water, and the prophet asks for a harp.

## The situation

Moab has rebelled. Three kings march against it — Jehoram of Israel, Jehoshaphat of Judah, and
the king of Edom.

They take a seven-day march through the wilderness of Edom and *there was no water for the army*.

*"Alas! For the LORD has called these three kings together to deliver them into the hand of
Moab."*

## The question

Jehoshaphat: *"**Is there no prophet of the LORD here, that we may enquire of the LORD by him?**"*

A servant answers: *Elisha the son of Shaphat is here, **who poured water on the hands of Elijah***.

The description is of a servant's job. That is how Elisha is identified.

## The reception

*Elisha said to the king of Israel, "**What do you and I have to do with each other? Go to the
prophets of your father and the prophets of your mother**."*

Ahab and Jezebel. The prophets of Baal and Asherah, four hundred and fifty and four hundred, whom
Elijah had faced on Carmel.

Elisha sends the king back to the family's own religious staff.

## The king's answer

*"No, for **it was the LORD who called us three kings together to hand us over to Moab**."*

He appeals to the LORD's name while blaming Him.

## The oath

*"**As surely as the LORD Almighty lives, whom I serve** — if it were not for **my regard for
Jehoshaphat king of Judah**, I would not so much as look at you or notice you."*

The Hebrew is **lule penei Yehoshaphat** — were it not for the face of Jehoshaphat.

He will speak, and he says exactly who he is speaking for.

## The request

*"**But now, bring me a musician**."*

The Hebrew is **qechu-li menaggen** — bring me one who plays.

**Nagan** — to play a stringed instrument. The verb used of David playing for Saul: *David would
take a harp and play it with his hand*.

## What is happening

A prophet has been asked for a word by three kings in a military emergency, in front of an army
dying of thirst.

He asks for a harpist first.

## The result

*And **as the musician played, the hand of the LORD came on Elisha**.*

The Hebrew is **vayehi kenaggen hamnaggen vattehi alav yad-YHWH**.

The playing is in progress when it comes.

**Yad-YHWH** — the hand of the LORD. The phrase for prophetic possession: *the hand of the LORD was
upon me* in Ezekiel, and *the hand of the LORD was there upon him*.

## Why the music

The text does not explain.

What it records is that a man who had just been furious with a king asked for a harp before he
would prophesy, and that the word came while it was being played.

## What he says

*"Make this valley full of ditches… **you shall not see wind, nor shall you see rain; yet that valley
shall be filled with water**."*

*And it happened in the morning… that suddenly water came by way of Edom, and the land was filled
with water.*

Ditches dug in dry ground, no rain, and water in the morning from a storm somewhere they never
saw.

## What the Moabites see

*And they saw the water on the other side as red as blood. And they said, "**This is blood**; the
kings have surely struck swords and have killed one another."

The sunrise on the water. They come out for plunder and are routed.

## Who else played

*And it happened, when the spirit from God was upon Saul, that **David would take a harp and play it
with his hand**. Then Saul would become refreshed and well.*

> **The request.** Three kings, an army without water, and a demand for a word — and the prophet's
> first instruction is *bring me a musician*. The hand of the LORD comes while the man is playing.
