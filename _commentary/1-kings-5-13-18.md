The workforce for the temple, and the detail that matters is where the stones were shaped.

## The numbers

Thirty thousand in shifts to Lebanon, a month away and two months home. Seventy thousand
carrying loads. Eighty thousand cutting stone in the hills. Thirty-three hundred supervisors.

A hundred and eighty thousand men, and a rota that sends two-thirds of them home at any given
time.

## Where the work happened

*At the king's command they **quarried great, costly stones, dressing them square** to lay the
foundation of the house with cut stone.*

Every stone was finished in the hill country, at the quarry, before it went anywhere.

## What that produced

The next chapter says it: *and the house, when it was being built, was built with stone
finished at the quarry, **so that no hammer or chisel or any iron tool was heard in the house
while it was being built**.*

A building the size of the temple went up in silence. All the noise happened somewhere else,
years earlier, out of sight.

## Where the stones are now

*You also, **as living stones**, are being built up a spiritual house, a holy priesthood.*

Peter takes the image and applies it to people — and the shaping in his version is not done at
the site either.

*In whom the whole building, being **fitted together**, grows into a holy temple in the Lord.*

The Greek is **synarmologoumene** — jointed together, fitted like masonry.

## Who else was on the crew

*Solomon's builders and **Hiram's builders and the men of Gebal**.*

Phoenicians from Tyre and Byblos. The house of the LORD was built by a workforce that was not
all Israelite, and the text names them without comment.

*Now, therefore, you are **no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens** with the
saints and members of the household of God… in whom the whole building is fitted together.*

## The man over the labour force

**Adoniram.** He is still in the post decades later, and when Rehoboam sends him out to the
northern tribes they stone him to death.

The chapter records the scale and the book records the cost.

> **Where the noise was.** Every stone squared at the quarry, so that the house itself went up
> without the sound of a tool. The shaping is done a long way from the building.
