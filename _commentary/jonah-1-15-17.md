The verse **Jesus** picked out of this book, and He picked it twice.

## The stilling

*"Then they lifted Jonah up and threw him into the sea, and **the raging waters grew still**."*

The Hebrew is **vayya'amod hayyam mizza'po** — and the sea stood still from its raging.

*Then He arose and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, "Peace, be still!" And **the wind ceased
and there was a great calm**. But He said to them, "Why are you so fearful?"… And they feared
exceedingly, and said to one another, "**Who can this be, that even the wind and the sea obey
Him?**"*

**Mark** 4. Two storms, two boats, two sleeping prophets — and in the second one nobody goes
overboard. The sea is stopped by a sentence.

## The crew

*"And the men were filled with **a deep and quiet fear of the LORD**. They offered a sacrifice to Him
and made their promises to Him."*

The Hebrew is *they feared the LORD with a great fear*. It is the same construction **Mark** uses of
the disciples.

Pagan sailors, converted before the prophet has preached a word.

## The provision

*"But **the LORD provided a great fish** to swallow Jonah."*

The Hebrew is **vayeman** — and He appointed, ordained, assigned. It is the same verb used three
more times in chapter 4, of a plant, a worm and an east wind.

Four times in the book God *appoints* something, and every one of them is uncomfortable for Jonah
and good for him.

The fish is not a punishment. It is the rescue. He was in the water.

## The three days

*"And Jonah was inside the fish **three days and three nights**."*

*"For **as Jonah was three days and three nights in the belly of the great fish, so will the Son of
Man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth**."*

**Matthew** 12, and it is **Jesus**' answer to scribes and Pharisees who had asked Him for a sign.

*"An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and **no sign will be given to it except the
sign of the prophet Jonah**."*

He says it twice in **Matthew**, in chapters 12 and 16. Of everything in the Old Testament He could
have named, the one sign He offers is this.

## What Jonah does inside

*From inside the fish, Jonah prayed to the LORD his God… **Salvation belongs to the LORD**.* See the
note on Jonah 2:8–9.

The prayer is almost entirely quotations from the psalms, and it is a thanksgiving rather than a
plea. He gives thanks for a rescue while still inside the fish.

## What it is not

*"**Out of the belly of Sheol I cried, and You heard my voice.**"*

Jonah's own word for where he was.

*"I am He who lives, and was dead, and behold, I am alive forevermore. Amen. And **I have the keys of
Hades and of Death**."*

> **The one sign.** Asked for a sign by the religious authorities, Jesus offered them a man in a
> fish for three days — the only sign He ever said would be given, and He named it twice.
