John turns to look at a voice and sees furniture from the tabernacle.

## "To look at the voice"

*I turned around **to look at the voice** that was talking to me.*

The Greek says it that way — to see the voice. John is not tidying his grammar.

## Seven lampstands

*There they were: **seven lampstands, all of them gold**.*

In Exodus there is one lampstand, hammered out of a single talent of gold, with six
branches from a central shaft. See the note on Exodus 25:31–40.

Here there are seven separate stands.

Jesus identifies them in the same chapter: *the seven lampstands which you saw are
the seven churches.*

## Where He is standing

*In among them stood a figure like **the Son of man**.*

Not above them, not in front of them. **In the middle of** them.

The lampstands in the holy place were the only light in a room with no windows, and
He is standing among these.

Two verses later He is described as holding the seven stars in His right hand — but
the lampstands He walks among.

## "Like the Son of man"

The phrase is Daniel 7: *one like the Son of Man, coming with the clouds of heaven…
and to Him was given dominion and glory and a kingdom.*

It is also the title Jesus used of Himself more than any other, some eighty times in
the Gospels.

## The robe and the band

*His robe ran **the whole length of Him, down to His feet**, and there was **a band of
gold around His chest**.*

The full-length robe is priestly — the Greek word is used in the Greek Old Testament
for the high priest's garment.

And the golden band across the chest, rather than at the waist. A working man belted
at the waist to move; a band across the chest is not for labour.

The work is finished, and He is dressed as the priest who is not hurrying.

## What He does with the lampstands

Chapter 2 opens: *these things says He who holds the seven stars in His right hand,
**who walks in the midst of the seven golden lampstands**.*

Walking among them. And in the temple, the priest's daily task at the lampstand was
trimming the wicks and topping up the oil so the light kept burning.

The letters that follow are addressed to seven churches, most of them with something
seriously wrong, and He is among them all.

> **The first thing He says to John.** *Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last.
> **I am He who lives, and was dead, and behold, I am alive forevermore.*** John had
> fallen at His feet as dead, and the hand that holds the seven stars is laid on him.
