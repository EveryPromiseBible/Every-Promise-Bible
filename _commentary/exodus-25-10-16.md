The first thing God tells Moses to build is not the tent. It is the box that goes
inside it.

## The order

*Start with the ark.* Before the table, the lampstand, the curtains, the courtyard
— the instructions begin at the innermost point and work outward.

The building starts where God will sit.

## Two materials

**Acacia wood** — the hard, durable timber of the desert, the only usable wood
they had out there. And **pure gold**, inside and out.

Wood from the wilderness where they were, and gold, which in Scripture is the
metal of what is divine and imperishable. The wood is not replaced by the gold or
hidden by it. Both are in the finished object, inseparably.

Readers have long seen the two natures of Christ in that combination, and the
instruction to overlay *inside and out* is part of why: gold on the visible
surface and gold on the surface nobody would ever see.

## The poles

*Slide them through the rings on the sides so the ark can be lifted and carried.
Leave the poles in the rings — **they never come out**.*

An odd, specific rule. The poles are not to be removed even when the ark is at
rest.

The practical effect is that the ark is permanently ready to move. Israel was
about to spend forty years going where the cloud went, and the piece of furniture
God sits over is built to travel at any hour.

Nobody had to prepare it. It was always packed.

> **What went inside.** The Testimony — the tablets. Hebrews adds two more items
> that ended up in there: the jar of manna and Aaron's staff that budded. The bread
> He supplied, the priesthood He chose, and the law they broke, in one box, under
> a lid of mercy.
