The last three verses of the shortest book in the Old Testament, and the last four words are the
point of all twenty-one.

## The reversal

*"People from the Negev will settle **the mountain of Esau**."*

The book has spent eighteen verses on a people who lived high in the rocks and asked *who can
bring me down?* See the note on Obadiah 1:1–4.

It ends with ordinary Judeans farming their hills.

## The list

Negev, lowlands, Ephraim, Samaria, Benjamin, Gilead, Zarephath, Sepharad.

The Hebrew is a survey — every direction accounted for, and the exiles specifically named twice:
*the exiles of Israel* and *the exiles of Jerusalem*.

*Zarephath* is where Elijah was fed by a widow during the famine. *Sepharad* is unidentified; later
Jewish tradition attached the name to Spain, which is where **Sephardi** comes from.

## The deliverers

*"Then **deliverers will go up on Mount Zion** to judge the mountain of Esau."*

The Hebrew is **moshi'im** — saviours. It is the plural of the noun behind **Yeshua**, and the same
word used of the judges: *the LORD raised up deliverers for them.*

They *go up*, and the mountain they judge is the one Edom lived on.

## The last four words

*"**And the kingdom will be the LORD's.**"*

**Vehaytah la-YHWH hammelukhah.**

Twenty-one verses about a grudge between brothers, and the final clause is not about Edom, Israel,
or the land. It is about who ends up holding the throne.

*Then the seventh angel sounded: and there were loud voices in heaven, saying, "**The kingdoms of
this world have become the kingdoms of our Lord and of His Christ**, and He shall reign forever and
ever!"*

**Revelation** 11, and it is Obadiah's last sentence with a name added.

*"For **Yours is the kingdom** and the power and the glory forever."*

**Matthew** 6 ends the prayer **Jesus** taught with the same claim.

*Then comes the end, when He delivers **the kingdom to God the Father**… that God may be all in
all.*

## Who the deliverer turned out to be

Obadiah's **moshi'im** is plural. The New Testament makes it singular:

*"And you shall call His name **JESUS**, for He will save His people from their sins."*

*"Nor is there salvation in any other, for there is **no other name** under heaven given among men
by which we must be saved."*

## What the book does not do

Resolve the family quarrel. Esau and Jacob are not reconciled in Obadiah, and the two brothers'
last recorded meeting in Genesis is the one where Esau *ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell
on his neck and kissed him, and they wept.*

*For **He Himself is our peace**, who has made both one, and has broken down the middle wall of
separation.*

> **The last four words.** *And the kingdom will be the LORD's.* The shortest book in the Old
> Testament is twenty verses of a border dispute and one sentence about a throne.
