The Roman state moves a pregnant girl eighty miles, and does not know why.

## The decree

*In those days **a decree went out from Caesar Augustus** that the whole empire was to be
registered.*

The Greek is **dogma** — an edict.

**Pasan ten oikoumenen** — all the inhabited world. Rome's way of saying the empire.

## Who Augustus was

Octavian, great-nephew of Julius Caesar, sole ruler after Actium. He closed the gates of the
temple of Janus to mark peace, and the age took his name.

Inscriptions call him **soter** — saviour — and **euangelia**, good news, announced his birthday.

Luke uses those words about somebody else in the next paragraph.

## The census

*This was **the first registration**, and it was carried out **while Quirinius was governor of
Syria**.*

The Greek is **apographe** — a registration, for tax.

Quirinius is known to have conducted a census in AD 6, which is too late. Luke's word **prote** —
first — may distinguish an earlier one, or the phrase may mean *before Quirinius was governor*.
The Greek allows it, and it is argued over.

## The method

*So **everyone traveled to his own town** to be counted.*

Roman censuses elsewhere required return to a home district; an Egyptian papyrus orders exactly
that.

## The journey

*That is why Joseph went up from **Nazareth in Galilee** to Judea, to the town of David called
**Bethlehem**.*

Eighty to ninety miles, on foot or with a donkey, through hill country.

## The reason

*Because he **belonged to the house and the family line of David**.*

The Greek is **ex oikou kai patrias David**.

A carpenter in a northern village, and the ancestral town is still on the record.

## Who is with him

*He went to be registered together with Mary, who was **promised to him in marriage** and was
**expecting a child**.*

The Greek is **emnesteumene** — betrothed.

Luke says betrothed, not married. Matthew says he *did not know her till she had brought forth her
firstborn Son*.

## What the census does

Moves them to Bethlehem.

*But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, though you are little among the thousands of Judah, **yet out of you
shall come forth to Me the One to be Ruler in Israel**.* See the note on Micah 5:2.

Written seven hundred years earlier.

## Who is doing what

Augustus issues a tax order for revenue.

The result is that a prophecy about a village gets fulfilled, and nobody in the imperial
administration is aware of it.

## What the same book says about another king

*This is what the LORD says to **Cyrus, His anointed one**… **though you did not know Me**.* See the
note on Isaiah 45:1–4.

## What happens there

*And while they were there her time came… and she wrapped Him around and around in strips of
cloth and **laid Him down in a feeding trough, because there was no room left for them in the
inn**.* See the note on Luke 2:6–7.

## Who is told

*And there were in the same country **shepherds living out in the fields**, keeping watch over their
flock by night.*

Not the court, not the temple, not the census officials.

*"For there is born to you this day in the city of David a Saviour, who is Christ the Lord."*

> **The mechanism.** An imperial tax order, issued for revenue, which happens to move a woman
> ninety miles to the village a prophet had named seven centuries earlier.
