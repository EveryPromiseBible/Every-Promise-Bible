The apostles are locked up, let out overnight, and sent straight back to the place
they were arrested.

## Why they were arrested

*Filled with **indignation**.*

The Greek is **zelos** — jealousy, zeal. Luke names the motive, and it is not
doctrine.

The verses before this describe crowds coming from the surrounding towns with the
sick, and *they were all healed*. The temple authorities are watching their standing
evaporate.

## When it happens

*But **during the night** an angel of the Lord opened the doors of the jail.*

No earthquake, no drama recorded. Doors opened, men brought out.

Luke reports the rescue in half a sentence and spends the rest of the paragraph on
what they were told to do.

## What the angel says

*"**Go, stand in the temple, and tell the people all the words of this life.**"*

Not *go home*, not *leave the city*, not *lie low*. Go back to the temple courts — the
most public place in Jerusalem, where they were picked up — and preach.

The rescue is not from the trouble. It is back into it.

## "All the words of this life"

The Greek is *this life* — a striking phrase.

They had been arrested for talking about a resurrection, and the message is
described by the angel as **life**.

## What they do

*Hearing that, they went into the temple **at daybreak** and began to teach.*

They do not wait or discuss it. They are teaching by first light.

## The comedy of the next scene

Meanwhile the high priest convenes the entire Sanhedrin, sends to the prison for the
prisoners, and the officers come back with:

*"We found the prison shut securely, and the guards standing outside before the
doors; but when we had opened them, we found no one inside!"*

The doors were locked. The guards were at their posts. Nobody had left, and nobody
was there.

And then somebody comes in and says: *"Look, the men whom you put in prison are
standing in the temple and teaching the people!"*

## What they say when re-arrested

*"**We ought to obey God rather than men.**"*

And Gamaliel's advice to the council: *if this plan is of men, it will come to
nothing; but if it is of God, you cannot overthrow it — lest you even be found to
fight against God.*

## How they leave

*They departed from the presence of the council, **rejoicing that they were counted
worthy to suffer shame for His name**.*

They had just been beaten.

> **What they did next.** *Daily in the temple, and in every house, they did not
> cease teaching and preaching Jesus as the Christ.* The angel's instruction was
> obeyed permanently.
