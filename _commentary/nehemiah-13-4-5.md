The enemy who could not get over the wall ends up living inside the temple, and a
priest gives him the keys.

## Who did it

*Now before this, **Eliashib the priest**, who **had charge of the storerooms of the
house of our God**.*

The high priest. The man who, in chapter 3, is named first in the list of builders —
*Eliashib the high priest rose up with his brethren the priests and built the Sheep
Gate.*

He helped build the wall. He also opened the door.

## The connection

*And was **closely tied to Tobiah**.*

The Hebrew is *near to* — allied, related. Tobiah's family had married into Jerusalem's
priestly households, and the alliance is stated as the reason for what follows.

## What was given

*Had **made ready for Tobiah a large room**.*

The Hebrew is *a great chamber*. Not a corner. The largest storeroom in the temple
complex.

An **Ammonite** — and the chapter opens with the law being read that day: *the Ammonite
and Moabite should never come into the assembly of God.*

The book puts the reading and the room three verses apart.

## What the room was for

*It was **the very place where they used to keep**:*

- **the grain offerings**
- **the incense**
- **the vessels**
- **the tithes of grain, new wine, and oil** set aside for the Levites, singers, and
  gatekeepers
- **the offerings for the priests**

Nehemiah lists every item that had to be moved out to make space.

The clearing was not incidental. Somebody carried the vessels of the house of God out
of that room and put a man's furniture in.

## Who it belonged to

The Levites, the singers and the gatekeepers. Their entire living came from those
stores.

## What happened to them

Four verses on: *I also realised that **the portions for the Levites had not been
given**, so that the Levites and the singers who did the work **had gone back to their
own fields**.*

The music stopped and the temple staff went home to farm. Nobody voted on it and nobody
announced it. The room was reallocated and the consequence followed.

## The timing

*During all this **I was not in Jerusalem**.*

Nehemiah had gone back to Artaxerxes. It happened while the governor was away, and it
took him going back to Persia and returning to find out. See the note on
Nehemiah 13:6–9.

## Who Tobiah was

The Ammonite official who mocked the wall — *if a fox climbed up on it, he'd knock the
stones right down.* See the note on Nehemiah 4:1–3.

His name means *the LORD is good*.

> **How he got in.** Not by force. He was invited by the high priest, on the strength of
> a family connection, into a room that had to be emptied of the tithes first.
