The workers are named, the materials are handed over, and the deliveries keep coming.

## The commission

*"So **Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person the LORD has gifted with the wisdom and
understanding** to know how to do all the work of building the sanctuary are to carry it out."*

The Hebrew is **chakham-lev** — wise of heart. The phrase for craft skill throughout these
chapters.

## The two men

**Bezalel** — *in the shadow of God*. Of the tribe of Judah.

**Oholiab** — *the father's tent*. Of the tribe of Dan.

Judah was the leading tribe. Dan was the smallest and later the most troublesome. The two men in
charge come from opposite ends of the standing.

## Where the skill came from

*The LORD **has gifted** with the wisdom and understanding.*

*And I have filled him with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, in knowledge, and in
all manner of workmanship.* See the note on Exodus 31:1–5.

The first man in Scripture described as filled with the Spirit of God is an artisan, and the
filling is for metalwork, stone-cutting and carpentry.

## The constraint

*Are to carry it out **exactly as the LORD has commanded**.*

The specification runs from chapter 25 to chapter 31 and is repeated in the execution from 35 to
40.

Skill in the highest degree, and no design freedom at all. The pattern was shown on the mountain.

## The calling

*So Moses called Bezalel and Oholiab and every skilled worker the LORD had gifted — **everyone
whose heart moved him to come and do the work**.*

Two qualifications: gifted, and moved to come.

The gift alone does not put a man on the site.

## The handover

*From Moses **they received all the offerings the Israelites had brought** for the work of building
the sanctuary.*

The materials had come in from the whole camp. See the note on Exodus 35:20–24.

Moses passes them to the craftsmen. He does not hold them.

## The last line

*And still **the people kept bringing freewill offerings, morning after morning**.*

The Hebrew is **babboqer babboqer** — in the morning, in the morning.

The doubling is the Hebrew way of saying *every single morning*.

## What that means

The work has started. The materials have been delivered. And people are still arriving with more
before breakfast, day after day.

## What happens next

*Finally all the skilled workers doing the work of the sanctuary **set down their tools and came to
Moses**.*

*"**The people are bringing far more than we need**."* See the note on Exodus 36:4–7.

The craftsmen stop work to report a surplus.

## The order that follows

*"**No man or woman is to make anything more** as an offering for the sanctuary."*

The only recorded instance in Scripture of giving having to be stopped.

## Where the gold had been

Round their ears and necks, brought out of Egypt.

And once already melted into a calf, at the foot of the same mountain, four chapters earlier. See
the note on Exodus 32:1–4.

Between the calf and this, Moses had gone back up and stayed forty days, and the LORD had said *I
will make My goodness pass before you*.

## What is being built

A tent to be carried through a desert, put up and taken down at every stage.

*Then the cloud covered the tabernacle of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the
tabernacle.*

> **The doubled word.** **Babboqer babboqer** — morning after morning. The work had started and
> the materials were in hand, and people kept turning up with more before the day began.
