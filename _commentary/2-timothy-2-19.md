title: "A seal with two lines on it"
updated: 2026-08-09
---
Right after saying that some people's faith is being capsized, Paul says the word the whole letter
turns on: *nevertheless*.

## What stands

*The **foundation God laid** stands there unmoved.*

**Ho stereos themelios tou theou hesteken** — the solid foundation of God has stood and stands. A
perfect tense: it was set, and it has not shifted since.

Notice whose it is. Not the church's foundation, not Timothy's. God laid it, so the capsizing of
somebody's faith in Ephesus is news about Ephesus and not about the foundation.

## The seal

Ancient foundation stones were cut with an inscription — the name of the builder and the purpose of
the house. Paul says this one carries two lines.

## The first line, and where it comes from

*"**The Lord knows the ones who belong to Him.**"*

That sentence is Moses', and he says it on the worst day of his leadership. Korah has led two
hundred and fifty men in revolt, the congregation is split, and nobody can tell by looking who is
loyal. Moses' answer is *tomorrow morning the LORD will show who is His*.

Paul reaches for it in exactly the same situation: a church where you cannot tell from the outside
what is real. The answer is not a test we administer. It is a knowledge God has, and He has had it
all along.

*I know My sheep… and no one is able to snatch them out of My Father's hand.*

## The second line

*"**Let everyone who names the name of Christ turn and walk away from wrongdoing.**"*

Two lines, and the order of them is the whole gospel. His knowing comes first and is not conditional
on anything on the second line. Then the departing — which is what a person does *because* he is
known, not in order to become known.

Put the second line first and you have a religion. Left as Paul wrote it, you have a seal: God's
side and ours, cut into the same stone, with His side underneath.

> **Two lines, and only one of them is load-bearing.** When you cannot tell what is real — in a
> church, or in yourself — the sentence to stand on is the first one. *The Lord knows those who are
> His.* He knew before you did, He is not revising the list, and the walking away from wrong is the
> answer to it rather than the price of it.
