One verse, and the turn of it is in the first four words.

## The trigger

*And **when Job prayed for his friends**, the LORD restored everything he had
lost.*

Not when he repented — that was four verses earlier and nothing was returned.

Not when he understood — he never did.

When he prayed for **them**.

## Who they were

Eliphaz, who told him no innocent man was ever destroyed. Bildad, who said his
children got what they deserved. Zophar, who told him he was getting off lightly.

Twenty-five chapters of it. He had called them *miserable comforters* and told
them they were grinding him to pieces with words, and he was right about all of
it.

Those are the three he is told to pray for, and God will not forgive them without
it.

## He still had nothing

Read the order of the chapter. Job prays while he is still on the ash heap. The
sheep are still dead, the children are still buried, the boils are still on him.

He is not praying out of an abundance. He has nothing to give but the prayer, and
he gives it to the men who hurt him.

## The word

*The LORD **restored** everything he had lost.*

The Hebrew is *turned the captivity of Job* — the same phrase used of God bringing
Israel back from exile.

It is release language. He had been held.

## Twice

*And gave him **twice as much** as he had before.*

Not restored to level. Doubled, and the numbers in verse 12 confirm it item by
item against chapter 1: seven thousand sheep become fourteen thousand, three
thousand camels become six thousand.

## What did not wait

Nothing in the verse says Job's own situation had to improve before he could pray
for theirs. It says the reverse — the praying came first, and the restoring
followed it.

He interceded for men who did not deserve it, out of a position where he had every
reason not to, and that is the sentence the whole book turns on.

> **The one thing that is not doubled.** He had seven sons and three daughters, and
> he ends with seven sons and three daughters. The livestock doubles; the children
> do not. Readers have always noticed it, and the usual reading is the kind one —
> the first ten were not lost to him.
