title: "Everyone in Asia"

One of the loneliest verses in the Bible, and it is a single sentence with two names in it.

## What it cost him to write

*Everyone over in Asia **has turned their backs on me**.*

**Apestraphesan** — they turned away. And Asia is not a far country he passed through: its capital
is Ephesus, the city Paul gave three years to, where he *did not cease night or day to warn everyone
with tears*.

He names two of them. Phygellus and Hermogenes are known to history for one thing, and this is it.

## What he does not do with it

He does not curse them. He does not write them out of the church. And when he comes back to the same
wound at the end of the letter, he does something remarkable with it:

*At my first defence **no one stood with me, but all forsook me**. May it not be charged against
them.*

That is Stephen's prayer, and behind Stephen's is the one from the cross. The man who held the coats
that day ends his life praying the same sentence for the people who left him.

## The clause that follows it

*But **the Lord stood with me and strengthened me**.*

Read the two sentences together and the shape of the whole letter appears. Everyone left; the Lord
stood. Paul does not resolve the desertion by minimising it — he lets it stand at full size, and
then puts one clause after it.

## What he had already written in the same letter

*If we are faithless, **He remains faithful; He cannot deny Himself**.*

Three chapters before Phygellus and Hermogenes are named, Paul has already said what he thinks about
people who fail. The faithfulness that holds is not ours, and the reason given is not that God is
merciful about it — it is that He *cannot deny Himself*. He would have to stop being who He is.

> **Deserted, and never alone.** Every friend in a province turned around, and Jesus Christ stood in
> the courtroom. It is a bleak verse and it is not a hopeless one, because the sentence Paul was
> ultimately resting on had already been spoken to him: *I will never leave you nor forsake you* —
> which turned out to be true on the one day it was tested hardest.
