---
key: Colossians 1:24–27
title: Christ in you
updated: 2026-08-07
---

A mystery kept back for ages turns out to be a preposition.

## The gladness

*Right now **I am glad in my sufferings for you**.*

The Greek is **chairō en tois pathēmasin**.

Written from house arrest, to a church he had never visited.

## The hard clause

*And in my own flesh **I fill up what is lacking in the afflictions of Christ**, for the sake of His
body, which is the church.*

The Greek is **antanaplērō ta husterēmata tōn thlipseōn tou Christou**.

## What it does not mean

That the atonement was insufficient.

The same letter says the reconciliation was completed *in the body of His flesh through death, to
present you holy, blameless, and above reproach*. See the note on Colossians 1:21–23.

## What **thlipsis** is

Pressure, affliction, persecution. Paul never uses it of the cross.

The word for the atoning death in this letter is **thanatos** and **haima** — death and blood.

## The likeliest sense

The afflictions Christ still endures in His body on earth.

*"Saul, Saul, **why are you persecuting Me**?"* See the note on Acts 9:3–6.

The question assumes an identity between the church and Christ, and Paul had it put to him
personally.

## The office

*Of this church **I became a servant**.*

The Greek is **diakonos**.

Twice in this passage he says it: a servant of the church, and a servant of the gospel.

## The commission

*By **the charge God gave me on your behalf**: to **bring the word of God to its full measure**.*

The Greek is **plērōsai ton logon tou Theou** — to fulfil, to complete, the word of God.

Not to add to it. To carry it to where it was meant to reach.

## The mystery

*The mystery that **lay hidden through ages and generations**, but has **now been made plain to His
holy people**.*

The Greek is **mustērion** — not something unknowable, but something that was concealed and has been
disclosed.

## Who it is now told to

*His holy people.*

The Greek is **tois hagiois autou**.

The mystery religions kept their secrets for initiates. This one is announced.

## The wealth

*God willed to make known to them **how rich and glorious this mystery is among the nations**.*

The Greek is **en tois ethnesin** — among the Gentiles.

That the nations were included at all is a large part of what was hidden. *That the Gentiles should
be fellow heirs, of the same body.* See the note on Ephesians 3:4–6.

## The content

*And it is this: **Christ in you, the hope of glory**.*

The Greek is **Christos en humin, hē elpis tēs doxēs**.

## The two words

**En humin** — in you. Or among you, since the pronoun is plural.

Both readings are defensible and the letter supports each: Christ indwelling the believer, and
Christ present among the nations.

## What is startling about it

Not that the Messiah came. That He took up residence in Gentiles.

## The pairing

*Christ in you* and *the hope of glory*, set in apposition.

The letter has already said *you were once alienated and enemies in your mind by wicked works*. The
indwelling is stated of those people.

## What Paul does about it

*Him we preach, warning every man and teaching every man in all wisdom, that **we may present every
man perfect in Christ Jesus**.* See the note on Colossians 1:28–29.

Three times *every man*, in one verse, in a letter arguing against a teaching that graded people
into levels.

## The labour

*To this end I also labour, **striving according to His working which works in me mightily**.*

The Greek is **agōnizomenos kata tēn energeian autou tēn energoumenēn**.

He strains, and the energy he strains with is stated not to be his.

> **The secret.** Kept back through ages and generations, and when it is disclosed it turns out to
> be two words and a preposition: *Christ in you*.
