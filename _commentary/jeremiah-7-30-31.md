One of the most disturbing passages in the prophets, and one of the most
revealing sentences about God in the Old Testament.

## What was happening

*They have built the high places of Topheth in the Valley of Ben Hinnom, and
there they burn their own sons and daughters in the fire.*

Child sacrifice, in a valley outside Jerusalem, by people who also kept the
temple running. Jeremiah is not describing a foreign nation; this is Judah.

The valley's name — Ge-Hinnom — comes through Greek into the New Testament as
**Gehenna**, the word Jesus uses for hell. The place became the image.

## The three denials

God says three things about it in a row, and the third is the one that stops
you:

- I never commanded it
- I never spoke of it
- **it never even entered My mind**

The Hebrew is *it did not come up on My heart*. Not merely that He forbade it —
that the notion had never crossed His thoughts.

## Why it needed saying

Because they were doing it in His name, in a valley within sight of His temple,
alongside sacrifices they believed He wanted. The whole passage is about worship
that had become something God did not recognise.

He has to state that He never conceived of it, because the people doing it
thought it was religion.

## What He wanted instead

Earlier in the same chapter: *obey My voice, and I will be your God and you will
be My people, and walk in all the way that I command you, that it may be well
with you.*

The alternative offered is not more sacrifice. It is listening.

> **Where the valley ends up.** Josiah later desecrated Topheth precisely to stop
> this, and the place became a refuse dump — which is how a name for a valley
> became a name for the fire. The worst thing people did to their children gave
> its name to the worst thing there is.
