God gives the design for the ark and then tells Moses where He will be found.

## What the lid was

**Kapporet** — from the verb to cover, to atone. It was not a decorative top. The
lid *was* the place of atonement, which is why English has long called it the
mercy seat.

Pure gold, hammered from one piece with the cherubim — not assembled. The angels
and the covering are the same object.

## What was underneath it

*Inside put the Testimony I'll give you.*

The stone tablets. The commandments Israel had already broken before Moses got
down the mountain lay inside the box, and the mercy seat lay over them.

That is the arrangement: the law underneath, the covering above, and the blood
sprinkled on the covering once a year.

## Where the cherubim are looking

*Facing each other, their eyes turned down toward the cover.*

The last time cherubim appeared they were at the gate of Eden with a flaming
sword, guarding the way back. Here they are bent over a lid, looking down at the
place where blood is applied.

Same creatures, different posture.

## The sentence that is the point of the whole passage

*And this is where I'll meet you. From above that mercy seat, from the space
between the two cherubim… Right there — that's where I choose to draw near and be
heard.*

Not from the altar where the sacrifices burned, and not from the tablets. From
the space above the covering.

God names the address at which He will speak, and it is the one place in the
tabernacle where mercy sits on top of the broken law.

> **What Paul calls Jesus.** In Romans 3 he uses the same word — *hilasterion*,
> the term the Greek Old Testament uses for this lid. God set Him forth publicly
> as the mercy seat. What was hidden behind a curtain and visited once a year is
> the thing Paul says was displayed in the open.
