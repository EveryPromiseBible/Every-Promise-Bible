They ask for a timetable and He answers with a warning about people who will offer
one.

## The question

*"**When will all of this happen? What will the sign be** that it is about to take
place?"*

They had just been admiring the temple stonework, and He had said not one stone would
be left on another.

Two questions: when, and what is the sign.

## The first answer

*"**Be careful that no one leads you astray.**"*

Before any content. The first thing He says about the end is a warning about
information regarding the end.

## What the deceivers say

*"Many will come using My name and saying, '**I am the one**,' and, '**The time has
arrived**.'"*

Two claims: an identity, and a date.

*"**Do not follow them.**"*

## The wars

*"When you hear of **wars and uprisings, do not be frightened**."*

The Greek is *do not be terrified*.

He does not say they will not happen. He says they will, and tells them what to do
with the news.

## The clause

*"Such things **have to happen first**, but **the end will not come right away**."*

Two things at once: these are necessary, and they are not the signal.

That is the sentence that undoes date-setting from both directions. The turbulence is
expected, and it does not mean the timetable has moved.

## What He says a few verses later

*"There will be great earthquakes in various places, and famines and **pestilences**;
and there will be fearful sights and great signs from heaven."*

The Greek is **loimos** — plague, pestilence. Named alongside earthquakes and famines,
as one of the things that are not the end.

## What He tells them to do about all of it

*"**By your patient endurance you will gain your souls.**"*

And, at the end of the chapter: *when these things begin to happen, **look up and lift
up your heads**, because your redemption draws near.* See the note on
Luke 21:25–28.

Two instructions in one chapter: do not be terrified, and look up.

## What He also says

*"Settle it in your hearts **not to meditate beforehand on what you will answer**;
for I will give you a mouth and wisdom."*

He tells them not to rehearse.

> **The one thing He rules out entirely.** *"It is not for you to know times or seasons
> which the Father has put in His own authority."* Said to the same men, in Acts 1,
> when they asked the question again — after the resurrection, and He still did not
> answer it.
